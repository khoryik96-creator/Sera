## Locked narrative functions

### #1 Tsubasa Kurokawa
Peak-Paragon ruling-guild leader. His Chained Crescent gives Shinrin a distinctive apex fighter rather than another fist, saber or conventional sword specialist.

### #2 Jin Takamori
Established Paragon. Yun Shizhen's attacker. His culpability is not softened into misunderstood romance or moral ambiguity.

### #3 Haru Ishikawa
Established Paragon. Strategic loyalist and precision spear specialist.

### #4 Hana Arakawa
Established Paragon. Major personal tie to Yurushi Amagiri / Black Radiance; exact emotional relationship remains to be developed in the Shinrin character-history pass.

### #5 Kai Moriyama
Established Paragon. Old-guard Shinrin warrior who remembers the period when centralization genuinely ended internal bloodshed.

### #6 Aya Katsuragi
Stable Paragon. Physician-cultivator with a medicine / Axtaya research connection. Her exact culpability and original research motive remain to be developed.

### #7 Kenji Narukami
Stable Paragon. Younger-generation Shinrin Paragon shaped by the centralized regime.

### #8 Jun Kajihara
Stable Paragon. Hard military expansionist and anti-defence battlefield breaker.

### #9 Eirik Voss
Stable Paragon. Former leader of Isgard from an earlier generation. Isgard knew him historically but did not know Shinrin existed or where he disappeared to.

### #10 Nao Shibasaki
Stable Paragon. Sympathetic official-ten member whose protective Water-Qi style contrasts with Shinrin's invasion policy.

## Yurushi Amagiri — Black Radiance

Yurushi is **not one of the official ten**.

- Personal name: **Yurushi Amagiri**.
- Title: **Black Radiance**.
- Cultivation: **Established Paragon**.
- Deliberately unranked in Shinrin.
- Avoids public rankings, ceremonies and formal officer status because he dislikes the spotlight.
- Only a small senior circle understood his true Established-Paragon cultivation.
- Later defects and joins the Quaint Teahouse as **Sixth Petal**.
- Rhen remains **Hidden Petal**.
- Domain: **Twilight Dominion**.
