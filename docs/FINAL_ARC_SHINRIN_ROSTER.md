# 2. Proposed Martial Roster

## #1 Rai Kurokawa — Peak Paragon

### Style — Continuous-Current Internal Boxing

Rai fights almost entirely barehanded. His martial philosophy is that no movement, impact or breath should ever end independently; every action is fed into the next. He is calm, economical and nearly impossible to make waste force.

### Signature skills

**River Without End — Transcended Passive**
- Rai keeps several internal circulation routes open at once.
- Physical momentum from a block, sidestep, shoulder turn or absorbed impact is carried through the body instead of being allowed to die.
- He does not steal enemy qi; he preserves and reroutes physical momentum and his own internal force.

**One Current Palm — Transcended Offense**
- A palm strike that collects the momentum of Rai's immediately preceding movements into one short-range impact.
- The longer a clean exchange continues, the more efficiently he can feed earlier motion into the next strike.

**Heaven Returns to Sea — Supreme**
- Rai collapses every active internal current into one unified circulation for a single overwhelming attack, defence or redirection.
- Afterwards he must rebuild the multi-current rhythm rather than spam it repeatedly.

### Domain — **Confluence Dominion**

Inside the Domain, every movement Rai himself successfully completes can be joined into one continuous martial current.

- a parry can feed a step;
- a step can feed a shoulder turn;
- a shoulder turn can feed a palm;
- physical momentum he successfully receives can be redirected through his own body into the next movement;
- almost no usable motion is wasted while he maintains the flow.

The Domain does **not** steal cultivation, copy techniques or automatically redirect attacks. Rai must physically receive, evade, guide or complete each exchange correctly first.

**Combat identity:** the longer Rai is allowed to maintain a clean continuous exchange, the more perfectly the whole fight becomes one movement belonging to him.

## #2 Jin Takamori — Established Paragon

### Style — Seizing Hand / Close-Range Capture Art

Jin is a brutal close-range specialist using grappling, palms, elbows and short hooked blades. His martial art was originally designed for capturing high-level fugitives alive, but he turned it into a cruel control system.

### Signature skills

**Five Lock Hand — Transcended Control**
- Precise fingers, palms and elbows strike major circulation junctions.
- A clean hit creates temporary localized numbness, weakness or delayed qi flow.

**Wolf Hook — Transcended Offense**
- Short hooked blades catch sleeves, weapons, armour gaps or limbs and drag the opponent into Jin's close-combat range.
- It is physical hooking reinforced by qi, not telekinetic pulling.

**Closed Meridian Cage — Supreme**
- A rapid sequence targeting several previously contacted circulation points.
- If enough contacts land, Jin can temporarily make a limb or section of the body extremely difficult to circulate qi through.

### Domain — **Hundred Locks Dominion**

Every clean physical contact Jin earns inside the Domain leaves a short-lived **lock** on that exact contacted region.

- one lock causes mild circulation drag;
- repeated locks on the same limb or nearby meridians compound;
- enough locks can make a limb sluggish, numb or temporarily unusable for high-level techniques;
- strong cultivators can purge locks by stopping and devoting circulation to the affected area, but this gives Jin pressure and time;
- locks expire and do not permanently cripple victims by default.

**Combat identity:** the more Jin is allowed to touch you, the less of your own body remains available to fight with.

## #3 Ren Ishikawa — Established Paragon

### Style — Precision Spear / Battlefield Measurement

Ren uses a long spear and treats combat as geometry rather than prophecy. He is Shinrin's cleanest tactical fighter: reach, angle, pace and formation spacing matter more to him than spectacle.

### Signature skills

**Three-Length Spear — Transcended Offense**
- Ren can shorten, normalise or extend the effective reach of one thrust by changing grip, body alignment and compressed spear qi at the last instant.
- The spear itself does not magically change length.

**Half-Step Outside — Transcended Movement**
- Ren positions himself just outside the measured end of an opponent's committed attack and counters before their stance fully recovers.

**Nine Lines Collapse — Supreme**
- Nine deliberately chosen spear lines are fired in sequence to remove viable angles one after another until the opponent is forced toward a final narrow lane.

### Domain — **True Measure Dominion**

The Domain gives Ren near-perfect real-time measurement of **actual** physical combat data inside its range:

- distance;
- angle;
- speed;
- weapon reach;
- visible body rotation;
- acceleration once movement has begun.

It does **not** read thoughts, predict choices or see the future. A feint can still fool him before commitment, but once a body or weapon genuinely moves, Ren measures it with terrifying precision.

**Combat identity:** Ren does not know what you will choose; once you choose, he knows exactly how far, how fast and where it can reach.

## #4 Mei Arakawa — Established Paragon

### Style — Twin Short Swords / Wind-Qi Qinggong

Mei is an elegant high-mobility fighter using twin short swords and compressed wind qi. She wins through angle changes and pressure wakes rather than raw force.

### Signature skills

**Falling Sky Step — Transcended Movement**
- Wind qi releases around each footfall to produce violent lateral and diagonal acceleration.
- Especially strong for changing direction without losing much speed.

**Twin Gale Crescent — Transcended Offense**
- Two sword cuts release intersecting compressed wind edges that arrive from slightly different angles.

**Silent Crosswind — Supreme**
- Mei hides one major directional shift inside the pressure wake of her previous movement, making her next attack seem to arrive from the wrong continuation line.
- It is airflow manipulation and footwork, not illusion.

### Domain — **Crosswind Dominion**

All significant movement inside the Domain generates real pressure wakes in the surrounding air.

Mei can use her own wind qi to bend, strengthen or oppose those wakes.

- her own movement can be accelerated by aligning with a wake;
- an opponent charging through a hostile crosswind can have footing or weapon trajectory pushed slightly off line;
- projectiles and broad qi releases can be curved or destabilized if they are light enough;
- massive or highly concentrated attacks cannot simply be blown away.

**Combat identity:** every movement creates wind, and Mei turns the battlefield's accumulated motion into angles only she fully understands.

## #5 Kai Moriyama — Established Paragon

### Style — Heavy Saber / Furnace Qi

Kai is an old-school frontline warrior. He uses a broad heavy saber and internal heat cultivated through repeated physical clashes. His style becomes more dangerous the longer he remains engaged, but he must manage his own temperature and circulation.

### Signature skills

**Furnace Heart — Transcended Passive**
- Physical clashes and powerful circulation gradually heat Kai's core and weapon.
- Higher heat improves muscle response and saber impact, but excess heat stresses his body.

**Red Mountain Cleave — Transcended Offense**
- A heavy saber cut that releases tightly compressed heat and force only at the point of impact rather than as a large fire wave.

**Last Ember Guard — Supreme Defence / Counter**
- Kai absorbs a dangerous clash through reinforced stance and vents stored furnace qi through the defending saber into an immediate short-range counterburst.

### Domain — **Ashen Furnace Dominion**

Every major clash involving Kai produces additional internal and environmental heat inside the Domain.

- Kai can feed that heat back into his body and saber;
- close-range opponents gradually face hotter air, hotter weapon contact and more punishing body pressure;
- Kai's striking power and durability rise as the furnace builds;
- if he pushes too far, his own meridians and organs begin to overheat, so the Domain has a genuine self-limit.

**Combat identity:** survive Kai early, because every real clash makes the next one hotter and heavier — including for Kai himself.

## #6 Aya Katsuragi — Stable Paragon

### Style — Medical Needles / Meridian Medicine

Aya is a physician-cultivator who can heal or disable using the same understanding of circulation. Her connection to Axtaya should remain morally complicated rather than making her a simple poison villain.

### Signature skills

**Seven Pulse Needles — Transcended Control**
- Seven fine needles are placed or thrown toward major pulse junctions.
- Depending on placement they can calm spasms, slow bleeding, reduce pain or disrupt a technique's circulation.

**White Thread Sutra — Transcended Healing**
- Fine qi threads stabilize damaged meridians and keep a patient alive long enough for deeper treatment.
- Far below Rhen's healing ceiling, but highly advanced conventional martial medicine.

**Borrowed Beat — Supreme**
- Aya temporarily synchronizes a patient's unstable circulation to her own controlled rhythm through needles/contact, keeping a failing body functioning for a short emergency window.
- It does not restore lost lifespan or resurrect the dead.

### Domain — **Hundred Pulse Dominion**

Aya perceives the real circulation pulse of every living body inside the Domain with extraordinary clarity.

- she can identify blocked, accelerated, poisoned or unstable meridian rhythms;
- she can time needles and palm pulses with near-perfect precision;
- healing allies becomes faster and more exact;
- offensive acupuncture becomes much more dangerous because she knows when a circulation junction is maximally vulnerable.

The Domain does **not** remotely control bodies. Aya must still physically deliver a needle, touch or qi pulse to alter someone.

**Combat identity:** inside her Domain, every heartbeat and circulation cycle becomes readable medical information.

## #7 Ryo Narukami — Stable Paragon

### Style — Straight Sword / Thunder-Qi Qinggong

Ryo is the youngest-feeling member of the official ten: fast, aggressive and raised under Shinrin's modern ideology. His thunder qi is built around conduction through body, weapon and ground rather than giant lightning spells.

### Signature skills

**Flashstep — Transcended Movement**
- A thunder-qi burst forces explosive neuromuscular acceleration over a short distance.

**Sky Crack — Transcended Offense**
- Sword contact releases a compressed electrical shock through the immediate clash point, disrupting muscle timing and grip for an instant.

**Seven Thunder Paths — Supreme Movement / Offense**
- Ryo creates a rapid sequence of grounded charge points through seven steps, then uses the prepared route for a final violent acceleration and sword strike.

### Domain — **Thunderstep Dominion**

Every grounded step Ryo deliberately plants can leave a temporary charged qi point in a conductive physical surface.

- moving between prepared points becomes faster and more explosive;
- sword contact near those routes can discharge stored thunder qi;
- enemies standing directly on charged paths risk brief muscular disruption when Ryo releases them;
- the points fade and require physical grounding; Ryo cannot freely teleport between them.

**Combat identity:** Ryo turns the ground he has already crossed into a network of explosive acceleration and nerve-shocking contact.

## #8 Jun Kajihara — Stable Paragon

### Style — Heavy Halberd / Siege-Breaking Martial Art

Jun is Shinrin's most openly expansionist battlefield officer. His martial art was built to break fortifications, shield walls and reinforced defensive formations rather than hunt agile duelists.

### Signature skills

**Gatebreaker Halberd — Transcended Offense**
- A downward or lateral halberd strike condenses force into a very small impact zone to crack reinforced guards.

**Second Wall — Transcended Follow-Up**
- Jun deliberately strikes the same defended line again before the structure or guard can fully recover its qi reinforcement.

**Third Wall Falls — Supreme**
- Three escalating impacts attack the same structural or defensive weakness in rapid succession, each one shaped around the fracture created by the previous hit.

### Domain — **Breach Dominion**

Whenever Jun cleanly strikes a defended physical point, structure, armour section, barrier or weapon guard inside the Domain, a trace of his halberd qi remains in the resulting stress fracture.

- later strikes against that same defended line penetrate more efficiently;
- static fortifications and repeated blocks become increasingly dangerous to rely on;
- highly mobile fighters who refuse to defend from the same position reduce his advantage;
- the Domain does not automatically weaken untouched targets.

**Combat identity:** Jun does not need to break the wall on the first hit. He only needs the wall to remain the same wall.

## #9 Eirik Voss — Stable Paragon

### Style — Isgard Longsword and Shield / Formation Leadership

Eirik's style should visibly look **Isgardian**, not Shinrin-born. He fights with a long northern sword and round shield, using disciplined marching footwork from the era when he ruled Isgard.

### Signature skills

**North Road Step — Transcended Movement**
- Eirik plants extremely stable advancing steps that preserve balance under charge, impact and bad terrain.

**King's Guard — Transcended Defence**
- Shield, shoulder and sword form a moving defensive frame designed to protect both Eirik and someone immediately beside him.

**Last Standard — Supreme**
- Eirik locks his stance and creates a short moving rally corridor behind his shield line; allies physically following his advance can preserve formation under heavy pressure.
- It is martial coordination and shared defensive qi pressure, not supernatural morale control.

### Domain — **King's Road Dominion**

Every deliberate step Eirik takes while maintaining proper stance leaves a temporary **road mark** of compressed stabilizing qi in the ground.

- Eirik and allies who physically follow those road marks gain better footing, formation stability and reduced loss of momentum under impact;
- enemies charging across the marked line meet opposing grounded pressure and are harder to knock cleanly through Eirik's formation;
- the road does not compel anyone to follow it and does not buff people who are nowhere near the physical route;
- if Eirik is forced airborne or denied stable ground, the Domain loses much of its value.

**Combat identity:** Eirik does not dominate a battlefield by standing alone; he creates a road others can actually survive marching behind.

This should make his eventual confrontation with Isgard emotionally uncomfortable: his Domain still looks like the art of a ruler who once protected them.

## #10 Nao Shibasaki — Stable Paragon

### Style — Flowing Jian / Defensive Water-Qi Swordsmanship

Nao is a defensive swordswoman whose water-like qi disperses force rather than meeting everything head-on. She is well suited to being one of the more sympathetic Shinrin officers because her martial identity is fundamentally protective.

### Signature skills

**Quiet Lake Guard — Transcended Defence**
- Circular sword motion spreads one heavy impact across several smaller body rotations and qi channels.

**Rain-Cut Sword — Transcended Offense**
- A series of light, precise cuts delivered while redirecting around a stronger opponent's guard rather than contesting it directly.

**Mercy Current — Supreme Defence / Rescue**
- Nao wraps water-like qi around herself and one nearby person, splitting incoming force across multiple flowing vectors to create an escape opening.

### Domain — **Stillwater Dominion**

Nao fills the immediate battlefield around her with layered circulating water-like qi currents.

- incoming physical force that enters those currents can be divided across several directions rather than taken head-on;
- she can use the redirected flow to shift herself or an ally away from the centre of impact;
- concentrated attacks vastly stronger than her can still punch through;
- she cannot reflect attacks back at equal strength like Qin's Echo Dominion;
- she cannot nullify techniques wholesale.

**Combat identity:** Nao survives by making one overwhelming force become many smaller forces that no longer arrive together.
