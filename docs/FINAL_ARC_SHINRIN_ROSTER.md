## Locked narrative functions

### #1 Tsubasa Kurokawa
Peak-Paragon Shinsei Guild leader and undisputed Shinrin No.1. A historical hero of unification whose greatest success taught him that overwhelming centralized strength could end civil bloodshed. At Heaven Gate he defeated three Paragons and reached Peak Paragon. His later-approved Ultimate is **One Chain Binds Heaven**.

### #2 Shunto Takamori
Established Paragon and legendary capture specialist. Yun Shizhen's attacker. His history explains why Shinsei elevated him but never softens his responsibility for Yun's assault.

### #3 Haru Ishikawa
28-year-old Established-Paragon prodigy, precision spear master and Tsubasa's most trusted young strategic mind. He grew up inside the stable Shinsei order rather than the worst pre-unification era.

### #4 Hana Arakawa
Established Paragon from the Skywell Cliffs. Pressure/Wind-Qi specialist, socially perceptive internal conscience and Yurushi Amagiri's former partner of roughly four years.

### #5 Kai Moriyama
Established Paragon from the Red Furnace region. Old-guard warrior, Tsubasa's longtime personal check and the man who defeated a much younger Tsubasa before later choosing to join him.

### #6 Aya Katsuragi
Stable Paragon physician-cultivator. Creator of the legitimate emergency-medicine precursor to Axtaya who voluntarily remained involved too long as Shinsei militarized it. Her central conflict is harm reduction versus complicity.

### #7 Kenji Narukami
25-year-old Stable Paragon / Thunder-Qi prodigy. A true child of unified Shinsei whose loyalty is rooted in the peaceful life the system gave his generation.

### #8 Jun Kajihara
Stable Paragon and hard military realist. His childhood experience of a fortress destroyed by prolonged indecision shaped his belief that genuinely necessary violence becomes worse when delayed.

### #9 Eirik Voss
Stable Paragon and former ruler of Isgard from an earlier generation. He disappeared to avert civil war, later saw early Shinsei as proof that centralization could work, and eventually became one of Shinsei's senior commanders.

### #10 Nao Shibasaki
31-year-old Stable Paragon / Water-Qi swordswoman. Compassionate officer whose **Stillwater Dominion** and Two Flags legend center on reducing escalation rather than glorifying victory.

## Yurushi Amagiri — Black Radiance

Yurushi is **not one of the official ten**.

- Personal name: **Yurushi Amagiri**.
- Title: **Black Radiance**.
- Cultivation: **Established Paragon**.
- Deliberately unranked in Shinsei.
- Avoids public rankings, ceremonies and formal officer status because he dislikes the spotlight.
- Only a small senior circle understood his true cultivation.
- Later defects and joins the Quaint Teahouse as **Sixth Petal**.
- Rhen remains **Hidden Petal**.
- Domain: **Twilight Dominion**.

His relationship with Hana and his defection history are authoritative in `docs/FINAL_ARC_SHINRIN_CHARACTER_LOCKS.md`.

## Xie Wuchen — later Wuyue No.10

Xie Wuchen is **not a Shinsei character**, but his late final-arc addition is cross-referenced here because Yun's death creates the Wuyue vacancy.

- **Xie Wuchen / The Unseen Gale**.
- Age **33**.
- Newly Sovereign.
- Last true successor of **Tempest Hall**.
- Secretly the masked Wind-Qi fighter who defeated Shen Rui before Shen Rui entered Wuyue's Top Five.
- Later accepts Yun Shizhen's vacant **Wuyue No.10** seat.
- No Dominion yet because he remains Sovereign.
- Full locked history and skill roster: `docs/FINAL_ARC_SHINRIN_CHARACTER_LOCKS.md`.
