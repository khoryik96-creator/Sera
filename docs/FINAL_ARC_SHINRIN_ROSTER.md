# 2. Proposed Martial Roster — Revised

## #1 Rai Kurokawa — Peak Paragon

### Style — Longbow / Threaded Bow-Qi Archery

Rai should not resemble Tae, Kael or Huo in silhouette or fighting rhythm. He is a **dedicated long-range archer** who prefers to decide a battle before an opponent reaches him.

He uses a powerful black longbow and specially balanced heavy arrows. His internal art produces extremely fine strands of bow qi that remain connected to arrows he has personally fired for a short interval. He is not telekinetically controlling objects; the qi strands act like temporary extensions of bowstring tension.

At ordinary levels, an archer wins by predicting where a target will be. Rai wins because a shot can still be adjusted **after the target believes the original trajectory has been solved**.

### Signature skills

#### **Silent String — Transcended Passive / Control**

Rai wraps bow, hands and shoulders in tightly controlled qi at the moment of release.

What it does:

- absorbs most recoil into his stance and circulation;
- suppresses the normal explosive crack of the bowstring;
- lets him fire accurately while stepping, turning or dropping from height;
- allows much faster follow-up shots because the bow settles almost instantly after release.

What it looks like:

The bow barely seems to move. Often the target hears the arrow striking before hearing anything from Rai's position.

It does not make him invisible and does not erase the physical arrow.

#### **Black Comet — Transcended Offense**

Rai condenses qi tightly along the entire arrow rather than creating a huge projectile blast.

Effect:

- extreme penetration;
- very little wasted outward explosion;
- can punch through layered armour, barriers or dense defensive qi if the shot is properly placed;
- loses some effectiveness if forced to fire before he finishes compression.

What it looks like:

A dark, almost ordinary-looking arrow crosses the battlefield with very little spectacle, followed a heartbeat later by the sound of whatever it penetrated breaking behind it.

#### **Nine Stars Descend — Supreme**

Rai fires nine arrows at deliberately different elevations, speeds and timings.

They initially appear unrelated.

Using his bow-qi control, he causes the nine shots to enter the target area within the same tiny window from different angles.

The opponent is therefore not blocking nine arrows one after another; they are dealing with **nine physical killing lines arriving almost together**.

It requires enormous concentration and is difficult to repeat immediately.

### Domain — **Heavenstring Dominion**

Inside the Domain, every arrow Rai personally fires can remain connected to him by an ultra-fine strand of bow qi until it hits, leaves the Domain or the strand breaks.

For each connected arrow, Rai can make **one meaningful correction** while it is still in flight.

Possible corrections include:

- slightly changing angle after an opponent dodges;
- accelerating an arrow late;
- delaying one arrow so several attacks arrive together;
- tightening several trajectories toward the same defensive opening;
- pulling a near-miss into a grazing or penetrating strike.

Important limits:

- arrows do **not** teleport;
- they still obey physical momentum and cannot turn at impossible right angles;
- Rai cannot attach strings to enemy weapons, bodies or unrelated objects;
- sufficiently powerful qi interference can sever a bow-qi strand;
- forcing Rai into cramped close combat makes maintaining many strings much harder;
- each additional connected arrow costs concentration and reserve.

At Peak Paragon level, however, he can sustain a frightening number of simultaneous shots.

**Combat identity:** Rai makes ordinary archery rules unreliable. Dodging the first trajectory is not enough, because the arrow may still have one decision left.

## #2 Jin Takamori — Established Paragon

### Style — Seizing Hand / Close-Range Capture Art

Jin fights with grappling, elbows, palms, finger strikes and paired short hooked blades.

His martial system began as a legitimate Shinrin capture art designed to arrest dangerous cultivators without immediately killing them. Jin transformed it into a method of progressively taking control of an opponent's body section by section.

He wants contact. A clean duel against Jin becomes increasingly dangerous every time he earns even a small touch.

### Signature skills

#### **Five Lock Hand — Transcended Control**

Jin strikes five major circulation junctions using fingers, palm heel, elbow or wrist.

A precise hit does not simply hurt. It disrupts the local relationship between muscle response and qi circulation.

Effects may include:

- numb fingers;
- a weakening grip;
- delayed leg response;
- reduced qi flow through one shoulder or elbow;
- short bursts of painful circulation when the victim tries to force power through the damaged point.

The effect is temporary unless Jin causes real physical injury.

#### **Wolf Hook — Transcended Offense / Capture**

Jin's hooked blades are designed to catch rather than slash cleanly through.

He can hook:

- a weapon guard;
- armour seam;
- sleeve;
- belt;
- shield rim;
- exposed limb.

He then combines body rotation and qi-reinforced pulling force to drag the target off structure and into elbow/grappling range.

There is no telekinetic pull; the hook must physically catch something.

#### **Closed Meridian Cage — Supreme**

Jin rapidly strikes several circulation points that he has already identified or contacted during the fight.

If enough land in the correct sequence, a whole limb or body section can become temporarily unable to circulate high-output qi cleanly.

The victim may still move physically, but attempting a major technique through that region becomes slow, painful or unstable.

### Domain — **Hundred Locks Dominion**

Every clean physical contact Jin earns inside the Domain leaves a short-lived **lock** in that exact contacted region.

One lock causes mild drag.

Repeated locks compound.

For example:

- one clean touch to the sword arm slightly slows circulation;
- several more contacts make the wrist and elbow heavy;
- enough accumulated locks can make a high-level sword art unreliable or temporarily unusable through that arm.

Strong cultivators can purge locks by deliberately stopping and forcing circulation through the affected region, but doing so costs time and attention while Jin keeps attacking.

Locks expire. The Domain does not automatically cripple people permanently.

**Combat identity:** every touch lets Jin remove another piece of the opponent's fighting capacity.

## #3 Ren Ishikawa — Established Paragon

### Style — Precision Spear / Battlefield Measurement

Ren uses a long spear and fights like a living measuring instrument.

He is not prophetic. He does not read minds or foresee choices.

Instead, once an opponent genuinely moves, Ren understands the physical reality of that movement with absurd precision: reach, speed, angle, recovery time and where the body must finish.

### Signature skills

#### **Three-Length Spear — Transcended Offense**

Ren changes the effective reach of one thrust at the final instant using grip shift, torso extension and compressed spear qi.

The spear does not physically grow.

The opponent may read the first grip and believe the tip will stop short, only for Ren to slide into the long grip and extend compressed qi along the thrust line.

He can also shorten unexpectedly, causing a defender to overreact to reach that never arrives.

#### **Half-Step Outside — Transcended Movement / Counter**

Ren deliberately positions himself just beyond the actual end point of a committed attack.

The enemy misses by inches rather than meters.

Because Ren never over-retreated, he is already inside perfect spear-counter distance while the enemy is still recovering.

#### **Nine Lines Collapse — Supreme**

Ren attacks nine carefully selected angles in sequence.

Each thrust is chosen to remove one viable escape or counter line.

By the final thrust, the opponent is not being overwhelmed by speed alone; they have been physically shepherded into a much smaller set of surviving positions.

### Domain — **True Measure Dominion**

Inside the Domain, Ren receives near-perfect real-time awareness of measurable physical combat data after movement begins:

- distance;
- angle;
- weapon reach;
- acceleration;
- body rotation;
- foot placement;
- recovery distance;
- actual speed.

A feint can still deceive him before genuine commitment.

Once the body, weapon or technique truly moves, however, he reads its physical limits almost instantly.

**Combat identity:** Ren does not know what you will choose. The problem is that the moment you choose, he knows exactly what that choice can physically reach.

## #4 Mei Arakawa — Established Paragon

### Style — Twin Short Swords / Wind-Qi Qinggong

Mei uses twin short swords and compressed Wind Qi.

She does not try to overpower stronger opponents. She turns tiny directional errors into major openings by attacking from angles that keep changing faster than the defender can reset.

Her style is graceful at first glance and vicious in practice.

### Signature skills

#### **Falling Sky Step — Transcended Movement**

Wind Qi bursts around Mei's feet and lower legs during directional changes.

Unlike ordinary qinggong, which often sacrifices speed when changing direction sharply, Falling Sky Step lets her redirect sideways, diagonally or downward while preserving much of her momentum.

This makes her extremely difficult to trap against a wall or predictable retreat line.

#### **Twin Gale Crescent — Transcended Offense**

Mei releases two compressed wind-edged sword cuts from slightly separated angles.

The first forces a guard.

The second arrives close enough that the same guard position is usually imperfect against it.

The wind edges remain extensions of real sword force rather than giant ranged blades.

#### **Silent Crosswind — Supreme**

Mei uses the pressure wake from one violent movement to hide the airflow signature of her next directional change.

An opponent reading her previous motion expects the continuation to go one way.

Mei changes direction inside that disturbed air and attacks from another.

It is not an illusion. The opponent's reading is wrong because the physical airflow is intentionally misleading.

### Domain — **Crosswind Dominion**

Every meaningful movement inside the Domain produces real pressure changes in the air.

Mei can manipulate those pressure differences with her Wind Qi.

She can:

- accelerate herself by moving with a favorable crosswind;
- slightly spoil an enemy's weapon angle;
- push a charging foot placement off by inches;
- curve light projectiles;
- destabilize broad, diffuse qi releases.

She cannot casually blow away a massive concentrated attack or move an opponent like a puppet.

The effect becomes especially dangerous in crowded fights because every moving body contributes new disturbed air for Mei to exploit.

**Combat identity:** everyone creates wind when they move; Mei is the only person treating all of that movement as usable battlefield material.

## #5 Kai Moriyama — Established Paragon

### Style — Heavy Saber / Tempered Flame Qi

Kai is an old frontline monster carrying a broad heavy saber.

His cultivation uses internal heat generated by powerful circulation and physical clashes. The hotter his system becomes, the more explosively his muscles respond and the denser the heat around his saber edge becomes.

The danger is that he is also cooking himself from the inside if he refuses to stop.

### Signature skills

#### **Redsteel Heart — Transcended Passive**

Every major clash raises Kai's internal temperature and circulatory pressure.

As heat builds:

- muscle response becomes faster;
- the saber becomes hotter at contact;
- impact force increases;
- minor cold/numbness effects become easier for him to burn through.

If pushed too far, however, his own organs and meridians begin taking heat stress.

#### **Crimson Cleave — Transcended Offense**

Kai compresses heat and saber force into the physical impact point rather than launching a giant flame wave.

The cut strikes first.

A heartbeat later, stored heat violently expands through the damaged guard or armour section, making a blocked hit far more punishing than it initially looked.

#### **Last Ember Guard — Supreme Defence / Counter**

Kai receives one major attack through reinforced stance and saber structure.

Instead of letting the clash's heat disperse, he catches it inside his own circulating flame qi and immediately vents the stored pressure back through a short countercut.

It is strongest when used against a genuinely heavy incoming attack.

### Domain — **Crimson Crucible Dominion**

Every major clash involving Kai inside the Domain adds heat to the battlefield and to Kai's internal cultivation cycle.

The Domain behaves like a martial crucible:

- weapon clashes become progressively hotter;
- close-range air becomes painful to breathe for weaker fighters;
- Kai's saber retains more heat between strikes;
- his muscles and circulation become more explosive as the crucible builds;
- armour, weapon grips and defensive qi become increasingly uncomfortable under repeated heated contact.

But the crucible is not free power.

Kai is inside it too.

If he keeps forcing output beyond what his body can vent, his meridians, blood and organs begin overheating. He can win by pushing the crucible higher than the enemy can endure, but he can also destroy himself doing it.

**Combat identity:** the fight becomes progressively hotter until one side cannot physically remain in the exchange anymore.

## #6 Aya Katsuragi — Stable Paragon

### Style — Medical Needles / Meridian Medicine

Aya is a true physician-cultivator.

The same anatomical knowledge lets her heal allies or disable enemies depending on where, when and why a needle is placed.

She should feel clinically precise rather than sinister.

### Signature skills

#### **Seven Pulse Needles — Transcended Control**

Aya uses seven fine needles aimed at major pulse and circulation junctions.

Depending on placement, they can:

- slow bleeding;
- calm spasms;
- reduce pain;
- stabilize erratic qi;
- temporarily disrupt a technique's circulation;
- weaken one muscle group without killing the patient/opponent.

#### **White Thread Sutra — Transcended Healing**

Aya extends extremely fine qi through implanted needles and uses it like temporary internal suturing.

The qi threads stabilize torn meridians and damaged vessels long enough for the patient's own body and medicine to begin recovering.

It does not instantly heal catastrophic injuries the way Rhen can.

#### **Borrowed Beat — Supreme Healing / Emergency Support**

Aya physically connects to a failing patient through needle points and synchronizes their unstable circulation to her own controlled pulse for a short period.

It can keep someone alive through shock, poison or meridian failure while proper treatment is prepared.

It does not restore consumed lifespan and cannot restart a truly dead person.

### Domain — **Hundred Pulse Dominion**

Inside the Domain, Aya can perceive the real circulation rhythm of every living body with extraordinary clarity.

She can distinguish:

- poison-distorted circulation;
- blocked meridians;
- internal bleeding;
- unstable heartbeat;
- qi overload;
- exhausted muscle groups;
- the exact instant a circulation junction opens during technique use.

This makes her healing frighteningly accurate and her offensive acupuncture much more dangerous.

She still must physically place a needle, touch a target or deliver a qi pulse. The Domain does not remotely control bodies.

**Combat identity:** Aya sees the battlefield as living anatomy in motion.

## #7 Ryo Narukami — Stable Paragon

### Style — Straight Sword / Thunder-Qi Burst Combat

Ryo is fast, aggressive and built around short explosive accelerations rather than sustained top speed.

His Thunder Qi travels through his own nerves, muscles, sword and grounded contact points.

He fights like a series of sudden detonations separated by tiny moments of reset.

### Signature skills

#### **Flashstep — Transcended Movement**

Ryo sends a controlled burst of Thunder Qi through leg muscles and nerves.

The body accelerates violently over a short distance.

It is not teleportation and the movement still has a readable physical line, but the first burst is difficult to react to without Paragon-level speed.

#### **Sky Crack — Transcended Offense**

When Ryo's sword makes physical contact with weapon, armour or body, he can discharge tightly compressed Thunder Qi through the clash point.

The shock is designed to disrupt:

- grip;
- muscle timing;
- immediate stance recovery;
- fine control of the contacted limb.

It is brief neuromuscular disruption rather than giant lightning damage.

#### **Sevenfold Thunder — Supreme Movement / Offense**

Ryo stores seven successive bursts of Thunder Qi through rapid grounded contacts and sword clashes.

He can release those stored bursts in one continuous sequence of accelerations, creating an attack that changes pace multiple times before the final sword strike.

Each burst is physical movement. There is no jumping between pre-marked locations.

### Domain — **Stormstep Dominion**

Inside the Domain, every strong grounded step, landing or weapon clash Ryo completes can generate a temporary **thunder charge** inside his own circulation.

He can hold a limited number of charges and spend them to:

- accelerate suddenly;
- sharpen one directional change;
- add a contact shock to a sword clash;
- recover his stance faster after a violent movement.

He cannot create an infinite charge network across the ground and cannot teleport between locations.

The more aggressively he moves, the faster he reloads—but the more stress he places on his own nerves and muscles.

**Combat identity:** Ryo turns his own movement and clashes into ammunition for the next burst.

## #8 Jun Kajihara — Stable Paragon

### Style — Heavy Halberd / Siege-Breaking Martial Art

Jun is built to destroy things that believe they can remain stationary and defend.

He is strongest against fortifications, shield formations, armour specialists and opponents who repeatedly trust the same guard structure.

### Signature skills

#### **Gatebreaker Halberd — Transcended Offense**

Jun compresses the full force of a heavy halberd swing into a very small impact zone.

Rather than spreading damage across a wall, shield or barrier, the force drives deeply into one structural point.

#### **Second Wall — Transcended Follow-Up**

After a defended impact, Jun immediately adjusts the next strike to the fracture, bent guard or disrupted qi pattern created by the first.

It is not automatically stronger; it is better targeted because the first collision revealed the weakness.

#### **Third Wall Falls — Supreme**

Jun delivers three escalating impacts into the same defensive structure in rapid succession.

Each strike is physically shaped around the damage caused by the previous one.

Against static defence, the third blow can be catastrophic.

### Domain — **Breach Dominion**

Whenever Jun cleanly strikes a defended physical point inside the Domain, a residue of his halberd qi settles into the stress fracture created by the collision.

That fracture becomes easier for Jun to exploit again.

Examples:

- repeatedly blocking with the same section of shield becomes dangerous;
- one damaged barrier node becomes progressively easier to break;
- the same armour plate becomes increasingly vulnerable;
- a weapon repeatedly used to receive his halberd begins accumulating stress at the clash point.

Highly mobile opponents who keep changing guard positions and refuse repeated contact reduce his advantage.

The Domain does not weaken untouched targets.

**Combat identity:** Jun punishes anything that insists on being the same defence twice.

## #9 Eirik Voss — Stable Paragon

### Style — Isgard Longsword and Shield / Formation Command

Eirik's fighting style must instantly look **Isgardian** rather than Shinrin-born.

He uses a long northern sword and round shield. His real strength is not flashy individual technique but the ability to turn several fighters into one disciplined defensive body.

This is the surviving martial art of the ruler he used to be.

### Signature skills

#### **Winter Brace — Transcended Defence**

Eirik sinks into a compact shield stance and distributes incoming force through shield rim, shoulder, hips and rear leg.

Rather than meeting force only with the arm, his entire frame absorbs the collision.

He can use the same stance while protecting someone immediately beside or behind him.

#### **King's Guard — Transcended Defence / Escort**

Eirik moves shield and sword in a paired defensive frame around one nearby ally.

He can catch one attack on the shield while the sword intercepts the follow-up line, creating a moving pocket of protection during retreat or advance.

It is particularly strong during civilian extraction or formation withdrawal.

#### **Last Standard — Supreme Formation Art**

Eirik releases a short, forceful pulse of shield qi and a visible battle signal that allied fighters can consciously synchronize with.

Those who match his breathing, stance timing and guard rhythm for the next exchange can receive attacks in coordinated sequence rather than as isolated individuals.

It is not mind control or supernatural morale. The allies must understand and deliberately follow the formation timing.

### Domain — **Crownwall Dominion**

Eirik's Domain turns **coordinated defence** into a Paragon-level martial system.

Any willing ally inside the Domain who consciously matches Eirik's stance rhythm can link their defensive qi pulse to the formation for short intervals.

When the formation receives a major impact:

- the first defender does not need to absorb everything alone;
- part of the force can be passed through coordinated stance changes into the next defender;
- shield lines recover faster because everyone knows exactly when to brace, yield and re-anchor;
- gaps become harder to exploit because nearby allies can rotate into them without breaking rhythm.

Limits:

- allies must consciously cooperate;
- poor timing breaks the link;
- scattered fighters gain little benefit;
- it does not make weak soldiers equal to Paragons;
- overwhelming force can still break the formation.

When Eirik fights alone, Crownwall Dominion is still useful for his own stance transitions, but it becomes far more dangerous when protecting a disciplined group.

**Combat identity:** Eirik's Domain reminds Isgard why people once followed him—he makes a group survive impacts that would crush them separately.

## #10 Nao Shibasaki — Stable Paragon

### Style — Flowing Jian / Defensive Water-Qi Swordsmanship

Nao uses a jian and soft Water Qi to disperse force instead of confronting it head-on.

Her martial art is protective by nature, fitting one of the more sympathetic Shinrin officers.

### Signature skills

#### **Quiet Lake Guard — Transcended Defence**

Nao receives a heavy attack through circular sword motion and layered body rotation.

Instead of letting one point take the entire impact, she spreads the force through several smaller turns of wrist, elbow, shoulder, waist and footwork.

#### **Rain-Cut Sword — Transcended Offense**

Nao delivers a sequence of light, precise cuts while flowing around the defender's stronger guard.

She does not try to overpower the weapon blocking her. She redirects around it and attacks exposed edges, wrists and openings.

#### **Mercy Current — Supreme Defence / Rescue**

Nao wraps Water Qi around herself and one nearby person.

A major incoming force is split across several flowing vectors, giving the protected person a chance to move away from the centre of impact.

It is primarily a rescue technique rather than a killing art.

### Domain — **Stillwater Dominion**

The Domain fills the nearby battlefield with layered circulating Water Qi.

When incoming physical force enters those currents, Nao can divide part of the impact across several directions rather than receiving it as one concentrated blow.

She can use that redirected motion to:

- shift herself out of the impact centre;
- pull an ally sideways;
- reduce the force reaching a protected civilian;
- turn a direct collision into several smaller, survivable movements.

Limits:

- vastly stronger concentrated attacks can punch through;
- she cannot reflect equal force back like Qin;
- she cannot nullify techniques;
- the Domain is best at defence, rescue and survival rather than overwhelming offense.

**Combat identity:** Nao survives by making one devastating force arrive as many smaller forces that no longer strike together.
