# Final Arc — Phase II Final Prose Audit

> **Status: FINAL PRE-MERGE PROSE AUDIT for Seasons 101–108 / Chapters 361–440.**

## Verdict

Phase II is structurally complete and continuity-ready. No missing season, replacement arc, or major character route remains.

The final audit found and fixed several prose-level defects that were not visible at map stage:

- Chapter 440 originally stepped Rhen out into the corridor while Chapter 441 began with him seated inside the chamber. The staging is now unified: Lu enters the completed chamber, Rhen remains seated, and Chapter 440 ends on **“How long?”**.
- Chapter 440 originally repeated Rhen's first detailed empty-reserve / violet-petal self-test that Chapter 441 immediately dramatizes. Chapter 441 now owns that personal discovery; Chapter 439 keeps the external first sighting of the violet frost.
- Chapter 436 contained a leaked author instruction about not turning Aya into an easy villain. It has been replaced by in-world narration distinguishing Aya's safeguards from the institution that weaponized her work.
- Chapter 432 contained the meta phrase **“The truth of Phase II.”** It has been removed.
- Chapter 399 contained the editorial sentence **“The fight should have been impossible to make easy.”** It has been replaced by in-world narration preserving the same difficulty.
- Chapter 400 had one narrator-note-style **“Good.”** after Tae notices Yurushi's reaction. It has been rewritten as Tae deliberately having the sense not to comment.
- Merged Phase III Chapter 442 says Luo ordered Sera to stop fighting twice. Chapter 435 now explicitly establishes the first order after the Kai/Eirik corridor fight and the second order during month-six strain.
