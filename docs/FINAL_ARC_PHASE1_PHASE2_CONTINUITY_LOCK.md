## 5. Axtaya — exact current doctrine

### 5.1 Medical origin

Aya Katsuragi originally develops **Aya**, a legitimate approximately **20–30% emergency circulatory stimulant** with no meaningful lifespan loss at correct medical dosage.

She later participates in controlled escalation and helps develop the **1.5× tier**. She is uncomfortable with 2×. She does **not** approve routine 3× Redline infantry issue. Shinsei military command continues the militarization over her objections.

Aya is culpable for opening the medical path, but she is not secretly enthusiastic about burning soldiers' lives. Her arc must preserve the distinction between medical intent and institutional militarization.

### 5.2 Standardized working doses

| Tier | Approx. peak window | Average future-vitality cost for a healthy adult |
|---|---:|---:|
| **1.5×** | ~15 minutes | ~6 months |
| **2×** | ~10 minutes | ~2 years |
| **3× Redline** | ~5 minutes | ~5 years |

Axtaya multiplies **combat throughput**, not realm, skill, intelligence or Dominion quality.

Same-day redosing compounds sharply:

- first 3× Redline: ~5 years;
- second same-day Redline: ~10 years;
- third same-day Redline: ~20 years;
- three Redlines in one day can therefore cost roughly **35 years** of future life;
- further same-day Redline use can become immediately fatal.

Once activated, a dose runs its cycle. It cannot be micro-switched off.

Crash consequences can include shaking, vomiting, motor loss, torn muscle, meridian damage, organ damage, collapse and visible aging with repeated use. A body cannot spend vitality it no longer possesses.

Ordinary healing can repair physical Axtaya damage but does **not** restore already-spent lifespan. Whether Rhen's locked Ultimate can override that remains separately approval-gated.

### 5.3 Shinsei frontline doctrine

Every proper frontline soldier assigned to the active Shinsei expedition receives **one sealed 3× Redline dose**. Replacement stock exists in reserves.

Ordinary soldiers are prohibited from casual self-administration except when isolated and facing imminent death or capture. Mass activation requires field-command authorization and is reserved for decisive windows such as:

- fortress breach;
- breaking encirclement;
- killing a command formation;
- seizing a bridge;
- rescuing a Paragon;
- destroying an evacuation corridor;
- forcing a landing.

Redosing is **not** normal doctrine. A second same-day dose is medical-emergency territory. A third is an extreme violation / obsessive overdraw, which is why Haru's later redosing is horrifying even to Shinsei personnel.

Knowledge is tiered:

- Aya / senior medical command know the approximate lifespan cost;
- senior officers know severe long-term vitality damage but may not know exact numbers;
- ordinary soldiers are usually told “emergency stimulant; serious recovery required; repeated use dangerous,” not that one Redline can cost years of life.

### 5.4 Aya's Phase II breakpoint

Aya's detailed Phase II progression is locked in `docs/FINAL_ARC_AYA_BREAKPOINT_LOCK.md`.

Core continuity:

- Jun's recovery demonstrates why Aya stayed inside Shinsei;
- the first mass Redline aftermath becomes a medical horror when Hundred Pulse Dominion shows her hundreds of bodies carrying lifespan loss that cannot be healed;
- she learns ordinary soldiers were not properly told the true lifespan cost;
- she begins active medical resistance from inside Shinsei rather than instantly defecting;

- Aya confronts Tsubasa, who tightens controls but refuses to end 3× Redline during the war;
- Hana, Aya and Nao begin deliberately overlapping their harm-reduction work without yet becoming a formal rebel faction;
- Aya's final Phase III institutional choice remains open.

## 8. Phase I — mandatory chronology

### 8.1 Peace / first Axtaya case

The first Axtaya case originates through Isgard cooperation. A Peak-Marquis-class fighter produces impossible output, crashes and is captured alive. The body shows torn meridians, internal damage and abnormal vitality depletion. A recovered sample reaches Luo and Yun.

Luo approaches it as medicine; Yun as poison. Their laboratory work must also serve their established romance. Lu Weiran follows the supply chain rather than the chemistry.

The substance is deliberately layered to stimulate circulation, suppress warning responses, force vitality into throughput and delay collapse. Its construction reveals serious medical knowledge.

A second voluntary victim establishes that users are being lied to about long-term cost. Dosage charts, body-weight categories and pulse screening imply a physician-designed precursor.

The trade trail yields the name **Axtaya**, then the distant place-name **Shinrin**.

### 8.2 Yun leaves openly, not irresponsibly

Yun tells Sera she is tracing a foreign poison / pharmaceutical network. At this stage nobody knows Shinrin has ten Paragons and a 200,000+ elite army.

Luo wants to accompany her. Yun refuses because two investigators double the profile and because someone in Wuyue must understand whatever evidence she sends back.

She expects **two to three weeks**. Their goodbye establishes a three-week expectation. Luo becomes the first person to know something is wrong when contact does not resume.

### 8.3 Shinrin discovery

Yun initially sees a prosperous, functioning society: maintained roads, irrigation, safe trade, clinics, granaries and disciplined law. Shinsei's positive historical achievements must be visible before its coercive logic is exposed.

She gradually learns:

- Shinsei Guild is effectively Shinrin's government;
- ordinary military elites can be Peak Marquis;
- Axtaya production is institutionally connected;
- early medical records signed by Aya contain safeguards that later military documents override;
- stockpiles are too large for black-market use;
- logistics, maps and fleet studies point to an Isgard invasion.

### 8.4 Shunto cat-and-mouse

Shunto Takamori is properly introduced as **Shinsei No.2 / High Paragon**, a capture specialist.

Yun escapes multiple encounters by making his circulation choices and expectation of poison part of the battlefield. She seriously poisons him once by exploiting predictable rerouting created by his own locking method. She never defeats him in direct power.

Over roughly three months she learns his position, the scale of the Ten Seats and enough military logistics to reconstruct the invasion.

### 8.5 Yun's evidence

Before final capture, Yun completes three layers:

1. physical proof — Axtaya sample / fragments;
2. coded medical notebook using private notation Luo can decode;
3. memorized essential facts.

She reconstructs the truth from warehouses, requisitions, maps, troop movement, shipping and medical paperwork; there is no convenient master file containing every secret.

Yun learns hierarchy / approximate power, invasion scale and Shunto's methods. She does **not** return with a wiki explaining every Shinsei Dominion.

### 8.6 Capture and assault

Shunto finally wins because he adapts: protected circulation, antidotes, controlled exits, no needless contact and a deliberately open escape lane that is actually the trap.

The sexual assault remains **non-graphic, non-erotic and consequence-focused**. Yun clearly refuses. Shunto's motive is control, entitlement and punishment. He makes a deliberate choice.

Yun remains active afterward: she inventories guard timing, medicines, restraints and weakening meridian locks and turns the prison environment into part of her escape plan.

### 8.7 Yurushi and Hana in the escape

Yurushi, Black Radiance, encounters the badly injured foreign prisoner and helps because the treatment is wrong before he knows her exact Wuyue identity.

He initially gives deniable assistance and later commits directly. Yun remains an active escape agent rather than a rescued damsel.

Yurushi's years-old civilian escape network becomes the route out. Hana quietly changes one patrol after an incomplete request from Yurushi. She knows someone is being hunted wrongly but does not know the full Yun / invasion context. Her help remains plausibly deniable.

Yun leaves Shinrin through a medical / export route toward Isgard. Yurushi stays temporarily because civilians still depend on his hidden routes.

### 8.8 Rhen's seclusion timing

Across Phase I, prose must foreshadow Rhen's qi reserve refilling too quickly, increasing suppression difficulty, unintended frost and the sense that his old reserve is physically too diffuse.

He tells Sera he needs the qi to take less space. Their final night before seclusion is intimate and adult but non-explicit. He explains that once condensation passes the early stage, forced interruption risks catastrophic meridian / body failure.

Rhen enters seclusion **exactly one day before Yun reaches Isgard and the warning is sent to Wuyue**. He does not enter because of Yun or Shinsei.

### 8.9 Yun's final report / knowledge boundary

Yun reaches Isgard alive but beyond recovery. Her military warning prioritizes:

- Shinrin / Shinsei identity;
- Axtaya and its broad effect;
- more than 200,000 trained elites;
- ordinary elite floor around Peak Marquis;
- multiple / all ten ranked officers at Paragon;
- Shunto is No.2 and High Paragon;
- Tsubasa is stronger;
- Isgard invasion preparation.

She says Shunto captured her and that an insider helped her escape. She does **not** spend her final moments describing the sexual assault in detail.

Yun dies in Isgard. Isgard warns Wuyue immediately under the defensive pact.

### 8.10 The later truth about Shunto

After Yurushi reaches Wuyue, he privately tells Sera what condition he found Yun in and what he knows / can infer Shunto did. Sera then tells Luo privately.

Do not make the entire Top Ten instantly omniscient. This creates a second, later wave of anger for Sera and especially Luo.

## 10. Shinsei intelligence asymmetry and why Tsubasa invades anyway

Shinsei does **not** have omniscient deep spies everywhere in Wuyue and Isgard.

Its foreign picture is built from centralized collection of:

- merchant guilds;
- port authorities;
- ship captains;
- foreign traders;
- mercenary reports;
- intercepted correspondence;
- refugee interviews;
- purchased intermediary intelligence.

### 10.1 What Shinsei broadly knows

- Wuyue is the dominant martial continent;
- Wuyue and Isgard fought a costly war;
- they later signed a formal defensive pact;
- the public Wuyue Top Ten and Sera / Pale Orchid are major figures;
- Wuyue is still rebuilding;
- Isgard was devastated further by a natural epidemic;
- Kael's greatest military value is tied to Stonecrown fixed defence;
- Sera's household / tea house is associated with medicine and support;
- Rhen is publicly a famous healer associated with Sera.

### 10.2 What Shinsei does not know

- Tae, Huo and Qin are High Paragons;
- Quaint contains 1,000 mature Marquis-or-higher members;
- the exact Quaint command structure;
- Rhen's true combat ceiling or Frozen Petals Garden refinement mechanics;
- exact Black March strategic-weight calculations;
- complete post-war hidden growth of Wuyue's armies.

Wuyue may have heard “Shinrin” through distant trade while still failing to understand that Shinsei is one centralized government with ten official Paragons and 200,000+ trained elites. Shinsei's state bureaucracy has a reason to collate foreign reports; Wuyue historically had little reason to build the same picture of remote Shinrin.

### 10.3 Tsubasa knows the defensive pact

Tsubasa invades **knowing Wuyue may intervene**. He predicts cautious aid because Wuyue recently paid a devastating price fighting Isgard and because Kael's strongest army remains homeland-oriented.

The original Shinsei invasion schedule is roughly four months away. Yun escapes with warning, so Tsubasa compresses the timetable to approximately **two weeks**, accepting incomplete Wuyue intelligence in exchange for striking before Wuyue can mobilize.

This gamble initially works: Isgard is badly mauled before Wuyue arrives. The long-term cost is that Shinsei never completes the deeper intelligence work that would have exposed Wuyue's hidden layer.

Rhen's public medical availability ends when he enters closed retreat. Shinsei can therefore infer he is unavailable without knowing why, how dangerous interruption is or what the seclusion will produce.

- initial landing: approximately **25,000** + five Paragons;
- after Wuyue arrives and Jun is defeated: rises toward **70,000–75,000**, with Kai reinforcing;
- later Phase II can exceed **100,000 involved across forward bases, occupation zones, maritime logistics and replacement waves**, without ever pretending all 200,000 stand on one battlefield.

### 14.1 First mass Redline reveal

Wuyue initially wins a meaningful counterattack and begins to think the battlefield is understood. Then a Shinsei formation authorizes mass **3× Redline**.
