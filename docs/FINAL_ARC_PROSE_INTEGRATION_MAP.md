## A. Phase I scene chain that must be dramatized

### A1. Peace before the fracture

Do not open on Shinsei exposition.

Show first:

- normal Quaint Teahouse business;
- Sera / Rhen domestic rhythm and mature romance;
- the 1,000-member organization as a lived institution rather than one paragraph of numbers;
- short evidence that Wuyue armies and Arin's sect are still rebuilding;
- Isgard recovering beneath the defensive pact;
- Rhen asking Sera before restoring Frozen Petals Garden over the core.

The passive scene must preserve Sera's agency. She had previously asked to stand on her own. Her eventual consent now means the passive is an extra layer after she has proved that independence, not a replacement for it.

### A2. First Axtaya victim → Luo / Yun laboratory

Use an Isgard-captured Peak-Marquis-class fighter who briefly produces impossible throughput and then crashes.

The examination must distinguish:

- higher **output** from higher **cultivation**;
- physical injury from lost vitality;
- physician thinking from poisoner thinking.

The laboratory chapters also establish Luo / Yun as a comfortable couple before Yun leaves.

Useful tonal model, not mandatory verbatim dialogue:

> Luo calls the compound strength. Yun calls it a body being forced to become suicidal.

Lu Weiran follows shipping, invoices and mislabeled medical cargo so his expertise contributes without pretending he understands poisoncraft.

### A3. Second victim / Axtaya name / physician origin

A voluntary user who believed Axtaya was merely a temporary cultivation enhancer demonstrates that sellers conceal the cost.

Body-weight charts, pulse screening and dosage categories should make Yun / Luo realize a physician designed the original medical logic.

The name **Axtaya** and the foreign place-name **Shinrin** should emerge from trade evidence rather than an exposition narrator.

### A4. Yun's departure

Yun openly briefs Sera. She believes she is tracing a dangerous foreign pharmaceutical / smuggling network, not knowingly entering a continent with ten Paragons.

Luo wants to go. Yun refuses for operational reasons and because someone in Wuyue must understand the evidence she returns.

Establish their expected two-to-three-week absence window and a small intimate goodbye. The later overdue silence must be measurable against this promise.

### A5. Shinrin's first impression

Show why ordinary Shinrin citizens can genuinely value Shinsei:

- safe roads;
- repaired bridges;
- irrigation;
- clinics;
- grain systems;
- disciplined patrols;
- reduced clan violence.

Only afterward let Yun realize the same centralized system contains an extraordinary standing military and institutionally connected Axtaya production.

### A6. Shunto pursuit progression

Do not make the first meeting the final capture.

Across multiple encounters:

- Shunto learns Yun's environmental poison tricks;
- Yun learns his capture locks and circulation requirements;
- Yun seriously poisons him once without pretending a Sovereign directly overpowers a High Paragon;
- she eventually identifies him as Shinsei No.2 / High Paragon;
- his eventual successful capture comes from adaptation, not Yun suddenly becoming careless.

### A7. Evidence before capture

Before Yun is caught, establish the physical sample, Luo-coded notebook and memorized essentials.

The invasion conclusion should be reconstructed from many mundane pieces — requisitions, shipping, troop movements, maps, medical stock — so Yun earns the discovery.

### A8. Captivity / assault / escape

Keep the sexual assault non-graphic, unambiguous and consequence-focused.

Immediately afterward, return narrative agency to Yun through observation and escape planning.

Yurushi's assistance should begin deniably and grow into direct opposition. Yun does not instantly trust him. Hana's one patrol alteration remains plausibly administrative because she lacks the full context.

### A9. Yurushi's old routes

The escape should reveal that Black Radiance has been quietly helping civilians for years.

Use hidden medicine caches, forgotten clan routes, merchant credentials and patrol gaps. Yurushi is not inventing a perfect network for Yun in one night.

He gets Yun onto the route toward Isgard but stays behind briefly because other civilians still depend on those corridors.

### A10. Rhen foreshadowing and seclusion

Before the seclusion chapter, repeatedly show:

- qi returning too quickly;
- suppressed aura becoming harder to keep ordinary;
- accidental frost;
- Rhen feeling physically “too full.”

The final Sera / Rhen night should be warm and intimate before the war cuts them apart. Explicitly establish that interruption after condensation begins can catastrophically damage Rhen.

The next-day Yun message is tragic timing, not causation.

### A11. Yun's final report

Yun prioritizes military facts, not a complete trauma disclosure.

She reaches Isgard beyond recovery, confirms the warning and dies.

The report contains enough to launch the defensive pact response, but not every Shinsei Domain or every officer biography.

### A12. Xie Wuchen mini-arc woven throughout Phase I

Do not insert Xie only after Yun dies.

Earlier scenes must include:

- Shen Rui recognizing the former masked Wind-Qi fighter who once defeated him while Rui acted on compromised intelligence;
- Xie refusing rank and affiliation;
- Sera challenging his habit of helping and leaving;
- Xie assisting around Lu's port / route work while insisting he remains detached.

After Yun dies, leave No.10 empty long enough to feel like a loss. Then dramatize Rui proposing Xie and Sera making clear he is not replacing Yun.

His acceptance line / idea is: **he is taking the empty number, not replacing her.**

## B. Phase II opening scene chain

### B1. Isgard alone for one month

The opening invasion must prove Shinsei Paragons are serious before Wuyue arrives.

Five initial Paragons:

- Eirik;
- Aya;
- Kenji;
- Jun;
- Nao.

Mandatory consequences:

- Halvek dies;
- Raska dies;
- Tor retires permanently;
- Eldran retires / is crippled;
- Solveig remains active but injured;
- Aldric, Maedra and Sigrun all survive in terrible condition.

Sigrun's **Graven Dominion** must be explained during the evacuation hold, including the cost when Last Body Standing ends.

Do not summarize this entire month in one casualty paragraph. At least several losses must be experienced in scene so the later Wuyue anger is earned.

### B2. Wuyue mobilization

Use the revised post-war army rather than the old previous-war 131,000 figure.

Reader-facing deployment: approximately **64,000 public personnel + Sera's 120 Quaint**.

Prose should make clear:

- armies are still rebuilding;
- Arin has only about 2,100 field-ready swordswomen and commits about 1,800;
- Kael sends only about 6,000 because most Stonecrown fixed defence remains home;
- Luo and Xie do not suddenly acquire invented personal legions;
- 60% is strategic weight, not headcount.

The army must be experienced through marching time, supply traffic, ships, medicine and command load rather than a pure stat dump.

### B3. Sera arrives to support before attacking

Sera's 120 immediately split into medicine, evacuation, reconstruction, logistics, reconnaissance and security tasks.

The first emotional proof of the pact is that Wuyue keeps Isgard alive.

Sera sees the three injured Paragons and the missing / retired commanders before the first Wuyue counterattack.

### B4. First counterattack / Jun defeat

The alliance wins one meaningful beachhead and captures Axtaya.

Jun is badly injured but not removed from the story. Aya treats him. The treatment takes several days to roughly a week, and his first return is around 70–80% combat condition.

Tsubasa sends Kai north because Jun's temporary loss plus Wuyue's arrival changed the arithmetic.

### B5. First mass Redline panic

Do not reveal the standard-issue 3× doctrine merely in a briefing.

Let Wuyue believe it has one formation contained. Then Shinsei authorizes Redline across thousands of troops and breaks lines that should have held.

The scene must distinguish tripled throughput from promotion in cultivation.

Luo recognizes the medical horror. Jin throws away a good plan quickly enough to preserve the army.

### B6. Five-Minute Retreat and Shinsei counter-adaptation

Jin develops the controlled retreat after studying timing with Luo.

Show at least one successful use before Kai counters with staggered waves. The war should feel adaptive: neither side discovers one tactic that permanently solves the other.

### B7. Kai arrives

Properly reintroduce Kai as Shinsei No.3 / High Paragon.

His first use is strategic, not an ego duel. A Kael clash should visibly establish the gap between newly Paragon Kael and experienced High Paragon Kai without humiliating Kael.

Sera intervenes when Kai's pressure becomes an army problem. Orchid Dominion's Counter-Bloom rule must be clear in the scene. Kai withdraws once his assigned objective is met rather than fighting for pride.

## C. Yurushi recruitment and home-front chain

### C1. Yurushi reaches Wuyue before Sera deploys

After Yun's death, Yurushi spends several days dismantling / handing off civilian routes under growing Shunto suspicion, then leaves for Wuyue.

He is useful because he brings **inside Shinsei knowledge**, not because Wuyue needs another random fighter in Isgard.

Do not have Sera instantly trust him. Let his old humanitarian trail, his account of Yun and his behaviour beside Quaint earn the invitation.

A light running beat can show him attending operations, moving supplies and drinking tea while insisting he has not joined.

Sera formally names him **Sixth Petal** before the Wuyue expedition leaves.

### C2. Why Yurushi stays home

Yurushi should initially assume he is going to Isgard.

Sera tells him to stay because her public apex is leaving and Shinsei may exploit it. His knowledge of Shinsei's targeting logic is more valuable in Wuyue.

This conversation is required. Without it, Yurushi's later presence at the Kael-region attack feels convenient.

### C3. Haru / Hana attack Quaint

Their target is Sera's support / home hub, not a secret 1,000-person army they already know about.

The shock is finding three hidden High Paragons.

Explain all five Domains in action. Tae → Huo → Qin take turns rather than immediately dogpiling. Qin wins through sustainable battlefield control, not automatic Domain cancellation.

### C4. Qin redistributes the hidden Paragons

After the attack, Qin sends Tae and Huo to separate vulnerable regions while he remains central. This is a strategic reaction, not plot convenience.

Haru / Hana then strike a vulnerable command / logistics node in Kael's Central Region rather than attempting to beat the entire home Stonecrown army.

### C5. Yurushi alone / Twilight Dominion

Yurushi reaches the target first and chooses to defend it.

This is his first full Twilight Dominion showcase. Make clear that all movement is physical: connected darkness conceals routes; Light Qi accelerates real travel.

Hana knows his habits from their four-year relationship, making the 2v1 dangerous without melodrama.

### C6. Haru's three Redlines

Haru takes one Redline because Yurushi is solving his precision battlefield. He later redoses despite Hana's objections and can reach a third dose.

Hana does not take Axtaya.

The scene must show the cost beginning while the power is still active: shaking, damaged circulation, the sense that Haru is burning future life to solve a present problem.

Yurushi delays but eventually becomes seriously injured because he refuses to abandon the objective.

### C7. Qin sends Tae through intelligence relay

Quaint scouts and relay signals report the battle and abnormal spikes. Qin does not sense the distant fight supernaturally.

Tae arrives while Haru is still at 3×.

### C8. Tae wins the hard fight

Iron Chain Dominion punishes Haru's accelerated aggression. Haru can break chains with Redline output, but each continued hostile action creates more chains and burns more borrowed qi.

Tae's years of Frozen Petals Garden refinement explain his superior sustainable foundation. The fight is not effortless.

Tae lets Haru / Hana retreat and brings Yurushi home. The emotional payoff is belonging: someone finally came for the man who spent years building escape routes for everyone else.

## D. Information boundaries that prose must preserve

### D1. Shinsei foreign intelligence

Tsubasa knows the Wuyue–Isgard defensive pact exists and expects some Wuyue intervention.

He does **not** know the hidden Quaint roster, Tae / Huo / Qin's High-Paragon status, Rhen's real ceiling or exact Black March math.

His mistake is underestimating commitment and hidden post-war growth, made worse by accelerating the invasion from four months to about two weeks after Yun escapes.

### D2. Yun's knowledge

Yun knows strategy / hierarchy / Axtaya / invasion preparation, plus Shunto's methods from direct experience.

She does not know every Dominion mechanic. Wuyue must learn Kai, Eirik, Kenji, Jun, Nao and other Domain rules in live combat.

### D3. Shunto assault disclosure

The Top Ten do not all learn the sexual-assault details from Yun's final report.

Yurushi later tells Sera privately what he found / understood. Sera tells Luo. This is a later second wave of grief and rage.

### D4. Hana's deniability

Shunto can suspect Yurushi strongly. He cannot prove Hana knowingly helped Yun. Her patrol adjustment must remain explainable as ordinary internal administration so Tsubasa can plausibly still deploy her.
