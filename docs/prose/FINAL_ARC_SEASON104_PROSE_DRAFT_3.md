## Chapter 398 — One Dose Becomes Three

The first Redline made Haru Ishikawa brilliant.

That was the danger.

He swallowed the 3× dose.

Five seconds later his pulse changed.

Ten seconds later his circulation stopped behaving like something meant to last decades.

Axtaya forced throughput.

Not cultivation.

Not a realm.

Haru remained High Paragon.

He simply became capable of spending High-Paragon output at a rate his body had no business sustaining.

His spear moved.

Yurushi barely saw the first thrust.

Not because Haru had teleported.

Because the distance between decision and physical execution had collapsed beneath overwhelming circulatory acceleration.

Yurushi crossed into shadow.

Haru had already stored the line.

He emerged elsewhere.

Second thrust.

Third.

Fourth.

Carved Spear Dominion filled Twilight with geometry faster than Yurushi could erase safe routes by changing state.

Hana moved too.

Not boosted.

Still herself. She used Haru’s new pace as structure rather than trying to imitate it.

Hollow Sky pressure pockets took away Yurushi’s easiest breathing corridors.

Haru’s spear lines filled the remaining exits.

For the first time, Twilight Dominion stopped feeling like Yurushi’s battlefield.

He blocked one spear.

The force drove him through a wooden support.

Hana’s blade arrived before he landed.

He turned with Night Severance.

Darkness Qi scattered part of her reinforcement.

Still not enough.

The sword cut his shoulder.

Yurushi hit the ground.

Haru was already there.

Piercing Dawn met spear haft.

The collision burst light across the road.

Haru did not give ground.

Three-times throughput.

Not three-times skill.

Not three-times foundation.

But when the person using it was already Haru Ishikawa, that distinction became academic very quickly.

Yurushi twisted aside.

Spear opened his flank.

Blood hit stone.

Hana looked at Haru.

[[speaker:hana]]“How long?”

[[speaker:haru]]“Four minutes thirty.”

[[speaker:hana]]“You sound proud.”

[[speaker:haru]]“I am timing.”

[[speaker:hana]]“Haru.”

[[speaker:haru]]“I know.”

Did he?

Yurushi heard the answer in the way Haru attacked.

Too clean.

Too certain.

The dose was solving the thing Haru hated most.

Being unable to find the correct line.

For a man who believed enough measurement could produce the right answer, Redline felt like proof that the answer had only needed more output.

That was worse than addiction to power.

It was addiction to being correct.

Yurushi expanded darkness across a row of buildings.

Haru stabbed once into the route.

Stored line.

Yurushi changed exits.

Second.

Third.

Haru was carving tomorrow’s battlefield faster than Yurushi could spend today’s.

The first Redline began to fade.

Yurushi felt it before Haru admitted it.

The spear slowed by a fraction.

Breathing changed.

A tremor touched Haru’s left hand.

Hana saw.

[[speaker:hana]]“Enough.”

Haru stepped back.

For one moment, Yurushi thought he had listened.

Then Haru reached for a second vial.

Hana caught his wrist.

[[speaker:hana]]“No.”

Haru looked at her.

[[speaker:haru]]“We are thirty seconds from the command archive.”

[[speaker:hana]]“Then we withdraw.”

[[speaker:haru]]“We can finish.”

[[speaker:hana]]“The first dose is ending.”

[[speaker:haru]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:hana]]“That is the signal.”

[[speaker:haru]]“No. It is a timing constraint.”

Hana stared.

Yurushi, bleeding twenty paces away, almost felt sorry for him.

Almost.

“Haru,” Hana said, quieter. “Tsubasa said no heroics.”

[[speaker:haru]]“This is not heroics.”

[[speaker:hana]]“It is exactly heroics with a ruler.”

He pulled his wrist free.

The second seal broke.

Hana’s face went white with anger.

Yurushi’s humor disappeared completely.

Second same-day Redline.

The cost did not reset because the clock did.

Aya’s old safeguards had been explicit about that.

Repeated overdraw compounded.

Rough field estimate:

First 3× dose — around five future years.

Second same-day dose — potentially around ten more.

Third — worse again.

Haru drank.

His body objected before the power arrived.

One blood vessel burst in his eye.

His fingers shook.

Then the throughput surged again.

Hana swore at him.

Haru attacked.

Yurushi stopped trying to win.

Completely.

He used Twilight to **delay**.

Shadow to force Haru to spend movement.

Light to pull him across unnecessary distance.

Radiant Step to relocate the fight away from the last evacuation route.

Black Veil to make Haru carve spear lines into empty geometry.

Every second mattered because Redline was a clock disguised as strength.

Haru knew that.

So did Yurushi.

That turned the fight into arithmetic.

Hana hated arithmetic now.

She saw Haru’s hand shake harder.

Saw qi leaking through microtears along one forearm.

Saw the skin beneath his collar flush too dark.

[[speaker:hana]]“Haru.”

No answer.

[[speaker:hana]]“Haru!”

He struck Yurushi across the ribs.

Something cracked.

Yurushi hit a stone wall.

Twilight Dominion flickered.

Hana moved toward him.

Not to finish.

To keep Haru from doing something worse.

That hesitation cost her objective time.

Yurushi coughed blood.

Still smiled.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“You always did have terrible timing.”

Hana’s eyes flashed.

[[speaker:hana]]“Shut up.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Comforting.”

Haru’s second dose began fading.

The command archive remained intact.

The last civilians were gone.

Medical stores had been moved.

Yurushi had succeeded.

He could leave now.

Except Haru saw the retreat path too.

The spear crossed it.

Stored lines closed behind Yurushi.

Hana looked at Haru.

Then at his belt.

One vial left.

Her face changed.

[[speaker:hana]]“No.”

Haru’s breathing was ragged.

His right hand shook visibly now.

He reached for it anyway.

Hana stepped between his hand and the vial.

[[speaker:hana]]“I said no.”

Haru stared at her.

[[speaker:haru]]“The objective is unfinished.”

[[speaker:hana]]“The objective is lost.”

[[speaker:haru]]“We are one breakthrough from—”

[[speaker:hana]]“You don’t know that.”

[[speaker:haru]]“I do.”

[[speaker:hana]]“No.”

Hana’s voice cracked like a blade.

[[speaker:hana]]“You know the line you want to be true.”

That hit him.

For a second.

Only a second.

Then Haru looked past her at Yurushi.

Still standing.

Barely.

Still between Shinsei and the objective.

The strategist made the worst possible calculation.

He confused **not yet winning** with **almost winning**.

He took the third vial.

Hana slapped it from his hand.

Haru caught it before it hit the ground.

Their eyes met.

Four years from now—if Haru still had four years—he would remember that look.

Then he drank.

Third same-day 3× Redline.

Yurushi felt the surge and knew immediately someone needed to be coming.

Not sensed magically.

Not hoped for vaguely.

Quaint relay scouts had already witnessed two impossible throughput spikes.

Qin would understand.

If the network worked, somebody was moving.

Yurushi only had to stay alive long enough for the network to matter.

Haru raised his spear.

Blood ran from his nose.

His body trembled.

His output became monstrous.

Thirty-five years of future life had just become a plausible medical estimate for one afternoon.

Hana whispered:

[[speaker:hana]]“You idiot.”

Haru attacked.

## Chapter 399 — Tae Arrives Before the Crash

Qin Luo dispatched Tae Muyeon during the second Redline spike.

Not the third.

That was why Tae arrived in time.

The relay worked exactly as Lu had designed it.

First report:

**Abnormal High-Paragon output. Possible Redline.**

Second, four minutes later:

**Output dropped, then returned to same 3× profile. Same hostile signature. Repeat use likely.**

Qin did not sense a distant battle through magic.

He read the report.

Looked at the map.

Tae was already stationed in the western-central response belt after redistribution.

[[speaker:qin]]“Send him now.”

The courier left.

By the time the third spike appeared, Tae was already moving.

Anchor Step turned roads, walls, bridge stones and wagon frames into launch points.

Not teleportation.

Violent physical acceleration chained through whatever solid surface he could touch.

He reached the central region with enough force that the last roof tile he pushed from shattered behind him.

Then stopped.

The battlefield looked wrong.

Yurushi was on one knee.

Blood down one side.

Twilight Dominion barely stable.

Hana’s Hollow Sky pockets had torn half the courtyard’s loose roofing away.

Haru stood in the center.

His body shook.

His qi did not.

That was the horrifying part.

The circulation was driving harder than the flesh containing it.

Tae understood.

[[speaker:tae]]“Three?”

Hana looked at him.

[[speaker:hana]]“Yes.”

Tae stared at Haru.

[[speaker:tae]]“You took three?”

Haru moved.

The spear hit Tae before the question finished.

Tae blocked with Ironheart.

Still flew through a stone wall.

The impact emptied his lungs.

He landed badly.

Haru was already there.

Second thrust.

Tae used Anchor Step against the broken wall and snapped sideways.

The spear grazed his ribs.

Third.

Stored line.

Fourth.

Stored line.

Tae understood immediately why Yurushi was losing.

Not because Yurushi was weak.

Because this version of Haru was spending himself faster than a sane opponent could justify matching.

Tae stood.

Iron Chain Dominion opened.

Haru attacked.

A chain formed.

He broke it instantly with Redline output.

Tae’s eyes narrowed.

Haru attacked again.

Two chains.

Broke both.

Again.

Three.

Broke them.

Tae smiled despite blood in his mouth.

There.

Haru saw it.

[[speaker:haru]]“What?”

[[speaker:tae]]“You’re solving the wrong problem.”

Haru thrust.

Tae barely avoided it.

Another chain formed.

Haru shattered it.

Tae laughed.

[[speaker:tae]]“Exactly.”

Hana understood first.

[[speaker:hana]]“Haru. Stop breaking them.”

He ignored her.

Iron Chain Dominion did not care that Haru was temporarily strong enough to shatter every restraint.

Breaking them cost qi.

Moving created more.

Attacking created more.

Defending created more.

Redline gave Haru an enormous throughput advantage.

Tae’s Domain turned that advantage into a machine for **spending it faster**.

Every solution Haru used accelerated his crash.

Haru drove Tae backward anyway.

One spear line cut Tae’s thigh.

Another struck his chest guard hard enough to fracture two ribs beneath it.

Hana entered from the side.

Tae turned too late.

Twin Gale Crescent opened his shoulder and back.

Two opponents.

One High Paragon at triple throughput.

One Established Paragon who knew how to build around him.

Tae was losing.

Actually losing.

There was no shame in it. Two Paragons—one of them burning himself at triple throughput—were supposed to be a disaster.

He anchored against a stone column.

Haru hit him.

Column cracked.

Ironheart reinforced Tae’s spine and shoulder at impact.

Still hurt.

Haru triggered three stored spear lines.

Tae broke one with Horizon Break.

Second caught his arm.

Third he took across reinforced ribs because dodging would create more positional loss.

Pain went white.

Tae stayed standing.

Hana saw the calculation.

[[speaker:hana]]“He’s waiting.”

Haru’s eyes sharpened.

[[speaker:haru]]“For what?”

Tae answered.

[[speaker:tae]]“You.”

The third Redline still had time.

Not much.

Haru’s tremor worsened.

Blood ran from both nostrils now.

One ear.

Microtears in the meridians were becoming macro damage.

He forced circulation harder.

Chains formed thicker.

Tae stopped trying to win exchanges.

He made Haru **pay** for them.

Haru thrust.

Chain.

Broke.

Moved.

Chain.

Broke.

Triggered stored line.

Chain.

Broke.

Hana shouted:

[[speaker:hana]]“STOP BREAKING THEM.”

Haru finally listened.

He let three chains remain.

His spear slowed.

That was the choice.

Spend future qi to remain free.

Or accept burden and lose tempo.

Tae advanced for the first time.

Horizon Break struck Haru’s spear shaft.

One physical collision.

Then layered iron-pressure impacts collapsed through the same vector.

Haru’s grip broke.

Not bone.

Grip.

The spear spun away.

Hana moved to recover it.

Tae used Anchor Step off the ground and intercepted.

Her sword caught his forearm.

Ironheart hardened at contact.

He still bled.

She still stopped him.

Then Haru attacked barehanded.

Tae stared.

[[speaker:tae]]“You really don’t know when to stop.”

Haru’s fist hit him in the jaw.

Tae saw white.

Chains wrapped both Haru’s arms.

He broke them.

Tae smiled through split lips.

[[speaker:tae]]“Thanks.”

Haru finally understood.

Too late.

The third Redline clock reached its final minute.

His circulation began collapsing while the Dominion burden was at its highest.

His knees shook.

Hana saw it.

Yurushi, still on one knee, saw it.

Tae stepped forward.

Haru tried to step back.

Chains formed.

He nearly fell.

Tae raised one fist.

Not an Ultimate.

Not a new technique.

Just Ironheart and years of foundation refined beneath Frozen Petals Garden.

The punch landed in Haru’s chest.

Haru flew backward.

His Domain collapsed.

The Redline did not.

For twenty more seconds, his body remained capable of absurd output while no longer coordinated enough to use it properly.

That was more frightening than the peak.

Hana reached him first.

[[speaker:hana]]“Haru.”

He tried to stand.

She shoved him down.

[[speaker:hana]]“No.”

Tae approached.

Hollow Sky Dominion widened.

Not as attack.

As space.

Pressure pockets broke line-of-approach, dust and debris surging between them.

Tae could have pushed through.

He looked at Yurushi.

Critical.

Looked at the command node.

Still standing.

Looked back at Hana dragging Haru toward retreat.

Sera’s lethal-force rule existed.

No execution authorization.

But even without it, chasing two retreating Paragons while abandoning the defended objective and a dying Sixth Petal would have been stupid.

Tae stopped.

Hana saw.

Their eyes met across the collapsing pressure field.

She nodded once.

Not thanks.

Acknowledgment.

Then she took Haru and left.

Tae exhaled.

The moment they vanished, he dropped to one knee.

Yurushi laughed weakly.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“You looked impressive until that part.”

Tae turned his head.

[[speaker:tae]]“You look dead.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Not yet.”

[[speaker:tae]]“Annoying.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Frequently.”

The third Redline ended somewhere beyond the hills.

Haru Ishikawa’s body began collecting the bill.

## Chapter 400 — Take Him Before I Change My Mind

Hana carried Haru for the first mile.

Then he vomited blood on her shoulder.

She stopped.

Not because of the blood.

Because his pulse vanished for one beat.

Then returned wrong.

[[speaker:hana]]“Haru.”

No answer.

She lowered him against a tree.

His eyes were open.

Not focused.

His fingers twitched around a spear that was no longer there.

Three doses.

Three.

Hana had spent years around Aya Katsuragi.

Enough to know what that meant even without precise numbers.

This was not exhaustion.

Not a heroic collapse.

His body had spent something it could not rebuild.

[[speaker:hana]]“Haru.”

His gaze found her.

[[speaker:haru]]“We lost.”

Hana stared.

Of all the things he could say.

She slapped him.

Hard.

His head turned.

He blinked.

[[speaker:haru]]“What—”

[[speaker:hana]]“You weren’t dying.”

He frowned.

[[speaker:hana]]“You were losing.”

Hana’s voice shook.

“And you burned yourself because you couldn’t tolerate the difference.”

Haru looked away.

That was the first time the sentence existed.

Aya would say it later with medical precision.

Hana said it now because she was furious enough to name the truth before anyone had a chart.

Haru closed his eyes.

[[speaker:haru]]“We need to move.”

[[speaker:hana]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:haru]]“You should leave me if—”

She slapped him again.

His eyes opened.

[[speaker:haru]]“That was less deserved.”

[[speaker:hana]]“No, it wasn’t.”

Hana got his arm over her shoulder.

They moved.

Back at the central node, Tae reached Yurushi.

The Sixth Petal had managed to stand.

Badly.

One hand against a broken wall.

Blood down his thigh, ribs and shoulder.

Twilight Dominion gone.

Tae looked him over.

[[speaker:tae]]“How many things are broken?”

Yurushi considered.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:tae]]“Helpful.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“I learned from physicians.”

Tae crouched.

[[speaker:tae]]“Get on.”

Yurushi stared.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“No.”

[[speaker:tae]]“You can barely stand.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“I can walk.”

[[speaker:tae]]“You are leaning on architecture.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“It is supportive.”

Tae looked at him.

Yurushi looked back.

Tae’s voice became dangerously calm.

[[speaker:tae]]“Get. On.”

Yurushi hesitated.

Something old moved through his face.

For years, escape had meant getting everyone else out.

He stayed.

Held routes.

Took the rear.

Made sure another person reached safety before he did.

Nobody came back for the route-maker because the route-maker had designed things specifically so nobody needed to.

Tae saw something change and had the sense not to comment.

Yurushi finally put one arm over his shoulder.

Tae stood.

[[speaker:tae]]“Heavy.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“I am injured.”

[[speaker:tae]]“That does not reduce mass.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“You are insufferable.”

[[speaker:tae]]“So I’ve heard.”

They crossed the damaged node slowly.

Quaint relay teams had arrived by then.

Regional troops secured the approaches.

Medics were already sorting wounded.

The command archive remained intact.

Yurushi looked at it.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Objective held.”

[[speaker:tae]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Haru nearly got it.”

[[speaker:tae]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Hana could have.”

[[speaker:tae]]“Yes.”

Tae felt Yurushi tense slightly at her name.

He ignored that too.

[[speaker:yurushi]]After a while Yurushi asked, “Why didn’t you chase?”

Tae stopped.

Looked at him.

[[speaker:tae]]“Because you were here.”

Yurushi said nothing.

Tae continued walking.

The answer stayed between them. Neither needed to press it further.

At the first field infirmary, the physician took one look at Yurushi and shouted for a trauma table.

Yurushi tried to object.

Tae pushed him onto it.

[[speaker:tae]]“Take him before I change my mind.”

The physician blinked.

“Change your mind about treatment?”

[[speaker:tae]]“About carrying him.”

Yurushi, half-conscious, muttered:

“Liar.”

Tae looked down.

[[speaker:tae]]“What?”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“You came.”

Then Yurushi passed out.

Tae stood very still.

The physician started cutting away blood-soaked cloth.

“Outside.”

Tae did not move.

“Lord Tae.”

[[speaker:tae]]“I’m not a lord.”

“Outside.”

He went.

Hours later, the first full report reached Qin and Lu.

**Central node held.**

**Yurushi critical but alive.**

**Tae injured, functional.**

**Haru used repeated 3× Redline — three probable same-day doses.**

**Hana extracted him.**

Qin read the line twice.

Lu read it once.

Then sat down.

[[speaker:luweiran]]“Three.”

[[speaker:qin]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:luweiran]]“How much life?”

Qin shook his head.

[[speaker:qin]]“Luo would know better.”

Lu looked at the map.

The attack had failed.

That did not make it a small event.

Shinsei now knew Wuyue’s hidden apex existed.

Wuyue now knew Shinsei would spend Paragon lives and future years trying to solve that fact.

Qin folded the report.

[[speaker:qin]]“Send Sera everything.”

Lu nodded.

[[speaker:luweiran]]“Also Aya?”

Qin paused.

Not their physician.

Enemy command.

Still a physician.

[[speaker:qin]]“Through the medical exchange channel,” Qin said.

Lu nodded.

That decision would matter later.

For now, the report moved north.

And somewhere in Shinrin, Hana Arakawa carried a dying strategist toward the physician who had warned him for years what medicine became when people stopped respecting why it existed.
