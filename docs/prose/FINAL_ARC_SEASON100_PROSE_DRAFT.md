## Chapter 351 — Too Full

Rhen knew the problem had become unsafe when sleep stopped helping.

Until then, he had treated the whole thing with the dignity appropriate to an inconvenience.

Very little.

A frozen cup could be thawed.

A frosted wall could be repaired.

A teapot encased in ice because he touched the table beside it was mostly Sera’s opportunity to insult him.

That had been manageable.

Then he woke before dawn with every lamp in the private rooms rimmed in white frost.

Not broken.

Not shattered.

Just touched by qi he had never meant to release.

Sera was asleep beside him.

Warm.

Untouched.

Rhen checked that first.

Then checked again because some instincts did not care how strong a person was.

Her breathing remained even.

Frozen Petals Garden lay around the core exactly as intended, its passive circulation steady and harmless.

The problem was not the Garden.

The problem was him.

Rhen sat at the edge of the bed.

He stopped suppressing himself for one breath.

The room changed.

Not violently.

Pressure entered the air the way deep winter entered a valley—without needing permission from any one object.

Frost crawled beneath the window frame.

The wash basin filmed over.

A white-blue petal formed near the ceiling.

Then another.

Rhen immediately compressed the aura back inward.

Everything stopped.

He stared at his hands.

The qi was not unstable.

That was the annoying part.

It was perfectly obedient.

There was simply too much of it occupying too much internal volume.

His old circulation architecture had been built for a reserve that could be vast because it remained diffuse.

Now Frozen Petals Garden restored what he spent faster than the old internal structure could comfortably hold.

The reserve returned.

Then kept returning.

Every day he forced more of it into the same body.

The answer was obvious.

He had been avoiding it because obvious answers could still be inconvenient.

Sera opened one eye.

[[speaker:sera]]“You are thinking loudly.”

Rhen looked back.

[[speaker:rhen]]“I did not know that was possible.”

[[speaker:sera]]“It is when you sit at the edge of the bed before sunrise looking like you want to argue with your organs.”

He almost smiled.

Sera sat up.

Then saw the frost beneath the window.

The smile left her face.

[[speaker:sera]]“How long?”

[[speaker:rhen]]“Since it became this bad?”

[[speaker:sera]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“This morning.”

[[speaker:sera]]“Truth.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“That is the truth.”

She watched him.

Rhen had survived armies, Sovereigns, Paragons and enough dramatic martial artists to develop a strong preference for tea-shop problems.

Sera remained the person most capable of making him feel caught with one look.

He sighed.

[[speaker:rhen]]“The pressure has been worsening for months.”

[[speaker:sera]]“I know.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“I thought I could keep adapting the suppression.”

[[speaker:sera]]“I know.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“The suppression is now part of every waking breath.”

That was new.

Sera’s expression hardened.

Rhen continued before she could ask.

“Not exhausting. Not yet. But continuous.”

[[speaker:sera]]“And sleep?”

[[speaker:rhen]]“This morning says sleep is no longer enough.”

Sera looked at the frosted window.

Then back at him.

[[speaker:sera]]“Unsafe.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“Yes.”

No argument.

That worried her more than an argument would have.

[[speaker:sera]]“What is the fix?”

Rhen rested one hand over his sternum.

[[speaker:rhen]]“Condensation.”

[[speaker:sera]]“You said that before.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“I was hoping not to need the full version.”

[[speaker:sera]]“What is the full version?”

[[speaker:rhen]]“The reserve has to take less space.”

Sera frowned slightly.

[[speaker:sera]]“Same qi?”

[[speaker:rhen]]“Eventually more.”

[[speaker:sera]]“That sounded suspiciously like more power.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“It is not the purpose.”

[[speaker:sera]]“Explain it without being annoying.”

Rhen considered.

[[speaker:rhen]]“Imagine packing snow.”

Sera stared.

[[speaker:sera]]“That is your explanation?”

[[speaker:rhen]]“You asked for less annoying.”

[[speaker:sera]]“I did.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“Loose snow fills a courtyard quickly. Packed snow occupies less volume without becoming less snow.”

Sera’s eyes narrowed.

[[speaker:sera]]“So your current qi is loose snow.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“Extremely dangerous loose snow.”

[[speaker:sera]]“And you need to compress it until your body can hold it without leaking winter into the furniture.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:sera]]“That was almost understandable.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“Thank you.”

[[speaker:sera]]“How long?”

Rhen looked toward the window.

[[speaker:rhen]]“Months, possibly.”

Silence.

Sera’s gaze moved to him again.

[[speaker:sera]]“How many?”

[[speaker:rhen]]“I do not know.”

That answer stayed between them.

Outside, Quaint was beginning to wake.

A cart rattled in the rear courtyard.

Someone in the kitchen dropped a metal bowl.

Tae shouted something about breakfast that sounded like a threat.

Ordinary life.

Rhen listened.

Sera said, “You are going to close the door.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:sera]]“Today?”

[[speaker:rhen]]“No.”

[[speaker:sera]]“When?”

[[speaker:rhen]]“Soon.”

She did not tell him not to.

That was why he loved her.

Sera had spent too much of her life being protected without consent to confuse love with refusing another person the right to make a necessary choice.

Instead she asked, “Can the Garden keep running while you are inside?”

[[speaker:rhen]]“Yes. The long-term passive is already established. It does not require me to consciously maintain every cycle.”

[[speaker:sera]]“Can Lu manage the clinic?”

[[speaker:rhen]]“With Luo and the apprentices.”

[[speaker:sera]]“Can I run Quaint?”

Rhen looked at her.

Sera’s eyebrow rose.

[[speaker:sera]]“Wrong answer and I freeze you myself.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“You can run everything.”

[[speaker:sera]]“Better.”

He took her hand.

Warm.

The frost in the room had already vanished.

Rhen said, “I am sorry.”

[[speaker:sera]]“For needing medical treatment?”

[[speaker:rhen]]“For timing.”

[[speaker:sera]]“What timing?”

He glanced toward the outer rooms.

Yun had been gone roughly three months.

Luo was barely pretending not to count every day.

Lu’s foreign route tracing had grown into a wall of ledgers.

Nobody knew enough to call it war.

Nobody knew enough to call it anything else.

Rhen said, “It feels like a bad time to disappear.”

Sera squeezed his hand.

[[speaker:sera]]“There is never a good time for someone useful to become unavailable.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“That is not comforting.”

[[speaker:sera]]“It is not supposed to be.”

She leaned closer.

[[speaker:sera]]“You told me when it became unsafe.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:sera]]“That was the deal.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:sera]]“Then keep the rest of it.”

Rhen nodded.

For the first time in months, the decision itself made him feel lighter.

Not less full.

That part would take longer.

## Chapter 352 — Three Weeks Became Three Months

Luo stopped asking Lu whether there was news.

That was how everyone knew he was worried.

For the first month, he asked every morning.

For the second, every evening too.

By the third, he simply appeared whenever Lu opened a new foreign ledger.

No question.

No need.

Three weeks had become three months.

The clinic table still had Yun’s cup in the back corner.

Nobody moved it.

That was not a memorial.

Luo would have objected if anyone called it one.

It was a cup belonging to someone who had not returned yet.

That distinction held the room together.

Lu Weiran entered near noon carrying a narrow export registry.

Xie Wuchen followed him through the door and immediately looked as though being indoors had been imposed on him unfairly.

Luo glanced up.

[[speaker:luo]]“News?”

So much for not asking.

Lu placed the registry on the table.

[[speaker:luweiran]]“Not enough.”

Luo’s face changed by almost nothing.

[[speaker:luo]]“Tell me anyway.”

Xie leaned against the wall.

[[speaker:xie_wuchen]]“This came through a merchant office two ports east.”

Luo looked at him.

[[speaker:luo]]“I thought you were done helping.”

[[speaker:xie_wuchen]]“I was.”

[[speaker:luo]]“Then?”

[[speaker:xie_wuchen]]“The courier was extorting refugees.”

Lu said, “He stopped the extortion, found the ledger and brought it here.”

Xie looked offended.

[[speaker:xie_wuchen]]“That makes it sound deliberate.”

[[speaker:luweiran]]“It was deliberate.”

[[speaker:xie_wuchen]]“Temporarily deliberate.”

Nobody argued.

Xie waited.

Then frowned.

[[speaker:xie_wuchen]]“You are supposed to tell me I keep coming back.”

Sera entered behind him carrying tea.

[[speaker:sera]]“We got bored.”

Xie looked more offended.

Good.

The room almost became normal for three seconds.

Then Lu opened the registry.

A chain of Shinrin export references ran down one side.

Medical tools.

Dried herbs.

Ceramic containers.

Routine cargo.

One merchant credential had been used repeatedly in districts far deeper inland than Yun’s original pharmaceutical trail should have required.

Lu tapped it.

[[speaker:luweiran]]“This credential crossed through three interior medical zones over eleven weeks.”

Luo stared.

[[speaker:luo]]“You think it was hers.”

[[speaker:luweiran]]“I think it matches one alias fragment from the first route she used.”

[[speaker:luo]]“Think.”

[[speaker:luweiran]]“Yes.”

Not certainty.

Lu refused to manufacture certainty for grief.

Luo respected him for that.

Hated him slightly too.

[[speaker:luo]]“Where does it end?”

Lu turned the page.

[[speaker:luweiran]]“Here.”

A transfer station near the northern administrative corridor.

Then nothing.

Luo’s jaw tightened.

[[speaker:luo]]“Coordinate?”

[[speaker:luweiran]]“No.”

[[speaker:luo]]“Local contact?”

[[speaker:luweiran]]“No.”

[[speaker:luo]]“Prison?”

[[speaker:luweiran]]“No.”

[[speaker:luo]]“Anything useful?”

[[speaker:luweiran]]“It tells us she went much deeper than she expected.”

Luo looked at him.

[[speaker:luo]]“That is not useful.”

[[speaker:luweiran]]“It is operationally useful.”

[[speaker:luo]]“It does not tell me where she is.”

[[speaker:luweiran]]“No.”

Lu did not soften the answer.

That was mercy in its own way.

Sera placed a cup beside Luo.

He did not touch it.

Xie pushed away from the wall.

[[speaker:xie_wuchen]]“I can go north.”

Everyone looked at him.

He immediately regretted speaking.

Sera said, “To where?”

Xie gestured at the ledger.

[[speaker:xie_wuchen]]“Shinrin.”

[[speaker:sera]]“Which city?”

Silence.

[[speaker:sera]]“Which road?”

Silence.

[[speaker:sera]]“Which building?”

Xie’s mouth tightened.

Sera continued.

[[speaker:sera]]“We know she crossed a continent-sized state under an alias. We know a medical credential touched multiple interior districts. We do not know whether she is still on any of those routes.”

Xie looked at Luo.

[[speaker:xie_wuchen]]“I could search.”

Luo finally spoke.

[[speaker:luo]]“And become a second missing person.”

Xie said nothing.

Luo’s voice was tired.

[[speaker:luo]]“Yun went because one person was hard to notice. If we send random strong people after her without a location, we turn the country into a net around everyone.”

That was exactly why she had left him behind.

He understood it.

Understanding did not make it kinder.

Lu pointed to another mark.

[[speaker:luweiran]]“There is one more thing.”

A northern routing code.

Not enough to reveal a destination.

Enough to indicate Yun had started moving toward Isgard-linked trade corridors before the record disappeared.

Luo stared at it.

[[speaker:luo]]“Home?”

[[speaker:luweiran]]“Possibly.”

[[speaker:luo]]“Captured?”

[[speaker:luweiran]]“Possibly.”

[[speaker:luo]]“Dead?”

Lu was quiet.

[[speaker:luweiran]]“Possible.”

Sera’s eyes closed briefly.

Luo looked at Yun’s cup.

Then finally picked up the tea Sera brought him.

It had already cooled.

He drank anyway.

Xie moved toward the door.

[[speaker:xie_wuchen]]“I am done after this.”

Nobody answered.

He stopped.

Looked back.

Still nothing.

[[speaker:xie_wuchen]]“You are all irritating.”

Sera said, “See you tomorrow.”

Xie left.

Lu watched the door close.

[[speaker:luweiran]]“He will be back.”

Luo said, “Probably.”

For the first time, there was no humor in it.

Three weeks had become three months.

And somewhere beyond every ledger they possessed, Yun had become a line that stopped before reaching home.

## Chapter 353 — Before the Door Closes

Sera locked the bedroom door.

Rhen looked at it.

[[speaker:rhen]]“That felt dramatic.”

[[speaker:sera]]“It was intentional.”

He was sitting on the edge of the bed with his traveling coat folded beside him.

Not wearing it.

That was strange enough that Sera disliked the sight immediately.

Rhen without the dark green coat usually meant sleep, bathing or injury.

Tonight it meant absence.

He entered the stone chamber tomorrow.

They both knew it.

Neither had said much about it during the day because Quaint contained one thousand people and apparently all one thousand could detect private emotion through walls when given sufficient opportunity.

Tae had offered to “guard the chamber by punching anyone suspicious.”

Huo had told him the chamber was underground and punching the mountain was unnecessary.

Tae had called that defeatist.

Lu had prepared medical redundancies.

Qin had reviewed command lines with Sera.

Luo had barely noticed anything beyond Yun’s silence.

Now the door was locked.

Finally.

Then the bedroom window refused to close.

Rhen stared at it.

Sera watched from beside the bed.

[[speaker:sera]]“You're losing.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“I'm assessing it.”

[[speaker:sera]]“The window?”

[[speaker:rhen]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:sera]]“It has two hinges.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“One is misaligned.”

[[speaker:sera]]“You once stopped a mountain from falling on a village.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“That mountain was structurally simpler.”

Sera covered her mouth.

Rhen looked over.

[[speaker:rhen]]“Are you laughing?”

[[speaker:sera]]“No.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“You are.”

[[speaker:sera]]“Petals Monarch defeated by carpentry.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“Windows are not carpentry.”

[[speaker:sera]]“What are they?”

Rhen looked back at the frame.

[[speaker:rhen]]“Currently hostile.”

Sera lost the fight and laughed into her hand.

Rhen gave the window one final, deeply offended look.

Then he shut it with two fingers and an amount of precision qi that could probably have severed a fortress gate without disturbing the wall around it.

Sera stared.

[[speaker:sera]]“You cheated.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“It is closed.”

[[speaker:sera]]“You used cultivation on a window.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“It was hostile.”

Rhen returned to the edge of the bed.

For a few breaths, tomorrow was not a medical danger or the beginning of months apart.

It was simply tomorrow.

And tonight, Rhen had lost to a window.

Sera crossed the room.

Rhen held out one hand.

She ignored it and sat directly across his lap.

He looked up.

[[speaker:rhen]]“Efficient.”

[[speaker:sera]]“You are disappearing for months.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“Possibly.”

[[speaker:sera]]“Do not make ‘possibly’ sound optimistic.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“I was trying.”

[[speaker:sera]]“Poorly.”

She kissed him.

Not gently at first.

There were weeks for gentleness later.

Months, apparently.

Tonight Sera wanted the physical certainty of him still being here.

Warm hands.

Familiar breathing.

The faint scar through his eyebrow.

The man whose absurd power had spent years disguising itself as an excuse to refill tea.

Rhen’s arms settled around her.

He did not turn the moment into a joke.

That was how she knew he understood.

When they finally separated, Sera rested her forehead against his.

[[speaker:sera]]“Explain the dangerous part again.”

Rhen sighed.

[[speaker:rhen]]“Romantic.”

[[speaker:sera]]“I am serious.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“I know.”

She shifted but did not move off him.

[[speaker:sera]]“Tomorrow you begin condensation. What happens if something goes wrong outside?”

[[speaker:rhen]]“For the first stage, I can still stop.”

[[speaker:sera]]“How long is first stage?”

[[speaker:rhen]]“Hours. Possibly a day.”

[[speaker:sera]]“And after?”

Rhen’s hands became still at her waist.

[[speaker:rhen]]“After the structure starts collapsing inward, interruption becomes dangerous.”

[[speaker:sera]]“Define dangerous.”

He looked at her.

Sera did not blink.

[[speaker:sera]]“Rhen.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“If the qi is halfway between diffuse and condensed states, forcing normal circulation too quickly can tear the meridians it is moving through.”

[[speaker:sera]]“How badly?”

[[speaker:rhen]]“Catastrophically.”

Her jaw tightened.

[[speaker:sera]]“Body?”

[[speaker:rhen]]“Also at risk.”

[[speaker:sera]]“Death?”

A pause.

[[speaker:rhen]]“Yes.”

There.

Plain.

No mythology.

No assumption that being Rhen made physiology optional.

Sera looked down.

His pulse beneath her hand was steady.

[[speaker:sera]]“You cannot just wake up if I knock.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“Not once I am deep enough.”

[[speaker:sera]]“What if Wuyue needs you?”

[[speaker:rhen]]“You decide whether Wuyue needs a dead Rhen more than a temporarily unavailable one.”

She glared.

[[speaker:sera]]“That was a terrible sentence.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“It was accurate.”

[[speaker:sera]]“What if I need you?”

That one changed him.

Rhen’s expression softened.

[[speaker:rhen]]“You wait.”

Sera swallowed.

He continued quietly.

[[speaker:rhen]]“And I come back.”

[[speaker:sera]]“You cannot promise the second part.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“I can promise I intend it.”

[[speaker:sera]]“That is annoyingly precise.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“You like me precise.”

[[speaker:sera]]“Sometimes.”

She kissed him again.

Slower.

The rest of the night belonged to them without needing an audience.

Warmth.

Teasing.

Hands familiar enough that nothing had to be proven.

Adult intimacy without spectacle.

At some point Rhen’s folded coat ended up on the floor.

At some point Sera accused him of doing that deliberately so she would have to pick it up tomorrow.

He denied it badly.

Later, they lay beneath the blanket while rain touched the roof.

Sera traced one finger along the pale scar near his eyebrow.

[[speaker:sera]]“Months.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“Probably.”

[[speaker:sera]]“You keep using that word.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“It remains accurate.”

[[speaker:sera]]“When you come out, I am charging you rent.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“For the chamber?”

[[speaker:sera]]“For emotional inconvenience.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“That sounds expensive.”

[[speaker:sera]]“It will be.”

Rhen smiled.

Then Sera’s expression changed.

[[speaker:sera]]“Yun.”

He understood immediately.

[[speaker:rhen]]“She is still out there.”

[[speaker:sera]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:sera]]“If news comes after you begin—”

Rhen was quiet.

Sera hated the answer before he gave it.

[[speaker:rhen]]“If I am still in the early stage, Lu can assess me.”

[[speaker:sera]]“And after?”

[[speaker:rhen]]“You already know.”

She did.

That was the cruelty.

They were making a medically necessary decision with incomplete information.

Not choosing Yun over Rhen.

Not choosing Rhen over Yun.

Choosing based on the world they actually knew tonight.

Sera rested her head against his chest.

[[speaker:sera]]“Bad timing.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:sera]]“You said that.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“I remain correct.”

She pinched him.

He pretended it hurt.

Eventually they slept.

For the last time before the door closed.

Neither knew that Yun Shizhen was already on the final road toward Isgard.

Neither knew she would arrive one day after Rhen entered the chamber.

## Chapter 354 — Closed Garden

The chamber was smaller than Sera expected.

That annoyed her.

For something about to swallow Rhen for months, it should at least have had the decency to be impressive.

Instead it was stone.

A low platform.

A narrow lamp niche cut into one wall.

A water basin.

Medical shelves.

Reinforced ventilation channels.

Nothing ceremonial.

Rhen preferred it that way.

His folded dark green traveling coat rested beside the platform.

Sera had picked it up from the bedroom floor that morning while complaining exactly as promised.

Now she looked at it and wished she had complained longer.

Lu checked the chamber one final time.

[[speaker:luweiran]]“Emergency access stays sealed unless the external monitor shows catastrophic deviation.”

Rhen nodded.

“Once condensation passes the early stage,” Lu continued, “nobody opens this because they are nervous.”

Tae stood behind him.

[[speaker:tae]]“What if I am very nervous?”

[[speaker:luweiran]]“No.”

[[speaker:tae]]“What if—”

[[speaker:luweiran]]“No.”

Huo folded his arms.

[[speaker:huo]]“This is why nobody lets you manage medicine.”

[[speaker:tae]]“I manage morale.”

[[speaker:huo]]“You damage morale.”

Qin Luo looked toward Sera.

She was not smiling.

Tae noticed and, with rare survival instinct, stopped talking.

The core had come to see Rhen in.

Not all one thousand.

That would have been ridiculous.

Sera.

Qin.

Tae.

Huo.

Lu.

Luo remained at the clinic because one missing person already occupied too much of his world.

Nobody resented him for it.

Rhen stood at the chamber entrance.

No aura.

No frost.

Perfectly ordinary because he was still forcing ordinary into place.

Sera hated that she could now see the effort.

Qin spoke first.

“Come back denser.”

Rhen looked at him.

[[speaker:rhen]]“That sounds rude.”

[[speaker:qin]]“It is medical encouragement.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“Luo has been influencing you.”

[[speaker:qin]]“Probably.”

Huo stepped forward.

[[speaker:huo]]“If something happens, we hold Quaint.”

Rhen nodded.

[[speaker:rhen]]“I know.”

Tae said, “If something happens, we punch it.”

Huo closed his eyes.

Rhen smiled.

[[speaker:rhen]]“I also know.”

Lu handed him a small monitoring strip.

[[speaker:luweiran]]“First stage, I can still call you out if the circulation pattern fails.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“And after?”

[[speaker:luweiran]]“After, I guard the door from idiots.”

Tae looked offended.

[[speaker:tae]]“Why did you look at me?”

Lu did not answer.

Finally, everyone except Sera stepped back.

The corridor suddenly felt longer.

Rhen looked at her.

Sera said, “You owe me rent.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“I remember.”

[[speaker:sera]]“And tea.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:sera]]“And several months.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“That may be difficult to return literally.”

[[speaker:sera]]“Do not become philosophical now.”

[[speaker:rhen]]“Sorry.”

She stepped close.

No dramatic speech.

They had done the real talking last night.

Sera adjusted the collar of his plain inner robe because his coat would remain outside the circulation field.

Then she kissed him once.

[[speaker:sera]]“Come back.”

Rhen touched her cheek.

[[speaker:rhen]]“I intend to.”

Still precise.

Still him.

Sera left the chamber.

Rhen sat on the low platform.

His traveling coat remained folded at his side.

Lu closed the stone door.

Locks engaged one by one.

Not prison locks.

Protection.

The distinction did not make the sound kinder.

Inside, Rhen placed both hands on his knees.

He stopped suppressing.

The chamber filled with winter.

No explosion.

No violent surge.

Qi unfolded through every meridian until his body felt too full even for him.

Then he began the opposite process.

Not gathering more.

Condensing what was already there.

White-blue petals formed around the platform.

One.

Ten.

Hundreds.

Each carried a fraction of the diffuse reserve as he folded circulation inward, layer by layer.

The work was delicate enough that strength offered almost no shortcut.

Too fast and the meridians tore.

Too slow and the reserve kept refilling around the process.

Rhen closed his eyes.

Outside, Sera stood with one hand against the stone door until Lu quietly told her the first-stage readings were stable.

Only then did she move.

Quaint returned to work.

Tea was served.

Ledgers opened.

Patrols changed.

Luo waited for news that had still not come.

Rhen’s seclusion had nothing to do with Axtaya.

Nothing to do with Shinrin.

Nothing to do with Yun.

It was a medical necessity created by his own evolving qi.

The timing was only coincidence.

A cruel one.

Exactly one day later, Yun Shizhen reached Isgard.
