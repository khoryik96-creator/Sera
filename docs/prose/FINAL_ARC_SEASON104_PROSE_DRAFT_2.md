## Chapter 395 — Qin Opens the Map

Qin Luo did not spend the next hour celebrating.

He spent it separating three High Paragons.

Tae objected first.

[[speaker:tae]]“Absolutely not.”

Qin stood over the continental map.

[[speaker:qin]]“You are going west.”

[[speaker:tae]]“No.”

[[speaker:qin]]“Huo goes southeast.”

Huo pointed at himself.

[[speaker:huo]]“Why southeast?”

[[speaker:qin]]“Because you are large.”

Huo frowned.

[[speaker:huo]]“That is not strategy.”

[[speaker:qin]]“It was not the reason.”

Tae folded his arms.

[[speaker:tae]]“We were just attacked here.”

[[speaker:qin]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:tae]]“So we stay here.”

[[speaker:qin]]“No.”

Lu Weiran entered with three new reports and placed them in order without speaking.

Qin touched the map.

[[speaker:qin]]“Shinsei now knows this building contains three High Paragons.”

Tae’s expression hardened.

[[speaker:tae]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:qin]]“If we all remain here, what have they learned?”

[[speaker:tae]]“That attacking here is stupid.”

[[speaker:qin]]“Correct.”

[[speaker:tae]]“So?”

Qin moved two markers.

[[speaker:qin]]“They stop attacking here.”

Silence.

Huo leaned closer.

[[speaker:huo]]“They hit somewhere else.”

[[speaker:qin]]“Yes.”

Lu opened the first report.

[[speaker:luweiran]]“Stonecrown’s fixed defence remains heavy, but Kael personally is north. Western command-road chain has two exposed logistics nodes. Central medical distribution is stronger than expected but still concentrated.”

Second.

[[speaker:luweiran]]“White Vein region has adequate Sovereign response. Imperial interior is numerically strongest.”

Third.

[[speaker:luweiran]]“Three smaller areas depend on public commanders who are currently in Isgard.”

Tae looked at the map.

The argument left his face.

Qin continued.

“Three High Paragons around one teahouse makes this building impossible to raid.”

His fingers moved apart.

[[speaker:qin]]“Three High Paragons distributed correctly makes Wuyue difficult to raid.”

Tae sighed.

[[speaker:tae]]“Fine.”

Huo looked at Qin.

[[speaker:huo]]“What about you?”

[[speaker:qin]]“I stay central.”

[[speaker:huo]]“That sounds convenient.”

[[speaker:qin]]“I am operational deputy.”

[[speaker:huo]]“That sounds like paperwork.”

[[speaker:qin]]“It is.”

Huo looked horrified.

Qin almost smiled.

Then the door opened.

Yurushi entered.

He had changed clothes after the fight.

No visible wounds because he had not been in it.

That bothered him more than he wanted to admit.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“What happened?”

Tae looked at him.

[[speaker:tae]]“You missed two Paragons.”

Yurushi stared.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Excuse me?”

[[speaker:tae]]“Haru and Hana.”

Everything in Yurushi’s face changed.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Hana.”

[[speaker:tae]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Here.”

[[speaker:tae]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Alive?”

Tae blinked.

[[speaker:tae]]“Obviously.”

Yurushi looked at Qin.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“What did she do?”

Qin answered the question he meant.

“She withdrew when the mission failed.”

Yurushi exhaled.

Small.

Controlled.

Then looked at the damage ledger.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Civilians?”

“Safe.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Patients?”

“Safe.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Quaint?”

“Eleven significant injuries. No deaths.”

Yurushi nodded.

Good.

Then he saw the map.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“You’re redistributing.”

[[speaker:qin]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Because Shinsei will stop probing the house and hit support elsewhere.”

Qin turned toward him.

[[speaker:qin]]“Where?”

Yurushi understood immediately what he was being asked.

Not memory.

Judgement.

He walked to the table.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Not Stonecrown itself.”

[[speaker:tae]]“Why?” Tae asked.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Too much fixed weight. Even without Kael, thirty-eight thousand plus fortress systems. Haru does not enjoy problems that cannot be narrowed.”

Huo looked at him.

[[speaker:huo]]“You know him?”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Enough.”

Yurushi pointed at a central logistics line.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“This.”

Lu leaned forward.

[[speaker:luweiran]]“Why?”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Because damaging it threatens Kael’s regional support without requiring Shinsei to defeat the region.”

He moved one finger.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Road command. Medical relay. Replacement equipment. If this node fails, Isgard learns about it through slower supply within days. Kael hears. Political pressure begins.”

Qin nodded.

[[speaker:qin]]“Hana?”

Yurushi’s hand stopped.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“She would choose the route with the best civilian evacuation geometry.”

Tae looked at him.

[[speaker:tae]]“That specific?”

Yurushi did not look up.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“We were together four years.”

Nobody said anything.

Huo slowly backed away from the table.

Tae stared at the map very intensely.

Lu, because he was Lu, simply wrote the information down.

Yurushi noticed.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Do not put that in the military record.”

[[speaker:luweiran]]“I wrote route preference.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Good.”

[[speaker:qin]]Qin said, “You’re staying central with me.”

Yurushi looked at him.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“I thought Tae was going west.”

[[speaker:qin]]“He is.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“And Huo southeast.”

[[speaker:qin]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Then why am I central?”

[[speaker:qin]]“Because you understand Shinsei targeting logic and I need you here until we know which prediction is real.”

That was Sera’s original reason for leaving him home.

Now it became work.

Yurushi nodded.

Tae picked up his travel coat.

[[speaker:tae]]“West, then.”

Huo looked toward the kitchen.

[[speaker:huo]]“Can I eat first?”

[[speaker:qin]]Qin said, “On the road.”

Huo looked betrayed.

[[speaker:huo]]“You’ve changed.”

[[speaker:qin]]“You met me after I went blind.”

[[speaker:huo]]“That is not what I meant.”

Tae walked out.

Huo followed, carrying enough food for several normal people.

Qin remained over the map.

Lu sat beside the relay board.

Yurushi took the opposite side.

Three hidden High Paragons stopped being a secret concentrated beneath one roof.

They became Wuyue’s unseen emergency line.

Far away, Haru and Hana were already moving toward the exact region Yurushi had pointed at.

## Chapter 396 — The Central Region

Yurushi recognized the attack before the messenger finished speaking.

“Central road-command node,” the runner said. “Pressure spike. Two Paragon signatures.”

Qin turned toward him.

Yurushi was already standing.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Haru and Hana.”

[[speaker:qin]]“Likely.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Distance?”

Lu answered.

“From here? Too far for ordinary response before they finish a limited strike.”

Yurushi looked at the map.

Tae was west.

Huo southeast.

Qin central but too important to leave immediately without reassigning the entire response network.

Regional Sovereigns were moving.

They would die against Haru and Hana if they arrived first and tried to contest directly.

Yurushi took his coat.

[[speaker:qin]]Qin said, “No.”

Yurushi looked at him.

[[speaker:qin]]“You cannot reach them fast enough without leaving this command blind.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“I can reorganize.”

[[speaker:qin]]“That takes time.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“So does dying.”

Yurushi’s mouth moved.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Comforting.”

Qin tilted his head.

[[speaker:qin]]“You’re one High Paragon against one High and one Established.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:qin]]“You are not ordered to win.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“I know.”

[[speaker:qin]]“Delay.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“I know.”

[[speaker:qin]]“Protect the node.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“I know.”

[[speaker:qin]]“Do not chase them.”

Yurushi stared.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“You repeat yourself like Sera.”

[[speaker:qin]]“She trained me.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“That explains many things.”

Lu handed Yurushi a narrow packet.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“What?”

[[speaker:luweiran]]“Updated road map. Stonecrown engineers changed two culverts last year.”

Yurushi took it.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“You had this ready?”

[[speaker:luweiran]]“I suspected your prediction might be correct.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“You are unsettling.”

[[speaker:luweiran]]“Yes.”

Then Yurushi left.

He did not teleport.

Light Qi gathered through his legs.

**Radiant Step.**

Acceleration became physical violence applied to distance.

He crossed the first ridge faster than most Sovereigns could process.

Not because space folded.

Because his body moved that fast.

Light burned along his path.

Residual afterimages lingered behind him for fractions of a second.

He used roads where they were faster.

Walls where they shortened turns.

Tree shadows where concealment mattered.

The central region rose ahead.

Smoke.

Good.

Smoke meant structures damaged, not necessarily destroyed.

He arrived at the outer medical relay while two regional commanders were trying to organize evacuation.

Both froze when they saw him.

“Black Radiance?”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Later.”

Yurushi looked at the building.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Where?”

One pointed.

“Road command.”

Yurushi moved.

Hana saw him first.

Of course she did.

Wind shifted around one broken arch.

Her head turned.

For a second neither moved.

Four years existed between them.

Tea in cheap rooms.

Arguments on rooftops.

Her stealing half his blankets and denying it.

Him leaving for covert operations without saying where.

Her furious because staying inside Shinsei meant protecting people from decisions he considered proof they should both leave.

None of that disappeared because they stood on opposite sides now.

Hana’s face changed.

[[speaker:hana]]“Yurushi.”

Haru looked toward him.

Recognition came slower.

Then faster.

[[speaker:haru]]“Black Radiance.”

Yurushi stopped between them and the command entrance.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Hana.”

She looked at the small black token beneath his coat.

Saw the flower.

Six petals.

Her eyes narrowed.

[[speaker:hana]]“You joined them.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Temporarily.”

Hana stared.

Despite the war, despite everything, one corner of her mouth almost moved.

[[speaker:hana]]“You’re unbelievable.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“That is what I said.”

Haru’s spear lowered.

[[speaker:haru]]“This node supports Kael’s regional command.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:haru]]“We are taking it out.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“No.”

Haru looked at him.

Simple.

No philosophy.

Yurushi did not need one.

Behind him, medics were evacuating patients.

Road clerks were burning sensitive codes.

Engineers were moving critical bridge schematics.

People.

Work.

Enough.

Hana’s gaze moved behind Yurushi.

He knew what she saw.

Civilian routes.

Narrow exits.

The left-side alley that would clog first.

The roofline safest for noncombatants.

She always noticed those things.

Haru noticed her noticing.

“Five minutes,” he said.

Hana looked at him.

[[speaker:hana]]“For?”

[[speaker:haru]]“Complete the objective or withdraw.”

Good.

Still Haru.

Measured.

Yurushi breathed out.

Darkness Qi spread across the broken courtyard.

Not a portal.

Not another world.

Ambient light vanished where Black Veil swallowed and scattered it.

Shadows connected physically through walls, eaves, rubble and narrow passages.

Light remained in open patches.

Dark.

Bright.

Border.

**Twilight Dominion** opened.

Hana’s expression became sad.

Not afraid.

Sad.

[[speaker:hana]]“You finally showed me the whole thing.”

Yurushi looked at her.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“You never asked while trying to stab me before.”

[[speaker:hana]]“That happened twice.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Three times.”

[[speaker:hana]]“One was training.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“You used a real sword.”

Haru looked between them.

[[speaker:haru]]“This is not useful.”

Hana sighed.

[[speaker:hana]]“You see why we broke up.”

Yurushi almost laughed.

Then the fight began.

## Chapter 397 — Twilight Dominion

Haru lost Yurushi in darkness.

Not magically.

That distinction mattered.

He could still hear.

Still feel qi.

Still strike the shadowed corridor if he wanted.

But Black Veil swallowed the visual information that made path prediction easy.

Yurushi moved through connected shadow like a man who had spent decades learning where human eyes failed first.

Haru stabbed into the dark.

The spear passed through empty air.

A stored Carved Spear line remained.

Useful.

Yurushi emerged into light behind him.

Radiant Step converted concealment into acceleration.

Real speed.

Haru turned.

Too slow.

**Piercing Dawn.**

Two fingers struck his shoulder guard.

Concentrated Light Qi penetrated at the contact point.

Haru slid sideways.

Hana arrived before Yurushi could follow.

Twin blades crossed.

Yurushi’s palm met one.

Darkness Qi concentrated.

**Night Severance.**

Not qi theft.

Not cancellation.

For the instant of contact, Darkness Qi scattered part of Hana’s outward defensive reinforcement.

Her blade line softened.

Yurushi moved past.

Hana rotated with him.

Because she knew.

Not the technique.

Him.

Yurushi preferred exiting an exchange toward civilian-safe geometry.

Always had.

He would choose the line that kept stray force away from the clinic.

Hana cut there before he fully arrived.

The blade opened his sleeve.

Blood followed.

Yurushi looked at her.

She looked back.

[[speaker:hana]]“I know you.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:hana]]“That is inconvenient.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“For both of us.”

Haru attacked again.

Nine Lines Collapse.

Spear lines narrowed the lit corridor.

Yurushi moved into shadow.

Haru triggered a stored thrust through the exit path.

Good.

Yurushi had to alter.

Hana was waiting.

Falling Sky Step dropped her diagonally through a pressure change no ordinary swordsman could preserve speed through.

Yurushi blocked both blades.

Hollow Sky Dominion opened.

Low-pressure pockets formed inside Twilight.

Darkness did not remove air.

Light did not restore it.

Hana’s Domain attacked the physical environment in a way Yurushi had to respect.

Breathing became harder in one shadow corridor.

He abandoned it.

Hana knew he would.

She drove him toward light.

Haru had already placed spear geometry there.

The trap was elegant.

Yurushi smiled despite himself.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“Still good.”

Hana’s jaw tightened.

[[speaker:hana]]“Don’t.”

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“What?”

[[speaker:hana]]“Sound proud of me.”

Yurushi stopped smiling.

Fair.

Haru triggered three stored lines.

Yurushi accelerated.

First missed.

Second clipped his ribs.

Third forced him back into a low-pressure pocket.

Hana collapsed it.

Air slammed inward.

Yurushi lost footing for half a step.

Haru’s spear struck his side.

Clean enough.

Blood darkened his coat.

He vanished into shadow again.

Haru’s eyes narrowed.

[[speaker:haru]]“He’s solving us.”

Hana breathed carefully.

[[speaker:hana]]“Yes.”

Yurushi heard.

They were also solving him.

That was the problem with fighting someone who had shared a bed with you for four years.

Hana knew his breathing.

Knew when his left shoulder tightened before Eclipse Crossing.

Knew he preferred two short Radiant Steps over one maximum burst if civilians were near.

Knew darkness was not a destination for him.

It was a route.

She started attacking the **transitions**.

Excellent.

Terrible.

Yurushi crossed from shadow into light.

Hana’s sword was already there.

He twisted.

Blade cut his thigh.

Haru’s spear followed.

Yurushi blocked with Darkness Qi.

Haru’s High-Paragon output broke through enough to drive him backward.

The wall behind Yurushi cracked.

Pain moved through his ribs.

He assessed.

Objective still functioning.

Evacuation eighty percent.

Road codes already removed.

Medical supplies moving.

He did not need to beat them.

He needed time.

That had always been his talent.

Not winning rooms.

Opening exits.

Yurushi expanded Twilight Dominion through the street network.

Darkness swallowed three alleys.

Light intensified across two roofs.

Haru looked upward.

[[speaker:hana]]“What?” Hana asked.

[[speaker:haru]]“He changed scale.”

[[speaker:hana]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:haru]]“Why?”

Hana understood before he did.

[[speaker:hana]]“Evacuation.”

Yurushi used Radiant Step not toward Haru.

Toward a collapsing cart.

He reached it before the axle gave.

Caught one side.

Redirected three patients into the shadowed alley where Shinsei eyes could not track the route cleanly.

Haru stared.

This was not how enemy High Paragons were supposed to use Domains.

Yurushi returned in light.

Piercing Dawn forced Haru back from the command entrance.

Hana attacked.

He blocked.

Their blades locked for one second.

She whispered:

[[speaker:hana]]“You could have stayed.”

Yurushi’s eyes met hers.

[[speaker:black_radiance]]“So could you have left.”

The old argument.

Still alive.

Neither answered.

They broke contact.

Haru called from behind:

“Time.”

Hana looked toward him.

Five minutes nearly gone.

The objective was not complete.

Yurushi was injured.

Still standing.

Haru’s eyes moved to the sealed red vial at his belt.

Hana saw.

[[speaker:hana]]“No.”

Yurushi saw too.

His expression changed.

Haru looked at the vial.

Then at the command entrance.

Then at Yurushi.

The measured strategist made his second mistake.

He opened the seal.
