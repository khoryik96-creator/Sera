## Chapter 375 — Eldran Stands Where Maedra Would Have Died

Maedra Dravaryn had already been wounded twice before Jun Kajihara found her. Nobody crossed a month of invasion as a fresh diagram.

Her left thigh carried a deep sword cut from an earlier Shinsei push.

Two fingers on her right hand no longer closed fully after a shield collision.

Crimson Road Dominion had been used three times in six days, and every use had taken something from a body already living on too little sleep.

She still led from the front.

Eldran hated that about her.

He also understood it.

[[speaker:eldran]]“Your leg is bleeding again,” he said.

Maedra looked down as if surprised to own one.

[[speaker:maedra]]“Rude.”

[[speaker:eldran]]“That is not a medical assessment.”

[[speaker:maedra]]“It is the assessment I have.”

They stood at the western cut where two Isgard retreat columns were crossing behind a temporary shield line.

The old road had been severed after Halvek's death.

Astrid's hidden route was still being opened farther north.

Until then, this cut was everything.

A scout came running.

“Breach unit.”

Maedra's face hardened.

[[speaker:maedra]]“Jun?”

“Yes.”

Eldran swore.

Maedra lifted her spear.

[[speaker:maedra]]“Move the wounded first.”

[[speaker:eldran]]“They are already moving.”

[[speaker:maedra]]“Then faster.”

[[speaker:eldran]]“That is not how wounded work.”

[[speaker:maedra]]“Today it is.”

Jun's first halberd strike hit the outer barricade two minutes later.

The reinforced timber failed as if reinforcement were a suggestion.

**Breach Dominion.**

Eldran had heard the reports.

Hearing was not understanding.

The second strike hit an iron-faced shield wall.

The shields remained physical objects.

The men behind them remained strong cultivators.

The contact simply refused to be softened by the normal things those men had spent their lives trusting.

Three soldiers went down.

One shield split.

Jun entered the cut.

Maedra met him.

Crimson Road Dominion opened around her.

The first blood came from Jun's forearm.

That was enough.

A thin red qi-path established itself between wound and attacker.

Not a magical road carved into reality.

A martial authority built on blood, pressure and the relationship between injury and pursuit.

Maedra could feel the line.

Use it.

Drive movement and pressure through it.

Jun adjusted instantly.

He stopped giving her clean repeated contact on the same vector.

Halberd changed angle.

Maedra forced him left.

Jun broke a rock barrier instead of contesting her spear.

The opening widened.

Shinsei infantry began entering behind him.

Eldran moved to close it.

For several minutes the fight became too crowded for legend.

Boots.

Blood.

Broken spear shafts.

Men dragging wounded by belts because there was no room for stretchers.

Maedra cut Jun's shoulder.

Jun struck her spear haft.

Breach did not care how well the haft had been reinforced.

It snapped.

Maedra discarded the broken length and drew her side blade.

Eldran saw the problem before she did.

Jun had stopped trying to defeat the line.

He was trying to finish **her**.

Not cruelty.

Command logic.

A wounded Paragon still organizing a retreat was a legitimate military target.

Jun stepped through a gap.

Maedra shifted weight onto the injured leg.

It failed for half a beat.

That was enough.

Jun's halberd came down.

Eldran moved.

He did not think.

Thinking would have been slower.

His shield entered between the blade and Maedra.

In any normal fight, that was the correct answer.

Against Breach Dominion, it was simply where the halberd landed.

The shield did almost nothing.

The impact went through the guard.

Through Eldran's arm.

Through shoulder and upper chest.

Something broke loudly enough that Maedra heard it over the battle.

Eldran hit the ground.

Jun's halberd had not cut him in half.

That would have been cleaner.

Instead the strike crushed the shoulder structure, tore major circulation channels and drove fragments inward.

Eldran remained conscious.

Unfortunately.

Maedra screamed his name.

Jun reset the halberd.

Maedra's broken spear point entered his thigh before he could finish the second strike.

Crimson Road caught the blood.

This time she used the path brutally.

Not to chase.

To throw her own body along the established line and shoulder Jun out of the narrow kill angle.

He staggered two paces.

That saved Eldran.

It also tore Maedra's injured leg worse.

She collapsed beside him.

[[speaker:maedra]]“Idiot,” she said.

Eldran tried to laugh.

It became a wet cough.

[[speaker:eldran]]“You were going to die.”

[[speaker:maedra]]“That was my decision.”

[[speaker:eldran]]“Bad decision.”

[[speaker:maedra]]“Still mine.”

[[speaker:eldran]]“Complain later.”

A Dravaryn extraction squad reached them.

Jun could have pursued.

Instead a Shinsei officer shouted that the second retreat column had cleared the cut.

Then at Maedra and Eldran being dragged away.

The battlefield objective was the road junction.

Not two bodies for a story.

“Take the junction,” he ordered.

Shinsei moved.

Maedra heard the order while medics carried Eldran.

She hated him for being disciplined.

Villains were easier when they wanted blood more than purpose.

Eldran's eyes opened once during transport.

[[speaker:eldran]]“Did they get through?”

Maedra thought he meant Jun.

Then understood.

[[speaker:maedra]]“The column?”

He blinked.

[[speaker:eldran]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:maedra]]“Both.”

His face relaxed.

[[speaker:eldran]]“Good.”

The medics did not tell him what his shoulder looked like.

They did not need to.

He would walk again.

He would command from a table if he wanted.

He would never again circulate enough qi through the ruined shoulder and chest junction to fight on a front line.

Maedra survived too.

Barely.

Within two days she joined Aldric in the protected hospital complex.

Two of Isgard's three Paragons were now out of the war.

Sigrun Veyrhald remained.

Newly ascended.

Already exhausted.

And Shinsei had not stopped moving.

## Chapter 376 — Graven Dominion

Kenji Narukami found Sigrun Veyrhald at the narrowest part of an evacuation road.

She was bleeding from five places.

He assumed that meant the fight was nearly over.

That was his first mistake.

The road ran between a rock face and a winter forest too dense for wagons to leave it.

Behind Sigrun, civilians and wounded soldiers moved north in ugly disciplined lines.

No screaming.

Too tired.

Ahead of her, Shinsei's spearhead kept trying to widen the road into a battlefield.

Sigrun refused.

Shield.

Spear.

Rock wall on one side.

Felled trees on the other.

Every advantage small.

All of them enough to matter.

Kenji stepped forward.

Thunder Qi snapped around his boots.

Sigrun looked at him.

[[speaker:sigrun]]“Raska.”

Kenji's expression changed.

Not much.

[[speaker:kenji]]“He died holding a retreat.”

[[speaker:sigrun]]“I know.”

[[speaker:sigrun]]“You killed him.”

[[speaker:kenji]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:sigrun]]“Good.”

Kenji blinked.

Sigrun lowered her spear.

[[speaker:sigrun]]“Now I know which one to hit.”

She attacked.

Kenji used Flashstep.

The first thrust missed his ribs by an inch.

He cut her upper arm.

Blood opened.

Sigrun did not retreat.

**Graven Dominion.**

The wound changed.

Not healing.

Never healing.

The injured region became reinforced around its damage.

Muscle tightened into structured support.

Qi braced the torn tissue instead of pretending it was whole.

Kenji's next cut struck near the same place.

The contact kicked back through his sword hard enough to sting his wrist.

His eyes widened.

Sigrun smiled without humor.

[[speaker:sigrun]]“Again.”

He did not.

Good.

He was not stupid.

Kenji changed target.

Low cut to thigh.

Sigrun blocked late.

The sword entered shallowly.

Another wound.

Another region became graven.

Her leg should have weakened.

Instead the Dominion structurally braced the damaged joint enough to keep it functional.

Not better.

Not faster.

Still usable.

That distinction became terrifying over time.

Kenji moved around her.

Each meaningful planted step from **Sigrun** called Stormstep's Thunder Qi onto her position.

She took the first strike on the shield.

The second hit the road beside her foot.

The third clipped her shoulder.

Another wound.

Another graven region.

The harder Kenji damaged her, the more of her body became difficult to damage the same way twice.

But the damage still existed.

Blood still left her.

Breathing still became harder.

Pain did not disappear simply because the body had learned to stand around it.

Kenji understood the rule.

[[speaker:kenji]]“Different targets.”

Sigrun heard him say it to himself.

[[speaker:sigrun]]“Very clever.”

[[speaker:kenji]]“You're rude.”

[[speaker:sigrun]]“So was Raska.”

That shut him up.

He attacked with Sevenfold Thunder.

The first burst hit shield.

The second cut hip.

Third struck the opposite arm.

Fourth tried the original upper-arm wound and recoiled violently when the graven reinforcement met it.

Kenji abandoned the line mid-sequence.

Fifth became movement.

Sixth drove toward her exposed side.

Sigrun planted the spear.

**Scarred Guard.**

The reinforcement across her damaged shoulder, ribs and forward leg linked into the shield-spear structure.

Not invulnerability.

A body made into architecture around its own injuries.

Kenji's sword crashed into the guard.

The impact shook both of them.

Sigrun stayed.

Behind her, a wagon wheel broke.

She heard it.

Did not look.

A civilian shouted.

Two soldiers moved to lift the axle.

Kenji saw her eyes flicker.

He looked past her.

The line was still too long.

He exhaled.

[[speaker:kenji]]“I need the road.”

[[speaker:sigrun]]Sigrun answered, “I know.”

[[speaker:kenji]]“You cannot keep standing.”

[[speaker:sigrun]]“I know.”

[[speaker:kenji]]“Then what are we doing?”

She smiled.

[[speaker:sigrun]]“Waiting.”

[[speaker:kenji]]“For what?”

[[speaker:sigrun]]“The road to stop needing me.”

Kenji hated that answer.

He had heard it before in different words.

Raska at the river.

Now Sigrun at the road.

He attacked harder.

Not angry.

Because delay was winning.

Sigrun accumulated wounds.

Jaw.

Forearm.

Lower ribs.

One cut behind the knee.

Thunder burns across the back.

Each serious region became graven.

Each became harder to repeat.

Kenji kept changing targets.

That was the correct counter.

Sigrun knew it.

Could not stop it forever.

The evacuation moved.

One wagon.

Another.

The broken wheel cleared.

Kenji's sword entered below Sigrun's collarbone.

Not deep enough to kill immediately.

Deep enough to change everything.

Her breath stopped.

The spear dipped.

Stormstep lightning hit her planted rear foot.

She went to one knee.

Kenji stepped back.

[[speaker:kenji]]“Done.”

Sigrun looked up.

[[speaker:sigrun]]“No.”

She stood.

Kenji stared.

The collar wound had graven.

So had the knee.

So had half her body.

She looked less like a person becoming stronger and more like a fortress desperately shoring every wall while fire spread inside it.

Behind her, the last civilian cart entered the northern bend.

Not clear yet.

Not yet.

Sigrun lifted the spear.

[[speaker:kenji]]Kenji whispered, “What are you?”

She answered with the truth.

[[speaker:sigrun]]“Tired.”

Then attacked him again.

## Chapter 377 — Last Body Standing Ends

Sigrun knew when her body stopped being able to continue.

It happened twelve minutes before she stopped.

There was a difference.

A large one.

Her left lung was partially collapsed.

The leg behind the knee had lost stable circulation.

Two ribs moved incorrectly when she breathed.

Her shield arm functioned because Graven Dominion had braced damage into structure, not because the arm was healthy.

Blood loss blurred the edge of Kenji's silhouette.

The body had made its decision.

Sigrun overruled it.

**Last Body Standing.**

Nothing healed.

That mattered enough that she said it aloud.

[[speaker:sigrun]]“Not healing.”

Kenji stopped for half a beat.

[[speaker:kenji]]“What?”

[[speaker:sigrun]]“I know what face you're making.”

[[speaker:kenji]]“I am not making a face.”

[[speaker:sigrun]]“You are.”

Sigrun drew breath through pain.

[[speaker:sigrun]]“Last Body Standing delays consequences. It does not remove them.”

Kenji looked at the blood covering her armour.

[[speaker:kenji]]“That is worse.”

[[speaker:sigrun]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:kenji]]“Stop.”

[[speaker:sigrun]]“No.”

He laughed once in disbelief.

[[speaker:kenji]]“I hate this country.”

[[speaker:sigrun]]“Get in line.”

Then the technique settled fully.

Catastrophic functions were delayed while she remained conscious and sustained the Dominion.

The half-collapsed lung kept contributing more than it should.

The damaged leg kept accepting weight.

The shield arm kept moving despite tears that would normally have ended reliable control.

Pain remained.

Blood remained.

Damage accumulated.

Consequences waited.

Kenji understood the danger better than most opponents would.

His entire art was movement.

He knew what it meant when a body borrowed movement from its own future.

[[speaker:kenji]]“You are going to die when that ends.”

[[speaker:sigrun]]“Maybe.”

[[speaker:kenji]]“That is not strategy.”

[[speaker:sigrun]]“The last wagon is still in sight.”

Kenji looked past her.

Damn.

Still moving.

He changed objective.

Not kill Sigrun.

Break consciousness.

A sword butt toward temple.

She blocked.

Thunder discharge through shield.

Her fingers spasmed.

Another strike to the jaw.

Graven reinforcement reduced repeat damage after the first clean hit, but internal shock still travelled.

Kenji varied angle.

Shoulder.

Hip.

Side of head.

He tried to end the technique without producing one fatal wound.

That restraint cost him time.

Sigrun knew it.

[[speaker:sigrun]]“Merciful?” she asked.

[[speaker:kenji]]“Efficient.”

[[speaker:sigrun]]“Liar.”

[[speaker:kenji]]“Rude.”

He swept her leg.

Sigrun hit the ground.

Stormstep did not trigger because she had not planted a new step.

Kenji raised the sword.

Sigrun rolled inside the line and drove the shield into his knee.

He stumbled.

She used the spear as a crutch and stood again.

Kenji stared at her.

[[speaker:kenji]]“You are making Paragons look stupid.”

[[speaker:sigrun]]“You are doing fine.”

[[speaker:kenji]]“That was almost nice.”

[[speaker:sigrun]]“Do not get used to it.”

A horn sounded behind Sigrun.

One long note.

Then two short.

The evacuation signal.

Road clear.

She heard it.

Kenji heard it.

Both stopped.

Sigrun looked past him at the Shinsei formation.

Then back toward the bend where the last wagon had vanished.

Done.

The road no longer needed her.

Her spear lowered.

Kenji's eyes widened.

[[speaker:kenji]]“Wait.”

Sigrun released Last Body Standing.

Every delayed consequence arrived.

There was no dramatic explosion.

Her body simply ceased negotiating.

The damaged leg folded.

The shield fell.

Blood came from her mouth.

Breath became almost nothing.

Graven Dominion collapsed a heartbeat later.

The reinforced wounds lost their temporary structural coherence all at once.

Sigrun hit the road.

Kenji caught her head before it struck stone.

He swore.

[[speaker:kenji]]“Medic!”

A Shinsei officer stared.

“Lady Sigrun is enemy command.”

[[speaker:kenji]]“Medic!”

The officer ran.

Sigrun's eyes opened a fraction.

[[speaker:sigrun]]“You won?”

Kenji looked down the empty road.

[[speaker:kenji]]“No.”

[[speaker:sigrun]]“Good.”

[[speaker:kenji]]“You are impossible.”

[[speaker:sigrun]]“Also good.”

Then she lost consciousness.

Kenji felt for a pulse.

Weak.

Present.

He exhaled.

A medic arrived.

Kenji moved aside.

[[speaker:kenji]]“Keep her alive.”

“Prisoner?”

He looked at Sigrun.

Then toward the northern bend.

[[speaker:kenji]]“Until Isgard comes for her.”

The medic blinked.

“Sir?”

Kenji's expression hardened.

[[speaker:kenji]]“We have the road. We do not need a dying woman for decoration.”

The medic nodded.

Sigrun was transferred under battlefield truce later that evening with other critical wounded.

Shinsei took the road.

Isgard kept the people who had been on it.

Kenji's report said:

**Enemy Paragon incapacitated. Corridor secured after evacuation completed.**

He stared at the sentence.

Raska had stayed until others crossed.

Sigrun had stayed until others disappeared around a bend.

Kenji had spent his life believing movement was courage.

Isgard was teaching him an irritating number of exceptions.

## Chapter 378 — Until One of Them Can Stand Again — Me
