## Chapter 406 — The First Five-Minute Retreat

The first Five-Minute Retreat almost failed because soldiers hated running from people they had just learned they could beat.

Jin had expected that.

He had not expected how loudly Arin Vale would solve it.

[[speaker:arin]]“BACK.”

Her swordswomen moved.

One captain hesitated.

Arin struck her across the helmet with the flat of her blade.

[[speaker:arin]]“Did I say debate?”

“No.”

[[speaker:arin]]“Then move.”

The Silver Horizon formation peeled away from the ridge.

Shinsei followed.

Exactly as Jin wanted.

Redline had just activated across the pursuing companies.

Three-times throughput turned their first downhill charge into something murderous.

Wuyue did not stand there to prove courage.

It left.

Predetermined lanes.

No panic.

Imperial shields disengaged in layers.

White Vein specialists used minimal-output footwork to preserve reserve.

Isgard cavalry refused the instinct to countercharge and instead drew pursuit across broken ground.

Every extra step mattered.

The Redline troops spent it all.

Qinggong.

Acceleration.

Weapon reinforcement.

Formation corrections.

Jin watched the cheap watch Luo had left him.

One minute.

The Shinsei line gained ground.

Two.

Wuyue gave more.

A nervous officer looked at Jin.

“We’re losing the ridge.”

“Yes.”

“Do we—”

“No.”

Three minutes.

The pursuing Shinsei officers sensed the trap.

Some tried to slow.

Their soldiers did not all receive the order at once.

Redline made physical action faster.

It did not make command communication instantaneous.

That was another weakness.

Four minutes.

Luo stood beside Jin.

[[speaker:luo]]“Watch hands.”

Jin did.

One swordsman’s grip trembled.

Another soldier stumbled after a direction change.

Skin flush became pallor in patches.

“Not yet,” Luo said.

Four-forty.

A boosted officer still looked strong.

Four-fifty.

The front rank began breathing too hard for the output they were producing.

Luo said, “Now.”

Jin raised one hand.

Three horn notes.

The retreat stopped being retreat.

Wuyue turned.

Not everywhere.

Selected sectors.

Fresh Imperial reserves hit the most unstable Redline cluster.

Arin’s swordswomen reversed direction through a prepared side lane and cut across the collapsing flank.

Liang Yue’s White Vein specialists targeted hands, knees and weapon timing rather than trying to match raw force.

Isgard cavalry finally charged.

Not into the Redline peak.

Into people whose bodies had just remembered the price.

The Shinsei formation broke.

Fast.

Too fast.

Jin saw some soldiers throw weapons down.

[[speaker:jin]]“Prisoners alive!”

Order relayed.

A few kept fighting.

They were stopped.

Some killed.

No miracle.

The Five-Minute Retreat was not pacifism.

It was timing.

By the end, Wuyue had surrendered nearly a mile of terrain and retaken half of it.

More importantly, the formation that would have shattered them at peak output no longer existed as a coherent fighting body.

Jin looked at the watch.

Six minutes twelve seconds.

Luo looked at him.

[[speaker:luo]]“You waited too long on the left.”

[[speaker:jin]]“We won.”

[[speaker:luo]]“You waited too long.”

Jin sighed.

“Yes.”

“Good.”

Arin arrived carrying a cracked helmet under one arm.

[[speaker:arin]]“Your retreat is insulting.”

[[speaker:jin]]“Effective?”

[[speaker:arin]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:jin]]“Then?”

[[speaker:arin]]“I still hate it.”

Jin nodded.

[[speaker:jin]]“Healthy.”

Sera approached from the rear line.

[[speaker:sera]]“How many?”

[[speaker:jin]]“Losses?”

[[speaker:sera]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:jin]]“Lower than last mass Redline clash by more than half.”

She looked at the field.

Wounded everywhere.

Still too many.

[[speaker:sera]]“Again?”

Jin answered.

[[speaker:jin]]“Until they solve it.”

Luo said, “They will.”

Jin looked at him.

“Yes.”

That was the point.

War was not finding one clever answer and keeping it forever.

It was surviving long enough to discover what the other side did to your answer next.

## Chapter 407 — Kai Moriyama Arrives

Kai Moriyama arrived in Isgard without banners announcing that he had once beaten Tsubasa Kurokawa.

He did not need them.

The older Shinsei officers knew.

The younger ones had heard the story until it became half history, half military bedtime myth.

Tsubasa at twenty-three or twenty-four.

Brilliant.

Already terrifying.

Still not unbeatable.

Kai Moriyama, then the stronger Sovereign of the Red Furnace region, had defeated him cleanly.

And then done something more important.

He had not killed him.

He asked whether a peace that required murdering every person who said no was meaningfully different from the warlords Shinsei claimed to replace.

Tsubasa returned later with negotiated terms instead of another duel.

Kai joined because the young man had learned.

That history mattered more to Kai than the victory.

Now he stepped off the transport platform in Isgard carrying a heavy saber over one shoulder and looked at the war like a forge he had been asked to inspect.

Too hot.

Too much stress.

Several fractures already visible.

Good steel could still fail if the smith insisted the answer was always more heat.

Kenji Narukami met him first.

[[speaker:kenji]]“You came.”

Kai looked at him.

[[speaker:kai]]“That was the order.”

Kenji smiled despite fatigue.

[[speaker:kenji]]“You hate boats.”

[[speaker:kai]]“I hate bad boats.”

[[speaker:kenji]]“That was a good one.”

[[speaker:kai]]“It moved.”

Kenji laughed.

There.

Still a young man under the war.

Kai put one hand on his shoulder.

[[speaker:kai]]“Raska?”

The smile vanished.

Kenji looked away.

[[speaker:kenji]]“I killed him.”

[[speaker:kai]]“Battle?”

[[speaker:kenji]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:kai]]“Was he trying to kill you?”

“Yes.”

[[speaker:kai]]“Then don’t turn honesty into self-indulgence.”

Kenji looked back.

Kai’s voice softened by one degree.

[[speaker:kai]]“But remember him.”

Kenji nodded.

They walked.

The front briefing was already waiting.

Eirik.

Nao.

Aya.

Jun, returned early and visibly not at full strength.

Shinsei officers.

Maps.

Casualty sheets.

Redline issue charts.

Kai looked at the latter longest.

[[speaker:kai]]“How many mass activations?”

“Two major,” Eirik said. “Several smaller.”

“Aya?”

She looked at him.

[[speaker:aya]]“Too many.”

Kai nodded.

Good answer.

Eirik moved to the main map.

[[speaker:eirik]]“Wuyue has developed a timed withdrawal against Redline.”

Kai studied it.

[[speaker:kai]]“They make us spend the window moving.”

[[speaker:eirik]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:kai]]“Then hit the crash.”

[[speaker:eirik]]“Yes.”

Kai grunted.

[[speaker:kai]]“Smart.”

Jun leaned on the halberd.

[[speaker:jun]]“Annoying.”

[[speaker:kai]]“Those are usually related.”

Kai looked at the Wuyue apex markers.

[[speaker:kai]]“Sera.”

“Peak Paragon,” Eirik said.

[[speaker:kai]]“Kael?”

“New Paragon. Better than initial model.”

Jun said, “He learns quickly.”

Kai looked at him.

[[speaker:kai]]“You lost.”

[[speaker:jun]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:kai]]“Good.”

Jun frowned.

Kenji grinned.

Kai continued.

[[speaker:kai]]“If losing teaches you faster than winning, it’s cheaper than stupidity.”

Aya said dryly, “Physically it was not cheap.”

Kai looked at Jun’s bandaging.

[[speaker:kai]]“Also fair.”

Then he pointed at the front.

[[speaker:kai]]“Do not send me to duel Kael for revenge.”

Eirik’s mouth moved slightly.

[[speaker:eirik]]“Tsubasa already said the same.”

[[speaker:kai]]“Good. He remembers things.”

[[speaker:eirik]]“Your purpose is pressure.”

[[speaker:kai]]“Exactly.”

Kai’s Crimson Crucible Dominion was not a ceremonial challenge field.

It was an army problem.

Heat.

Dehydration.

Exhaustion.

Every prolonged engagement became worse for everyone except the man whose body had been trained inside a furnace philosophy for decades.

Kai looked at the terrain.

[[speaker:kai]]“Where does Jin expect us to hit?”

Eirik pointed.

Kai shook his head.

[[speaker:kai]]“Then not there.”

Jun raised an eyebrow.

[[speaker:jun]]“That simple?”

[[speaker:kai]]“No.”

Kai looked at him.

[[speaker:kai]]“But we can begin by respecting that their strategist is not an idiot.”

He moved one marker three miles south.

[[speaker:kai]]“Make them choose between protecting the evacuation road and preserving their Five-Minute Retreat geometry.”

Nao studied it.

[[speaker:nao]]“That increases civilian pressure.”

Kai looked at her.

[[speaker:kai]]“Then we build an exclusion corridor and announce it before engagement.”

Jun nodded.

Nao did too.

That was Kai.

Brutal when necessary.

Not careless.

He looked at the map again.

[[speaker:kai]]“Let’s see what Wuyue’s new Paragon learned from Jun.”

Far away, Kael Veyran sneezed.

Luo looked at him.

[[speaker:luo]]“Medical issue?”

[[speaker:kael]]“No.”

[[speaker:luo]]“How do you know?”

[[speaker:kael]]“Instinct.”

Luo sighed.

Somewhere south, the temperature began to rise.

## Chapter 408 — Kael Learns What High Paragon Means

Kael understood Kai Moriyama’s Domain when his mouth went dry.

That was the first sign.

Not fire falling from the sky.

Not a magical furnace.

Heat.

Physical.

Relentless.

**Crimson Crucible Dominion.**

The air temperature rose high enough that breathing itself became work.

Sweat appeared almost immediately.

Armor trapped heat.

Muscles lost water.

Qi-assisted cooling became another drain on reserve.

And across from him, Kai looked increasingly comfortable.

That was offensive.

Kael lifted both hands.

[[speaker:kael]]“You look happy.”

Kai rested the heavy saber against one shoulder.

[[speaker:kai]]“I grew up near furnaces.”

[[speaker:kael]]“I grew up near mountains.”

[[speaker:kai]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:kael]]“Mountains are better.”

[[speaker:kai]]“Objectively false.”

Then Kai moved.

The first saber clash taught Kael the tier gap.

Not simply stronger.

More complete.

Kai’s body understood how to convert Dominion heat into movement, how to let Redsteel Heart build through repeated clashes, how to choose exchanges that made the enemy spend cooling qi while increasing his own conditioned output.

Kael blocked one saber strike through Worldweight terrain.

The ground accepted the force.

Stored burden returned into Kai’s stance.

Kai adjusted before it settled.

Good.

Experienced.

He did not fight Worldweight as though it were new.

He reduced planted commitment when needed.

Used shorter saber arcs.

Sometimes deliberately gave Kael smaller burdens because the heat was already doing the long-term work.

Kael hated him immediately.

[[speaker:kael]]“Jun was more straightforward.”

Kai laughed.

[[speaker:kai]]“Jun is thirty-six and formal.”

[[speaker:kael]]“What are you?”

[[speaker:kai]]“Forty-six and tired.”

The saber came down.

**Crimson Cleave.**

Kael redirected.

Not enough.

The blade impact landed against his sand-reinforced forearm guard.

Then stored heat expanded through the collision point.

Armor buckled.

Kael’s skin burned beneath it.

He retreated.

Bad.

Heat followed.

Crimson Crucible was not a wall he could step out of casually while Kai controlled the engagement area.

Kael changed terrain.

Sand rose.

Dry.

Too dry.

The Domain itself made loose sand more irritating to breathe and less useful for long wet-packed pressure structures.

Kai had not nullified Kael.

He had made Kael’s normal battlefield less comfortable.

That was what High Paragon felt like.

Not a bigger number.

A person whose entire system had survived enough real fights to remove easy answers.

Kael attacked.

Worldweight accumulated through the ground.

Kai received the first strike with Last Ember Guard.

Not passively.

He structured saber and stance to catch part of the clash-generated heat, then immediately vented it into a short countercut.

Kael barely moved in time.

The edge opened his side.

Blood evaporated strangely fast on heated armor.

Kael’s breathing worsened.

Kai saw.

[[speaker:kai]]“Still want mountains?”

[[speaker:kael]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:kai]]“Stubborn.”

[[speaker:kael]]“Public requirement.”

Kai laughed again.

The fight continued.

Ten minutes.

Too long inside Crucible.

Kael’s muscles started cramping.

His cooling qi expenditure became significant.

Worldweight still functioned.

He still landed two serious hits.

One cracked Kai’s chest guard.

Another drove him through a ridge of stone.

Kai came out hotter.

Literally.

Redsteel Heart had built.

His launches became more explosive.

Saber faster.

Physical response sharper.

Kael realized the truth.

He could lose this fight while fighting well.

That was unpleasant.

Also useful.

Kai entered close.

Kael tried to redirect burden through the terrain.

Kai let the stance sink.

Accepted the burden.

Then used the planted weight as structure for a brutal rising saber strike.

Kael blocked.

Heat burst.

His damaged shoulder reopened.

He went to one knee.

Kai stopped the next cut before it reached his neck.

Not mercy.

Assessment.

[[speaker:kai]]“You’re done.”

Kael looked up.

“No.”

[[speaker:kai]]“You are.”

[[speaker:kael]]“No.”

Kai sighed.

[[speaker:kai]]“I see why people find you tiring.”

Kael stood.

Badly.

Worldweight Dominion remained.

Kai raised the saber again.

Then the air changed.

A pale orchid petal formed between them.

Kai’s eyes shifted.

Kael closed his.

[[speaker:kael]]“Of course.”

Sera stepped into the edge of Crimson Crucible.

Kai looked at her.

[[speaker:kai]]“Pale Orchid.”

Sera looked at Kael.

[[speaker:sera]]“You learned?”

He glared.

“Yes.”

[[speaker:sera]]“Good.”

Then she looked at Kai.

[[speaker:sera]]“My turn.”

## Chapter 409 — Pale Orchid

Kai Moriyama liked Sera immediately.

That was inconvenient.

She entered a High-Paragon furnace without posturing.

No speech.

No declaration that she would avenge Kael.

She simply looked at the battlefield and opened **Orchid Dominion**.

The overlap became complicated.

Crimson Crucible remained.

Heat did not vanish because another Domain appeared.

Sera still had to spend qi cooling her body.

Her breathing still mattered.

Kai’s physical conditioning still favored him.

But every serious offensive commitment he made inside Orchid authority created something he disliked almost as much as losing.

An opening he had earned himself.

**Counter-Bloom.**

Kai swung.

A full saber commitment opened his left rib line for one instant.

An orchid response bloomed there.

Needles.

He twisted.

Two missed.

One entered shallow.

Not enough to disable.

Enough to remind him the rule was real.

Kai smiled.

[[speaker:kai]]“Annoying.”

Sera’s expression did not change.

[[speaker:sera]]“I get that often.”

He attacked smaller.

Good.

Reduced commitment.

Short saber cuts.

Less opening.

Smaller Counter-Blooms.

The same answer strong fighters always eventually discovered.

Orchid Dominion punished aggression.

It did not forbid intelligence.

Kai’s experience showed immediately.

He stopped feeding Sera large blooms.

Instead he used Crucible to make time expensive.

Heat.

Dehydration.

Small exchanges.

Force Sera to spend reserve regulating body temperature while giving her fewer decisive openings.

Sera understood.

Changed her answer too.

She stopped chasing Counter-Blooms as attacks.

Used them for position.

False needles.

Meridian feints.

Short disabling pressure that made Kai guard one side while she moved through the other.

The Orchid system was not one gimmick either.

Kai attacked with Crimson Cleave.

Bigger commitment.

Sera accepted the heat risk because the bloom would be better.

The saber came down.

She moved half a step.

Too small.

The edge cut her upper arm.

Blood appeared.

Kai’s eyes sharpened.

Then his own committed hip line opened.

Sera’s Counter-Bloom struck the femoral meridian junction.

Not full severance.

Enough to numb the leg for one step.

Kai nearly fell.

He recovered.

Sera’s second strike hit his shoulder.

Kai laughed.

Actually laughed.

“Good.”

Kael, being treated twenty paces away, muttered:

[[speaker:kael]]“Stop encouraging people.”

Luo shoved a bandage into his hand.

[[speaker:luo]]“Be quiet.”

The fight continued.

Kai remained dangerous.

Sera was better.

Not infinitely.

Not Rhen.

Peak Paragon against High Paragon.

A real gap, but one Kai’s experience could make expensive.

Crucible pushed her body.

Sweat ran down her neck.

Cooling qi cost reserve.

Kai’s saber opened one cut across her thigh.

Sera answered with a Counter-Bloom that partially disrupted his weapon hand.

He switched grip.

She adapted.

Then something changed beyond them.

A Shinsei signal horn.

Three notes.

Kai listened.

Objective achieved.

Not victory over Sera.

The southern pressure operation had forced the alliance to abandon one planned reinforcement route and reposition two formations.

His job was done.

Kai stepped back.

Sera did not chase.

[[speaker:sera]]“Leaving?”

[[speaker:kai]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:sera]]“You can still fight.”

[[speaker:kai]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:sera]]“Then why?”

Kai looked at her.

[[speaker:kai]]“Because I came here to change the battlefield, not prove I have a larger ego than Kael.”

From the medical line:

[[speaker:kael]]“I HEARD THAT.”

Kai looked past Sera.

“Good.”

Sera almost smiled.

Then Kai’s expression became serious.

[[speaker:kai]]“You are stronger than the report.”

[[speaker:sera]]“So are you.”

[[speaker:kai]]“Mine was accurate.”

[[speaker:sera]]“Then your report writers are better.”

Kai barked a laugh.

He withdrew under disciplined cover.

No rout.

No humiliation.

Sera let Orchid Dominion fade.

The heat receded slowly.

Kael looked at her.

[[speaker:kael]]“You could have finished him.”

[[speaker:sera]]“Maybe.”

[[speaker:kael]]“Maybe?”

[[speaker:sera]]“He’s High Paragon and not stupid.”

Kael frowned.

[[speaker:kael]]“That sounded respectful.”

[[speaker:sera]]“It is.”

[[speaker:kael]]“Enemy.”

[[speaker:sera]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:kael]]“Still respectful.”

[[speaker:sera]]“Yes.”

Kael considered that.

Then nodded.

Fair.

Sera looked toward the retreating Shinsei lines.

The war had just become harder.

Not because Kai was evil.

Because he was competent.

Those were always worse.

## Chapter 410 — The Second Wave Opens at Minute Four

Jin knew Shinsei had solved the Five-Minute Retreat when the first Redline wave began dying and nobody followed it.

That was wrong.

The old pattern:

Activate.

Pursue.

Peak.

Crash.

Wuyue reverses.

This time, Shinsei’s first boosted formation chased exactly as expected.

Wuyue gave ground.

Jin watched the timer.

Three minutes thirty.

The first signs of future crash appeared.

Hand tremor.

Overheating.

One soldier’s step shortened.

Jin prepared the reversal signal.

Three-fifty.

Then Kai’s southern reinforcement officer raised a different flag.

Fresh troops moved.

Jin’s face changed.

[[speaker:jin]]“No.”

Four minutes.

A second line of Shinsei soldiers broke Redline seals.

Not after the first wave crashed.

Before.

Fresh 3× output surged into the battlefield just as Wuyue’s formations began turning to punish the first wave.

The trap reversed.

Wuyue was no longer retreating cleanly.

It was **mid-transition**.

Front units turning forward.

Rear units moving up.

Lanes partially closed.

Perfect time to hit them.

Jin slammed one hand onto the table.

[[speaker:jin]]“Abort counterattack!”

Signal horns changed.

Too slow in two sectors.

The second Redline wave crashed into them.

Imperial infantry lost a full company line.

One Isgard formation folded before Varok could reposition.

Arin’s swordswomen escaped only because she recognized the second activation before the horn and ordered lateral movement.

Jin saw it all.

Shinsei had learned.

Not just reacted.

Learned.

The Five-Minute Retreat still worked against one timed wave.

So Shinsei staggered the waves.

Make the first expensive.

Force Wuyue to turn.

Then activate the second.

Borrow lifespan in sequence instead of all at once.

Luo arrived at the command position already swearing.

[[speaker:luo]]“They redosed?”

[[speaker:jin]]“Fresh troops.”

[[speaker:luo]]“Better medically.”

[[speaker:jin]]“Worse strategically.”

[[speaker:luo]]“Yes.”

Jin tore another plan.

Luo looked at him.

[[speaker:luo]]“You enjoy that too much.”

[[speaker:jin]]“I hate paper.”

[[speaker:luo]]“That explains nothing.”

Sera entered from the right sector.

Blood on one sleeve from the Kai clash.

[[speaker:sera]]“Status.”

Jin pointed.

[[speaker:jin]]“They found the seam.”

She saw immediately.

[[speaker:sera]]“Second wave before crash.”

[[speaker:jin]]“Yes.”

[[speaker:sera]]“How do we answer?”

Jin looked at the map.

[[speaker:jin]]“Not today.”

Sera stared.

He continued.

[[speaker:jin]]“Today we survive it. Then we answer.”

Good.

No fake genius.

No instant solution because Jin was No.3 strategist.

Sometimes the enemy found something first.

The skill was recognizing that before pride killed people.

Wuyue gave more ground.

Sera used Orchid Dominion only where collapse threatened hospitals.

Kael, still injured, did not return because Luo threatened to sedate him.

He believed the threat.

Liang Yue coordinated efficient retreat sectors.

Solveig rerouted Isgard reserves through Astrid’s hidden roads.

Varok physically held one retreat corridor together long enough for the second wave to spend itself.

The battle ended worse than the first Five-Minute Retreat test.

Not catastrophic.

Bad enough.

Jin stood over the evening casualty ledger.

[[speaker:jin]]“Deaths?”

The aide gave him the number.

He closed his eyes.

Then opened them.

[[speaker:jin]]“Write them all.”

“They are being written.”

[[speaker:jin]]“I know.”

He looked at the map.

Wuyue had found a tactic.

Shinsei found the counter.

Tomorrow they would search again.

Across the Shinsei medical network, Aya Katsuragi received the same staggered-wave report and understood something else.

Command had not responded to the moral horror of Redline by reducing dependence on it.

It had improved the doctrine.

That frightened her more than the first mass activation.

Because cruelty could be impulsive.

This was learning.
