## 1. Aya Katsuragi — Paragon-level healing ceiling

Aya remains **Shinsei No.7 / Established Paragon / Hundred Pulse Dominion**, and her medical ability must scale accordingly.

### 1.1 Full-restoration rule

Aya can restore another living Paragon from severe but medically recoverable combat trauma back to **effectively 100% fighting condition** when she has enough uninterrupted treatment and recovery time.

This includes, when not explicitly destroyed beyond recovery:

- torn or destabilized meridians;
- internal bleeding;
- organ trauma;
- broken bones;
- major muscle / tendon injury;
- foreign qi contamination;
- qi deviation caused by combat;
- circulation damage caused by Dominion overuse;
- severe conventional weapon / impact trauma.

A patient returning at 70–80% means Aya **rushed them back before treatment was complete**, not that she lacks the cultivation or medical skill to finish the restoration.

### 1.2 Explicit examples

- **Kai Moriyama** can be badly wounded, receive several days of uninterrupted treatment from Aya, and later return at **100%**.
- **Eirik Voss** can likewise be restored to **100%** after conventional Paragon-level battle injuries.
- **Jun Kajihara** may first return at approximately 70–80% because Shinsei needs him early; if later given complete treatment, Aya can finish restoring him.
- **Kenji, Hana, Nao and other living Shinsei Paragons** follow the same medical rule for recoverable injuries.

### 1.3 Hard limits

Aya cannot:

- resurrect the dead;
- restore **already-consumed Axtaya lifespan / future vitality**;
- regenerate a loss explicitly established in prose as permanently beyond medical reconstruction;
- instantly treat multiple critically injured Paragons plus thousands of ordinary casualties without time, triage and resource pressure.

She can repair the **physical** consequences of Axtaya — torn muscle, damaged meridians, organ injury, shock — while the lifespan already spent remains gone.

Therefore Haru can eventually look physically recovered after his three-Redline incident while still having permanently burned roughly decades of future life.

### 1.4 Strategic consequence

Aya is not merely a battlefield support character. She is one of Shinsei's greatest strategic advantages because she can **return apex fighters to the war at full strength**.

Wuyue may spend enormous effort seriously injuring Kai or Eirik, only for intelligence reports several days later to confirm that the same High Paragon has returned fully restored.

This strengthens the late-Phase-II attrition problem:

- Shinsei has deeper ordinary manpower;
- Shinsei can replace formations;
- Shinsei also possesses a Paragon physician capable of recycling surviving apex fighters back into battle.

The limiting resource is often **time**, not Aya's healing ceiling.

# 2. Tsubasa Kurokawa — current apex benchmark

Tsubasa remains:

- **age 42**;
- **Shinsei No.1**;
- **Peak Paragon**;
- Chained Crescent practitioner;
- owner of **Tethered Heaven Dominion**;
- owner of **One Chain Binds Heaven**.

He does **not** ascend into a new cultivation realm. The series retains Paragon as the highest normal martial level.

### 2.1 Historical scale

At approximately 31, Tsubasa's Heaven Gate battle against three Paragons completed the principle that carried him into Peak Paragon.

Present-day Tsubasa is eleven years beyond that version of himself.

**Current lock:** present-day base Tsubasa is the strongest conventional cultivator yet produced by the normal ranking system and is **stronger overall than Aethon Vael**.

Aethon remains an absurd historical monster whose confrontation forced old Rhen to reveal power that had never previously been necessary. Tsubasa being stronger must not retroactively make Aethon weak.

The distinction is:

- Aethon remains a legendary apex opponent;
- present-day Tsubasa has surpassed that benchmark;
- post-seclusion Rhen remains completely outside the ordinary comparison.

# 3. New Tsubasa art — Heaven Returns What It Receives

## **Heaven Returns What It Receives — Supreme Passive / Counter**

Every genuine martial attack Tsubasa **receives** can contribute its transmitted force to his next attack.

The art captures usable martial force / qi that actually enters Tsubasa's defensive structure through:

- direct physical impact;
- weapon collision;
- qi transmitted through a clash;
- shockwave / pressure impact that genuinely reaches him;
- force received through his crescent;
- force received through the connected chain.

The captured force is converted into compressed return energy routed through Tsubasa's own circulation and Chained Crescent structure.

### 3.1 Mandatory next-attack rule

The converted energy is not a permanent battery.

It must fuel **Tsubasa's next committed attack**.

If several opponents hit him before he attacks again, their received force may accumulate together up to what his body can safely process. When Tsubasa makes his next committed offensive release, the stored return energy is discharged with it.

He cannot collect attacks indefinitely and save them for another day.

### 3.2 Damage is not negated

This art does **not** make incoming attacks harmless.

Tsubasa may simultaneously:

- be cut;
- suffer broken bone / organ trauma;
- receive meridian damage;
- lose blood;
- be physically displaced;

and still convert part of the martial force that created those consequences.

**Core identity:** hurting Tsubasa and empowering Tsubasa can happen at the same time.

Poison, suffocation, disease and other non-impact conditions are not magically converted into attack energy simply because they harm him.

### 3.3 Base-state throughput limit

In base Peak-Paragon condition, Tsubasa's circulation still has a maximum amount of foreign transmitted force it can safely process before his next attack.

Excess force beyond that limit:

- damages him normally;
- may partially dissipate;
- can overload circulation if he greedily tries to retain too much.

The passive therefore rewards Tsubasa for accepting calculated collisions, not for standing motionless inside infinite attacks.

# 4. Tsubasa takes one 3× Redline in the final battle

**Locked:** Tsubasa personally uses a **3× Redline** during the final confrontation.

This is a deliberate choice by a man who understands the approximate cost. It is not accidental dosing and not ignorance.

Standard working consequence remains:

- approximately **3× combat throughput**;
- approximately **five-minute peak window**;
- approximately **five years of future vitality / lifespan** consumed for a healthy standard case.

Tsubasa's exact personal cost can vary with physiology and battle damage, but the story should treat the choice as knowingly burning years of his own future.

One Redline is currently locked for Tsubasa. A same-day second or third Tsubasa dose is **not** automatically approved by this file.

### 4.1 Redline does not create the conversion art

**Heaven Returns What It Receives already belongs to Tsubasa.**

Redline instead multiplies the rate at which his body can:

- circulate his own Peak-Paragon output;
- process received attack force;
- compress converted return energy;
- move that energy through body → hand → chain → crescent;
- survive the transition from one received clash into the retaliatory strike.

### 4.2 Why boosted attacks can exceed a simple “3×” impression

Axtaya multiplies Tsubasa's **throughput**, not his realm, intelligence, skill knowledge or Dominion quality.

However, a retaliatory attack can temporarily carry:

1. Tsubasa's own Redline-accelerated output; **plus**
2. stored force converted from attacks he just received.

Therefore a single counter can hit harder than an observer would expect from a simple flat 3× comparison.

The extra force came from the enemy; Redline merely gives Tsubasa the throughput to process and return much more of it before his body becomes the bottleneck.

# 5. Tethered Heaven Dominion — present-day severity

The existing two laws remain canon and are not replaced.

### 5.1 Detached hostile qi weakens with distance

Hostile projected qi loses cohesion as it travels after leaving continuous physical connection with its owner.

Tsubasa's own crescent remains connected through:

**body → hand → chain → crescent**,

so his weapon can carry near-full martial output far beyond normal close range without becoming detached qi.

### 5.2 Closing distance creates the retreat trap

At close range, Tethered Heaven establishes increasingly oppressive qi tether pressure against attempts to **increase distance from Tsubasa**.

The opponent can still retreat through sufficient physical / qi output, but repeated forced withdrawals become increasingly expensive and circulatorily punishing.

At current Peak-Paragon refinement the practical trap is:

- staying far away makes detached attacks progressively inefficient;
- entering close range is possible;
- escaping after entering costs much more than approaching;
- attacking Tsubasa hard risks feeding **Heaven Returns What It Receives**.

This creates a final-antagonist system without gravity magic, spatial manipulation, teleportation or reality rewriting.

# 6. One Chain Binds Heaven — present-day apex use

**One Chain Binds Heaven** remains Tsubasa's Ultimate.

Present-day usage is far more refined than the version associated with Heaven Gate.

Tsubasa can establish a large prepared physical chain network using real anchor points such as:

- stone structures;
- fortress towers;
- cliffs;
- trees;
- ships;
- embedded chain stakes;
- heavy battlefield debris;
- sufficiently durable weapons / fixtures.

There remains:

- one Tsubasa;
- one physical Chained Crescent system;
- no clone;
- no teleportation;
- no alternate space;
- no telekinetic chain.

The network exists to preserve momentum, redirect the crescent through physically plausible angles and let Tsubasa deliver connected martial force across an enormous prepared field.

### 6.1 Conversion synergy

Force received through the physically connected crescent / chain can feed **Heaven Returns What It Receives**.

Converted return energy can then be discharged back through the same body → hand → chain → crescent route on Tsubasa's next committed attack.

The chain network does **not** generate free energy. It gives Tsubasa an extraordinarily efficient physical delivery system for power he already owns or has just converted from an enemy collision.

### 6.2 3× apex state

The locked maximum conventional Tsubasa state is:

**Tethered Heaven Dominion + One Chain Binds Heaven + one 3× Redline + Heaven Returns What It Receives.**

For the Redline window:

- detached hostile ranged qi is disadvantaged;
- retreat from Tsubasa's close pressure is expensive;
- the chained crescent retains physical connection and high output at range;
- Tsubasa operates at approximately triple combat throughput;
- every genuine attack he receives can feed his next attack within his accelerated processing limit.

The wrong response is therefore to have many Paragons blindly fire their largest attacks at him at once. If he survives / receives those collisions, they may give him an even more violent retaliation.

A strategist or experienced fighter should eventually recognize the principle as:

**stop feeding him.**

# 7. Tsubasa vs Aethon — locked comparison

The final arc should not leave this ambiguous internally.

### Base present-day Tsubasa

**Stronger overall than Aethon Vael.**

Aethon's predictive advantage remains terrifying, but Tsubasa's present system attacks the decision tree itself:

- distance weakens detached attacks;
- closing distance creates a retreat problem;
- direct violent commitment can become fuel for Tsubasa's next strike;
- the Chained Crescent changes attack geometry through genuine physical anchors.

Knowing what Tsubasa is about to do does not guarantee that a good response exists.

### 3× Tsubasa

**Decisively above the Aethon benchmark for the Redline window.**

Do not imply that 3× Tsubasa has entered a new realm. He remains Peak Paragon operating at violently accelerated throughput with a uniquely synergistic martial system.

# 8. Sera matchup rule

Sera remains **Peak Paragon** and one of the only conventional cultivators capable of forcing Tsubasa to reveal his complete apex package.

Her **Orchid Dominion** is an unusually dangerous matchup because Tsubasa's committed attacks can earn Counter-Blooms.

At the same time, Tsubasa's return mechanic creates reciprocal escalation:

1. Tsubasa commits an attack;
2. Sera earns / releases Counter-Bloom pressure;
3. Tsubasa may receive part of the collision and convert it;
4. his next attack becomes more violent;
5. the larger commitment creates another dangerous Orchid response.

This loop must make Sera look exceptional rather than disposable.

Current intended hierarchy:

- Sera can genuinely wound, pressure and surprise base Tsubasa;
- she can force him toward Dominion / Ultimate / Redline escalation;
- her full Peak-Paragon system remains relevant even against the strongest conventional cultivator;
- in a clean prolonged 1v1 against Tsubasa's full 3× apex package, Tsubasa remains favored.

Exact final choreography and the precise moment of **Ten Thousand Blooms** remain subject to the detailed Phase III chapter map, but the matchup must preserve Sera's agency and power.

1. Aya can fully restore Kai / Eirik and other living Paragons from recoverable conventional injuries if given sufficient treatment time.

8. Tsubasa takes one deliberate 3× Redline during the final confrontation.
9. Redline accelerates throughput and conversion rather than granting a new realm or new technique knowledge.
10. Tsubasa's full apex state must be frightening enough that readers understand why he is Shinsei's final martial weapon.
