## Locked Domain identity notes

### Tsubasa — Tethered Heaven Dominion
- Detached hostile qi progressively loses cohesion with distance from its owner.
- Tsubasa's chained crescent remains physically connected through body → hand → chain → blade, preserving his own extended weapon output.
- The closer a hostile target gets to Tsubasa, the more retreat becomes burdened by tether pressure.
- Movement feels progressively heavier at close range, especially when trying to increase distance from him.
- This is not gravity and does not literally increase body mass.

### Jin — Hundred Locks Dominion
Every clean touch progressively restricts the contacted body's circulation and usable fighting capacity.

### Haru — Carved Spear Dominion
Completed spear thrusts leave temporary stored attack lines that Haru may trigger once later along their original path.

### Hana — Hollow Sky Dominion
Moving low-pressure zones make breathing, balance and sustained exertion worse for enemies; pressure equalization can create violent openings.

### Kai — Crimson Crucible Dominion
Extreme heat rapidly dehydrates enemies, exhausts them and can eventually make them collapse or lose consciousness, while Kai's conditioned body becomes increasingly explosive.

### Aya — Hundred Pulse Dominion
Hostile bodies suffer progressive paralysis. They may force movement, but doing so tears and damages suppressed meridians.

### Kenji — Stormstep Dominion
Every meaningful hostile planted step or landing calls a Thunder-Qi strike onto that position. Individual strikes are avoidable; sustained movement causes relentless repetition.

### Jun — Breach Dominion
If Jun's attack actually lands, armour, shields and conventional defensive mitigation provide no meaningful reduction to the connected strike. The Domain does not guarantee contact.

### Eirik — Crownless Dominion
Hostile cultivators cannot properly replenish spent qi from the battlefield's ambient energy. Their existing reserve remains theirs, but once spent, very little returns while the Domain persists.

### Nao — Stillwater Dominion
Hostile circulatory pressure progressively falls. Enemies may accept declining output or force themselves back toward full power by overpressurizing their meridians and risking internal damage.
