## Shinsei Guild — LOCKED

- Shinrin's ruling institution is permanently named **Shinsei Guild**.
- Shinsei began as a coalition meant to end clan warfare, protect trade routes and coordinate civilian relief rather than as an openly conquering state.
- By the final arc it functions as Shinrin's central government, military command, taxation authority, judiciary, infrastructure administration and martial regulator while retaining the historical word `Guild`.
- Symbol: **a broken circle joined by one horizontal line**.
- Old motto: **“Peace Requires Someone to Hold the Line.”**

## Paragon rule — LOCKED

Every normal cultivator who reaches **Paragon** has a personal **Dominion**.

- The official Shinsei ten all have Dominions.
- Yurushi Amagiri is an unranked Established Paragon and has **Twilight Dominion**.
- Xie Wuchen is only newly Sovereign and therefore has no Dominion yet.
- Rhen remains outside the ordinary ranking system; **Petals in Stasis** remains the non-Dominion anti-Domain exception.

## Tsubasa Kurokawa — later approved additions

- Present age: **42**.
- He is Shinsei's undisputed No.1 and has a clear gap over Shunto despite Shunto being the official No.2.
- Young Tsubasa became famous for holding the final crossing during the **Seven Bridges** crisis until civilians escaped.
- Kai Moriyama defeated Tsubasa when Tsubasa was roughly 23–24; Tsubasa later returned with negotiated terms rather than demanding a rematch, which is why Kai chose to join him.
- At approximately 31, Established-Paragon Tsubasa fought three Paragons at **Heaven Gate**, completed his apex chained-crescent principle, defeated all three and advanced to Peak Paragon.

### ULTIMATE — One Chain Binds Heaven — LOCKED

Requires **Tethered Heaven Dominion**.

Tsubasa establishes genuine physical chain anchors through terrain and structures so the uninterrupted chain becomes a continentally scaled extension of his connected martial reach within the prepared battlefield.

- one Tsubasa, one chain, one crescent;
- no clones, teleportation, duplicated weapons or spatial magic;
- continuous body → hand → chain → crescent connection preserves his output against the Domain's detached-qi weakening rule;
- anchor geometry lets the crescent repeatedly redirect while preserving real physical momentum;
- targets near the connected chain network can encounter the same retreat-resistance principle normally strongest near Tsubasa;
- anchors and chain sections can be destroyed by sufficient force;
- the strain on body, meridians, concentration and weapon is extreme.

Present-day Tsubasa has had roughly eleven further years to refine the power that defeated three Paragons at Heaven Gate.

## Locked Domain identity notes

### Tsubasa — Tethered Heaven Dominion
- Detached hostile qi progressively loses cohesion with distance from its owner.
- Tsubasa's chained crescent remains physically connected through body → hand → chain → blade, preserving his own extended weapon output.
- The closer a hostile target gets to Tsubasa, the more retreat becomes burdened by tether pressure.
- Movement feels progressively heavier at close range, especially when trying to increase distance from him.
- This is not gravity and does not literally increase body mass.

### Shunto — Hundred Locks Dominion
Every clean touch progressively restricts the contacted body's circulation and usable fighting capacity.

### Haru — Carved Spear Dominion
Completed spear thrusts leave temporary stored attack lines that Haru may trigger once later along their original path.

### Hana — Hollow Sky Dominion
Moving low-pressure zones make breathing, balance and sustained exertion worse for enemies; pressure equalization can create violent openings.

### Kai — Crimson Crucible Dominion
Extreme heat rapidly dehydrates enemies, exhausts them and can eventually make them collapse or lose consciousness, while Kai's conditioned body becomes increasingly explosive.

### Aya — Hundred Pulse Dominion
Hostile bodies suffer progressive paralysis. They may force movement, but doing so tears and damages suppressed meridians.

### Kenji — Stormstep Dominion
Every meaningful hostile planted step or landing calls a Thunder-Qi strike onto that position. Individual strikes are avoidable; sustained movement causes relentless repetition.

### Jun — Breach Dominion
If Jun's attack actually lands, armour, shields and conventional defensive mitigation provide no meaningful reduction to the connected strike. The Domain does not guarantee contact.

### Eirik — Crownless Dominion
Hostile cultivators cannot properly replenish spent qi from the battlefield's ambient energy. Their existing reserve remains theirs, but once spent, very little returns while the Domain persists.

### Nao — Stillwater Dominion
Hostile circulatory pressure progressively falls. Enemies may accept declining output or force themselves back toward full power by overpressurizing their meridians and risking internal damage.
