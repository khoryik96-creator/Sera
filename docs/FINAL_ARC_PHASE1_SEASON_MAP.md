# Audit conclusions that this map fixes

1. **There was no actual Phase I prose or dedicated Phase I season map.** The repository jumped from authoritative Phase I continuity requirements to an obsolete conspiracy outline, while detailed maps existed only for Phases II and III.
2. The old `FINAL_ARC_OUTLINE.md` Season 95 route beginning with **The Order Jin Never Gave** is stale. It must not be used for prose.
3. Phase I must dramatize the full approved chain: peace → Axtaya cases → Luo/Yun investigation → Shinrin discovery → three-month Shunto pursuit → capture/assault → Yurushi/Hana escape → Rhen's unrelated seclusion → Yun's warning/death → Xie taking the empty No.10 seat.
4. Rhen's seclusion now has an exact chapter anchor: he enters in **Chapter 354**, exactly one day before Yun reaches Isgard in **Chapter 355**.
5. Xie Wuchen receives a real mini-arc across the six seasons instead of appearing only after Yun dies.
6. Shinrin is first shown as a functioning society with real civilian benefits before Yun uncovers the coercive state and invasion machine.
7. Yun's investigation lasts roughly **three months**, not the two-to-three weeks she originally expects.
8. The Phase I / Phase II seam is corrected: **Yun's warning reaches Wuyue and the pact activates before Phase I ends**, because Xie must take the empty No.10 seat before entering Phase II. Therefore old Phase II Chapter 361 wording that says the warning first arrives there is superseded. Phase II opens with mobilization after the warning, while its Day-0 clock remains Yun's death/warning day.

# Phase I clock

- **Opening:** two full years after the Wuyue–Isgard war.
- **Early Phase I:** peace is genuine; there is no hidden war already underway.
- **Yun departure:** she expects a **two-to-three-week** foreign pharmaceutical trace.
- **Week 3:** Luo becomes the first person to know the silence is wrong.
- **Months 1–3:** Yun remains inside Shinrin because the threat grows from smuggling investigation into government-scale invasion discovery while Shunto progressively closes her routes.
- **Late Month 3:** Shunto captures Yun after adapting specifically to her poison/circulation tactics.
- **Escape:** Yurushi helps Yun onto an old civilian/medical route toward Isgard; Hana alters one patrol without knowing the complete situation.
- **Chapter 354:** Rhen enters closed seclusion for his own qi-condensation problem.
- **Chapter 355, exactly one day later:** Yun reaches Isgard alive but beyond recovery.
- **Day 0:** Yun delivers the warning, dies, and Isgard immediately activates the Wuyue defensive pact.
- **End of Phase I:** Wuyue receives the warning, leaves Yun's No.10 seat empty briefly, then Xie accepts the operationally necessary vacancy before the invasion begins.
- **Phase II:** begins roughly Day 1–2 after the warning; all later Day-14 / six-week / six-month timing remains measured from Day 0.

# Season 95 — Two Years Beneath the Crooked Sign
## Chapters 301–310

### Chapter 301 — Two Years Beneath the Crooked Sign
Open on genuine peace. The Quaint Teahouse is busy, lived-in and mildly chaotic; rebuilt roads, veterans and changed customers show two years have passed without turning the opening into a history lecture. Sera and Rhen's relationship is visibly mature and domestic, with warmth, teasing and adult intimacy beneath ordinary shop work.

### Chapter 302 — The Garden Is Asked For, Not Imposed
Rhen asks Sera before restoring / continuing **Frozen Petals Garden** over the core. Sera's consent matters because she previously chose to stand on her own; the Garden is now an extra layer after she proved that independence, not a replacement for it. The scene naturally establishes Sera, Qin, Tae, Huo and Lu as the deepest long-term recipients and shows the 1,000-person Quaint as a working institution rather than a statistics paragraph.

### Chapter 303 — What Two Years Did Not Repair
Briefly visit the world beyond the tea room: Arin's Silver Horizon still carries catastrophic losses, Wuyue armies are rebuilding rather than magically restored, and Isgard remains a pact partner recovering from war and epidemic. Rhen's reserve refills a little too quickly after an ordinary exertion; he dismisses it as annoying rather than ominous.

### Chapter 304 — The Man Who Was Strong for Five Minutes
An Isgard-linked medical handoff brings a captured Peak-Marquis-class fighter who briefly produced impossible output before collapsing. His cultivation has not risen, but his throughput did. Torn meridians, internal trauma and abnormal depletion immediately distinguish **borrowed output** from a cultivation breakthrough.

### Chapter 305 — Physician and Poisoner
Luo Wen and Yun Shizhen examine the recovered substance together. Their established romance is visible through comfortable teasing, shared habits and the way they disagree without ceremony: Luo sees a medical stimulant built beyond sane limits; Yun sees a body being induced to poison itself. Their relationship exists before tragedy, not because tragedy is coming.

### Chapter 306 — Something Was Spent
Rhen repairs what is physically repairable in the first victim but recognizes that part of the man's future vitality is not an injury waiting to be healed. This does not reveal the whole Axtaya doctrine; it establishes the frightening distinction between **damage** and **something already consumed**.

### Chapter 307 — Lu Follows the Box, Not the Poison
Lu Weiran traces mislabeled medical cargo, invoices, transshipment dates and merchant intermediaries. He contributes through logistics rather than suddenly understanding toxicology. The trail suggests the substance has moved through legitimate-looking medical channels before reaching black-market hands.

### Chapter 308 — He Thought It Was Temporary
A second user survives long enough to explain he took the substance voluntarily because he was told it was a temporary cultivation enhancer with a difficult recovery period. His body demonstrates that the seller concealed the true cost. Weight categories, pulse screening and standardized dosage markings imply the underlying system began with serious medical knowledge.

### Chapter 309 — The Wind That Would Not Stay
During Lu's port investigation, Shen Rui recognizes the circulation and movement of a masked Wind-Qi cultivator: **Xie Wuchen, the Unseen Gale**, Tempest Hall's last successor and now a newly ascended Sovereign. Rui remembers losing to the same man years earlier while acting on compromised information. Xie helps prevent harm, then immediately tries to leave before anyone can turn gratitude into affiliation.

### Chapter 310 — Axtaya
Luo, Yun and Lu finally connect the trade fragments to one repeated foreign term: **Axtaya**. A second label / route reference yields the distant place-name **Shinrin**. Nobody yet knows Shinsei has ten Paragons or a government-scale army; at this point the problem still looks like a foreign pharmaceutical network with frighteningly sophisticated medicine.

# Season 96 — The Road to Shinrin
## Chapters 311–320

### Chapter 311 — Strength With a Dosage Table
Luo and Yun reconstruct Axtaya's layered purpose: force circulation, suppress warning responses, push vitality into combat throughput and delay collapse. Their laboratory work continues to carry their dry romantic rhythm. They conclude that the original logic was medical before someone militarized it.

### Chapter 312 — Frost Where Rhen Did Not Mean It
A mundane moment at Quaint produces unintended frost from Rhen's suppression. His reserve has refilled much faster than it should after earlier use, and making himself feel ordinary now takes noticeable attention. He still treats it as a personal technical problem, completely unrelated to Shinrin.

### Chapter 313 — Leaving Is Still a Choice
Xie quietly helps Lu identify one shipping intermediary, then says he is done. Sera challenges his habit without trying to recruit him by force: helping people and disappearing before anyone can depend on him is still a choice. Xie does not suddenly join; he leaves irritated because the point landed.

### Chapter 314 — Yun Asks for the Road
Yun briefs Sera openly. She proposes tracing the pharmaceutical chain into Shinrin because her poison expertise gives her the best chance of recognizing production methods and hidden contamination. She is not volunteering to infiltrate a known ten-Paragon military state; nobody possesses that knowledge yet.

### Chapter 315 — Three Weeks
Luo wants to accompany her. Yun refuses for sound operational reasons: two foreign investigators double the profile, and somebody in Wuyue must be able to decode anything she sends back. Their goodbye is small, intimate and confident rather than melodramatic. She expects to be gone **two to three weeks**.

### Chapter 316 — A Country That Works
Yun reaches Shinrin through ordinary merchant / medical traffic and initially finds a prosperous, functioning society: maintained roads, irrigation, clinics, granaries, disciplined patrols and safer trade. The people praising Shinsei are not fools. The institution genuinely ended much of the clan violence that came before it.

### Chapter 317 — The Guild That Became the State
Through permits, clinic rules, tax records and ordinary bureaucracy, Yun realizes the Shinsei Guild is effectively Shinrin's government, military regulator, judiciary and infrastructure authority. The broken-circle symbol and old peace logic appear first in civilian context, not villain exposition.

### Chapter 318 — Aya's Old Safeguards
Yun finds early medical records connected to **Aya Katsuragi** that contain screening limits, treatment safeguards and conservative stimulant logic. Later military paperwork has stripped or overridden those protections. Yun does not conclude Aya personally approved everything that followed; the documentary trail shows medical work being institutionalized and escalated.

### Chapter 319 — Do Not Let Him Touch You
Yun narrowly avoids a capture attempt by a man whose entire method is built around contact, leverage and disabling joints / circulation rather than flashy killing. She does not yet receive a complete biography. What matters is the practical warning she learns from watching him work: letting this man establish repeated contact is losing by inches.

### Chapter 320 — The Third Week Ends
Back in Wuyue, Luo reaches the date Yun was supposed to return or make contact. He is the first person to say plainly that something is wrong. Sera does not launch an impossible rescue into a continent they barely understand; Lu intensifies route tracing while Luo starts preserving every fragment Yun might have intended only he could read.

# Season 97 — No.2 Hunts the Pale Venom
## Chapters 321–330

### Chapter 321 — Too Much Medicine for a Black Market
Yun discovers Axtaya stock and component movement on a scale that cannot plausibly belong to smugglers. Warehouses, medical requisitions and protected transport routes point back into Shinsei's institutional supply system.

### Chapter 322 — Peak Marquis Is Ordinary Here
Military manifests and training rosters reveal the first true scale shock: Peak-Marquis-class fighters are not rare curiosities inside Shinsei's trained elite structure. Yun starts revising the threat model from dangerous state to continental war machine.

### Chapter 323 — The Aura That Would Not Stay Small
At home, Rhen's suppression becomes harder again. A cup freezes at the rim before he notices; Sera notices that he noticed. He tells her his qi is returning too aggressively and taking up too much internal space, but he is not ready to close the door yet.

### Chapter 324 — Shunto Takamori
Yun's pursuer is properly identified as **Shunto Takamori — Shinsei No.2 / High Paragon**, a capture specialist. The tier difference is explicit: Yun is a newly Sovereign specialist and has no business trying to overpower him. Her objective becomes surviving encounters, understanding his method and completing the investigation.

### Chapter 325 — Poison the Room, Not the Man
Shunto corners Yun in a controlled environment. She escapes by contaminating routes, surfaces and assumptions rather than trying to penetrate a High Paragon head-on. Shunto begins learning her environmental habits.

### Chapter 326 — The Gale Brings One More Ledger
Xie reappears in Wuyue with a shipping ledger he claims he acquired only because the courier was being mistreated. Lu accepts the useful information without pretending Xie has joined. Shen Rui sees that Xie keeps returning to the same moral line even while denying he belongs anywhere.

### Chapter 327 — Shunto Changes His Bloodflow
In the next encounter, Shunto uses protected circulation, prepared antidotes and fewer unnecessary contacts. Yun recognizes that he is learning her toxin timing as quickly as she is learning his locks. The pursuit becomes genuinely adaptive rather than repeated escapes from the same trick.

### Chapter 328 — The Arm He Could Not Feel
Yun deliberately exploits one predictable circulation reroute created by Shunto's own lock method and seriously poisons him. One arm becomes temporarily compromised. This is a major technical win for Yun without pretending she defeated a High Paragon in direct combat.

### Chapter 329 — He Learns From Pain
Shunto survives, treats the toxin and changes procedure. His frustration becomes personal because Yun keeps forcing him to react, but he remains competent rather than cartoonishly reckless. From this point he begins building the exact capture plan that will eventually work.

### Chapter 330 — Maps Point North
Yun links naval studies, shipping capacity, medical stock, troop requisitions and Isgard maps. It is not enough yet for a full invasion warning, but it is enough to make leaving with only the original smuggling answer feel irresponsible. She decides on one final evidence push while actively searching for a route home.

# Season 98 — Three Months Under One Government
## Chapters 331–340

### Chapter 331 — Safehouses Become Calendar Marks
Time passes through physical consequence rather than a montage sentence: changed rooms, burned contacts, healing injuries, different market weather and the shrinking number of routes Yun can safely use. Her two-to-three-week trace has become a roughly three-month survival investigation because the state-scale discovery and Shunto's pressure progressively close the exits.

### Chapter 332 — Ten Seats
Yun reconstructs enough hierarchy to understand that Shinsei maintains **ten ranked officers at Paragon level**. She learns Shunto is No.2 and that **Tsubasa Kurokawa, No.1, is stronger**. She does not acquire a convenient list explaining every Dominion mechanic.

### Chapter 333 — Two Hundred Thousand Is Not a Rumor
Grain, payroll, equipment, clinic throughput and reserve rotation collectively establish **more than 200,000 trained elites** in Shinsei's wider system. The conclusion is earned from many boring records, which is precisely why it is convincing.

### Chapter 334 — Proof That Can Survive Her
Yun secures a physical Axtaya sample / fragment in a form that can survive travel or seizure. At the same time, the pressure from Rhen's own qi at home is worsening; another brief domestic scene shows that even rest is no longer making him feel less full.

### Chapter 335 — Luo's Private Notation
Yun builds a coded notebook using the private medical shorthand she and Luo developed together. A stranger could read symptoms and numbers without understanding the operational message. Luo, if it reaches him, will know what the ordering means.

### Chapter 336 — What Paper Cannot Carry
Because evidence can be confiscated, Yun memorizes the essential military facts separately: Shinsei identity, Axtaya, troop scale, elite floor, ten Paragons, Shunto's tier, Tsubasa above him and Isgard invasion preparation. The three layers are now distinct: physical proof, coded notebook and memory.

### Chapter 337 — No Needless Contact
Shunto returns using the version of himself built specifically to counter her: protected circulation, antidotes, controlled exits and no gratuitous contact. Yun escapes again, but the cost is that another viable route disappears.

### Chapter 338 — Four Months
Fleet schedules, landing studies and medical staging finally make the Isgard invasion undeniable. Yun reconstructs an original timetable of roughly **four months**, not because one master file says so but because independent preparations converge on the same window.

### Chapter 339 — No Master File
Yun completes the final logic chain from warehouses, requisitions, troop movement, shipping, maps and medical paperwork. The chapter should feel like expertise paying off: she now has enough to warn Wuyue and Isgard even if every single document cannot leave with her.

### Chapter 340 — The Open Door
Shunto deliberately leaves an escape lane that appears imperfectly guarded. Yun notices several things wrong with it, but exhaustion and the collapse of alternatives force a choice. The open route is the trap: Shunto closes exits only after Yun has committed, and this time his adapted capture plan works.

# Season 99 — The Locked Room
## Chapters 341–350

### Chapter 341 — Captured Alive
Shunto completes the capture without requiring Yun to become careless or weak. His locks target movement and circulation while his preparation denies the poison routes she previously used. He has won the immediate contest; he has not yet won the information war.

### Chapter 342 — She Does Not Give Him the War
Interrogation focuses on what Yun knows, where her proof is and who in Wuyue is waiting for it. She lies selectively, withholds the important structure and keeps at least part of the evidence beyond Shunto's certainty. He cannot simply read truth from her body or Domain.

### Chapter 343 — What Shunto Chose
Shunto sexually assaults Yun. The portrayal remains **non-graphic, non-erotic and consequence-focused**: Yun clearly refuses; Shunto deliberately uses superior physical / cultivation control and restraint; the scene frames the act as entitlement, domination and punishment, never passion or romance. The narrative does not linger on anatomy.

### Chapter 344 — The Prison Is Still a Laboratory
Agency returns immediately to Yun. She inventories guard timing, medicines, restraints, damaged circulation and weak points in the locks. A subtle toxin transferred during captivity later produces numbness in Shunto, proving that even while overpowered she continued resisting.

### Chapter 345 — Black Radiance Notices
**Yurushi Amagiri** encounters enough of the situation to understand that a badly injured foreign prisoner is being hunted and controlled in a way he cannot morally accept. He does not initially know Yun is Wuyue No.10. His first help is deniable: medicine where none should be, a door delayed, a patrol arriving seconds late.

### Chapter 346 — Yun Does Not Trust a Convenient Savior
Yun tests the pattern before trusting it. She uses Yurushi's openings as ingredients rather than surrendering the escape to him. Their first real cooperation is practical and wary: she contributes poison knowledge, timing and evidence priorities; he contributes routes and institutional blind spots.

### Chapter 347 — The Roads He Built for Other People
The escape reveals that Yurushi did not invent a perfect network for Yun overnight. For years he has maintained hidden medicine caches, forgotten clan paths, merchant credentials and civilian extraction corridors for people harmed by Shinsei's harder edges. Helping Yun is an escalation of an existing moral habit.

### Chapter 348 — Hana Moves One Patrol
Yurushi asks **Hana Arakawa** for one limited administrative change without telling her the full Yun / invasion story. Hana knows only that someone is being hunted wrongly and that Yurushi would not ask lightly. She reroutes one patrol in a way that remains plausibly deniable.

### Chapter 349 — She Escapes on Her Own Feet
Yun escapes through a medical / export route with Yurushi's help, but she remains an active escape agent: choosing timing, contaminating pursuit paths, protecting the evidence and moving despite injuries. The scene must not turn her into a rescued damsel.

### Chapter 350 — Yurushi Stays Behind
Yurushi gets Yun onto the route toward Isgard, then remains in Shinrin temporarily because civilians still depend on corridors Shunto will now investigate. Shunto realizes an insider helped her and begins hunting the unknown traitor. Yun travels on with worsening injuries and very little margin left.

# Season 100 — The Warning She Carried
## Chapters 351–360

### Chapter 351 — Too Full
Rhen finally admits the problem has crossed from irritating to unsafe. His reserve refills too aggressively, aura suppression takes continuous effort and the old diffuse qi state physically occupies too much of him. He tells Sera the answer is not more power but **making the qi take less space** through long condensation.

### Chapter 352 — Three Weeks Became Three Months
Luo has now lived with Yun's silence for roughly three months. Lu and Xie recover one last Shinrin export / route fragment that confirms she reached far deeper into the foreign network than anyone expected, but it gives them no magical rescue coordinate. Xie again tries to describe his help as temporary; nobody argues with him this time.

### Chapter 353 — Before the Door Closes
Sera and Rhen spend their final night together before seclusion. The scene is warm, sensual and adult without explicit sex. Rhen explains the medical rule plainly: once condensation passes its early stage, forced interruption can catastrophically damage his meridians and body. Neither knows Yun will reach Isgard the following day.

### Chapter 354 — Closed Garden
Rhen enters seclusion for the qi-condensation problem he has been foreshadowing across Phase I. The timing is explicitly unrelated to Axtaya, Yun or Shinsei. **This is exactly one day before Yun reaches Isgard.**

### Chapter 355 — One Day Later
Yun reaches Isgard alive but beyond recovery. Isgard physicians and officials recognize that a Wuyue Top Ten officer has crossed the border carrying an emergency warning, and the defensive-pact machinery begins moving before anyone understands the full story.

### Chapter 356 — The Last Report of the Pale Venom
Yun prioritizes the military facts: **Shinrin / Shinsei; Axtaya and its broad effect; more than 200,000 trained elites; Peak-Marquis ordinary elite floor; ten Paragon-ranked officers; Shunto No.2 / High Paragon; Tsubasa stronger; invasion preparation aimed at Isgard.** She states that Shunto captured her and that an insider helped her escape. She does not use her final report to narrate the sexual assault in detail.

Yun dies after completing the warning. Isgard immediately sends the pact alert to Wuyue.

### Chapter 357 — The Warning Reaches Wuyue
The emergency message reaches Wuyue on Day 0. Sera, Luo, Jin, the public Top Ten and Quaint leadership react to the facts they actually have. They know Yun was captured and escaped with insider help; they do **not** yet possess Yurushi's later private account of the assault. The Wuyue–Isgard defensive pact activates immediately.

Rhen's chamber is already closed. Sera understands the cruelty of the timing but does not yet turn the chapter into a repeated seclusion exposition; the explicit medical choice not to force the door is paid off at the opening of Phase II.

### Chapter 358 — The Empty Number
Yun's No.10 seat is left empty long enough to feel like a death rather than an administrative vacancy. Luo remains functional but quiet. The Top Ten do not instantly jump to replacement politics; the operational problem emerges because invasion preparation means Wuyue may soon need every public seat active.

### Chapter 359 — Shen Rui Names the Gale
Shen Rui proposes Xie Wuchen because Phase I has already shown what kind of man he is, how strong he is and why Rui trusts his conscience despite their history. Xie objects that No.10 was Yun's number. Sera answers that nobody can replace Yun; the seat is empty because she died warning them, and the people she warned them about are coming.

### Chapter 360 — The Empty Number Is Still a Number
Xie accepts publicly:

> **“I'm not replacing her. I'm taking the empty number.”**

He enters the coming war as **Wuyue No.10 / newly Sovereign / no Dominion / no invented personal army**. Phase I ends with Wuyue already warned, the defensive pact active, Rhen unavailable by tragic coincidence, and mobilization beginning.

Do **not** repeat Yun's warning arrival in Chapter 361.

# Mandatory information boundaries for prose

- Yun does **not** knowingly enter a ten-Paragon state. The true scale reveals itself after she arrives.
- Yun never directly overpowers Shunto. Her success comes from poison specialization, environment, circulation prediction and persistence.
- Yun does not learn every Dominion mechanic or return with a Shinsei encyclopedia.
- Shunto's assault is never eroticized or treated as romantic tension.
- Yurushi helps because the situation is wrong before he knows exactly who Yun is.
- Hana's contribution is limited and plausibly deniable because she lacks the full context.
- The entire Top Ten does not learn the sexual-assault details from Yun's death report. Yurushi's later private disclosure to Sera, then Sera's private disclosure to Luo, remains a Phase III payoff.
- Tsubasa knows the Wuyue–Isgard pact exists but does not know Quaint's hidden roster, Tae/Huo/Qin's High-Paragon status or Rhen's true ceiling.
- Rhen's seclusion is a personal qi-engineering necessity and **not caused by the crisis**.
- Xie is not a replacement Yun. His acceptance preserves the distinction explicitly.

# Writing-density guidance

Phase I should feel like a slow tightening rather than six seasons of constant combat.

- Seasons 95–96: warm, investigative, relationship-heavy.
- Seasons 97–98: mounting foreign pressure and cat-and-mouse suspense.
- Season 99: captivity / escape, kept consequence-focused rather than exploitative.
- Season 100: parallel tragedy — Yun racing toward the warning while Rhen independently closes the seclusion door one day too early.

Target roughly 2.0k–2.8k words only when a chapter's material supports it. Do not pad quiet chapters merely to match battle-chapter length.
