# 2. Corrected Phase II war clock

## 2.1 The invasion was NOT four months away on Day 0

Merged Phase I is now authoritative:

- Shinsei uses a **four-month readiness cycle**.
- Yun leaves Shinrin in **late month three**.
- Her final report says the remaining launch window may be **weeks**.

Therefore any older wording saying Tsubasa compresses **four months remaining** into fourteen days is stale.

### Correct interpretation

Yun discovers Shinsei **late in an already-running four-month readiness cycle**. On Day 0, several weeks of final preparation remain. Once Tsubasa learns that Yun successfully warned Isgard/Wuyue, he deliberately cuts even that remaining readiness window to roughly **fourteen days**.

He is not building an invasion from zero in two weeks. He accepts unfinished intelligence, incomplete staging and less preparation in exchange for striking before Wuyue can finish mobilizing.

### Reader-facing shorthand

Use:

> **late readiness cycle → roughly fourteen days**

Do **not** use:

> four months away → fourteen days

The Season 101 working title **Four Months Become Fourteen Days** is therefore superseded. Use a title such as **The Remaining Weeks** / **Fourteen Days** or another prose title that does not imply four full months remained.

## 2.2 Corrected Wuyue movement clock

- **Day 0:** Yun dies in Isgard; warning reaches Wuyue; pact is active. Rhen entered seclusion the previous day.
- **Days 1–14:** Wuyue mobilizes immediately; ships, food, medicine, horses and army assembly begin. Yurushi dismantles final routes and moves toward Wuyue. Tsubasa cuts the remaining Shinsei readiness period to ~14 days.
- **~Day 14:** Shinsei lands in Isgard with ~25,000 troops + five official Paragons.
- **Days 14–~34:** Isgard bears the main military burden while Wuyue continues mobilization. Wuyue is not idle: intelligence, medical stores, liaison traffic and naval staging move under the pact.
- **~Day 34–35:** the main Wuyue expedition sails / begins the final crossing after Yurushi has been vetted and named Sixth Petal.
- **~Day 42:** approximately 63,800 public Wuyue personnel + Sera + 120 Quaint Hands reach Isgard.

The old Chapter 380 wording that Wuyue **departs as Isgard finishes its first month alone** is superseded. Wuyue must depart **before** the ~Day-42 arrival, with the crossing occupying real time.

“Isgard fights mostly alone” means **without Wuyue's main army**, not that the pact partner sends no medicine, intelligence, ships, couriers or logistical preparation during those weeks.
